# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. serves as the Borrower, with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor. The company operates casino facilities under the oversight of Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG).

**Transaction Description:** This Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver is granted by Bank of Montreal as administrative agent on behalf of the Required Lenders, extending previous COVID-19 related covenant relief through March 31, 2021.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver provides continued covenant relief during the COVID-19 pandemic, specifically addressing government-mandated casino closures, waiving closure defaults, financial covenant breaches in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2, and compliance certificate delivery requirements.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions. The company benefits from financial relief provided by OLG under their Casino Operating Services Agreement (COSA), including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments. The original Credit Agreement was executed on June 10, 2019 and amended on July 17, 2019.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government support through OLG providing financial relief including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA agreement
• Maintains minimum liquidity requirement of $15,000,000 throughout the waiver period
• Weekly liquidity reporting requirement provides ongoing monitoring and transparency
• Extension fee of 5 basis points compensates lenders for additional risk
• Structured waiver with defined termination date provides clear timeline for covenant restoration

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Casino closure for extended period creates significant operational disruption and revenue loss, mitigated by government financial support
• Borrower prohibited from requesting additional advances under either credit facility during waiver period
• Applicable margin increased to Level 5 pricing, reflecting heightened credit risk
• Restricted from making certain distributions and acquisitions during waiver period
• New event of default triggered if OLG ceases financial relief for more than 5 business days, creating dependency on government support
• Multiple previous waivers indicate ongoing financial stress from pandemic impact
