# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In November 2020, Bank of Montreal as administrative agent granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver to MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., extending COVID-19 related covenant waivers through March 31, 2021.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, serves as the Borrower with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor. The original Credit Agreement was dated June 10, 2019 and amended July 17, 2019.
- This waiver extends previous COVID-19 related relief through the "Extended Waiver Period" until March 31, 2021, addressing casino closure defaults and financial covenant breaches caused by government-mandated casino closures.
- The purpose is to provide continued covenant relief during the COVID-19 pandemic, specifically waiving closure defaults, financial covenant breaches in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2, and compliance certificate delivery requirements while the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) provides financial relief to the borrower.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities that were temporarily closed due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions authorized by OLG, with the company receiving certain financial relief under their Casino Operating Services Agreement (COSA).

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Government support through OLG providing financial relief including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA agreement
- Maintains minimum liquidity requirement of $15,000,000 throughout the waiver period
- Weekly liquidity reporting requirement provides ongoing monitoring and transparency
- Extension fee of 5 basis points compensates lenders for additional risk

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Casino closure for extended period creates significant operational disruption and revenue loss
- Borrower prohibited from requesting additional advances under either credit facility during waiver period
- Applicable margin increased to Level 5 (5.00% for Bankers' Acceptances/Letters of Credit/LIBOR Loans)
- Restricted from making certain distributions and acquisitions during waiver period
- New event of default triggered if OLG ceases financial relief for more than 5 business days
