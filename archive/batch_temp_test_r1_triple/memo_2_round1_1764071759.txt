# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. is a casino operator with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor under the original Credit Agreement dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019).

**Transaction Description:** Bank of Montreal, as administrative agent on behalf of Required Lenders, granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver extending COVID-19 related covenant relief through March 31, 2021.

**Purpose of Financing:** To provide continued covenant relief during the COVID-19 pandemic, specifically waiving closure defaults, financial covenant breaches, and compliance certificate delivery requirements while Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) provides financial relief under the Casino Operating Services Agreement (COSA).

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under agreement with OLG. The facilities were temporarily closed due to COVID-19 restrictions, with OLG providing financial relief including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government support through OLG providing financial relief under COSA agreement
• Minimum liquidity covenant of $15,000,000 maintained throughout waiver period
• Weekly liquidity reporting ensures ongoing monitoring and transparency
• Extension fee of 5 basis points compensates lenders for additional risk

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extended casino closure creates significant operational disruption and revenue loss
• No additional advances permitted under either credit facility during waiver period
• Pricing penalty with applicable margin increased to Level 5 (5.00% spread)
• Distribution and acquisition restrictions limit financial flexibility
• New event of default if OLG financial relief ceases for more than 5 business days
