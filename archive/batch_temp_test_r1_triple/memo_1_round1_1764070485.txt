# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

Date of Agreement: 2020-09-30

MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, entered into a Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent on behalf of the Required Lenders. This waiver extends previous COVID-19 related covenant relief through November 30, 2020 (Extended Waiver Period).

The transaction addresses ongoing financial covenant breaches and operational challenges due to government-mandated casino closures related to the COVID-19 pandemic. The purpose of this financing waiver is to provide temporary relief from multiple covenant defaults while the borrower navigates the unprecedented disruption to casino operations.

MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under agreements with OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation), with Complex Services Inc. serving as guarantor. The company was significantly impacted by mandatory casino closures lasting more than 60 consecutive days due to COVID-19 restrictions, which would have constituted an Event of Default under the original credit agreement dated June 10, 2019.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) is providing financial relief including relief from threshold payments and continued payment of Fixed Component Per Diem under the COSA agreement
• Borrower maintains liquidity covenant of minimum $15,000,000 providing cash flow buffer
• Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
• Applicable Margin set at Level 5 (highest pricing tier) reflecting appropriate risk adjustment for elevated credit risk
• Government gaming authority support demonstrates regulatory backing during crisis period

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Multiple covenant breaches waived including financial covenants in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicating significant operational stress
• Casino closure lasting more than 60 consecutive days created Event of Default under Section 11.1.11 of the original credit agreement
• Advances under credit facilities are suspended during waiver period, limiting financial flexibility and access to additional liquidity
• New Event of Default triggered if OLG ceases relief payments for more than 5 business days in aggregate, creating dependency on government support
• Restrictions on distributions and acquisitions limit strategic flexibility during recovery period
