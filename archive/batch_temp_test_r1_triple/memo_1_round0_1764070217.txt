# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- Date of Agreement: 2020-09-30
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, entered into a Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent
- This waiver extends previous COVID-19 related covenant relief through November 30, 2020 (Extended Waiver Period)
- The transaction addresses ongoing financial covenant breaches and operational challenges due to government-mandated casino closures related to the COVID-19 pandemic
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under agreements with OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation), with Complex Services Inc. serving as guarantor. The company was significantly impacted by mandatory casino closures lasting more than 60 consecutive days due to COVID-19 restrictions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) is providing financial relief including relief from threshold payments and continued payment of Fixed Component Per Diem under the COSA agreement
- Borrower maintains liquidity covenant of minimum $15,000,000 providing cash flow buffer
- Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
- Applicable Margin set at Level 5 (highest pricing tier) reflecting appropriate risk adjustment

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Multiple covenant breaches waived including financial covenants in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicating operational stress
- Casino closure lasting more than 60 consecutive days created Event of Default under Section 11.1.11
- Advances under credit facilities are suspended during waiver period, limiting financial flexibility
- New Event of Default triggered if OLG ceases relief payments for more than 5 business days in aggregate
- Restrictions on distributions and acquisitions limit strategic flexibility during recovery period
