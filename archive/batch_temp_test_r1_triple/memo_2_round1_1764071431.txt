# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. serves as the Borrower, with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor. The company operates casino facilities under agreements with Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG).

**Transaction Description:** This is a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver granted by Bank of Montreal as administrative agent on behalf of the Required Lenders. The waiver extends previous COVID-19 related covenant relief measures through an "Extended Waiver Period" until March 31, 2021.

**Purpose of Financing:** To provide continued covenant relief during the COVID-19 pandemic, specifically waiving closure defaults, financial covenant breaches in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 of the original Credit Agreement, and compliance certificate delivery requirements while OLG provides financial relief to the borrower.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities that were temporarily closed due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions authorized by OLG. The company receives financial relief under their Casino Operating Services Agreement (COSA), including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments from OLG.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government support through OLG providing financial relief including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA agreement
• Maintains minimum liquidity requirement of $15,000,000 throughout the waiver period
• Weekly liquidity reporting requirement provides ongoing monitoring and transparency
• Extension fee of 5 basis points compensates lenders for additional risk

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Casino closure for extended period creates significant operational disruption and revenue loss
• Borrower prohibited from requesting additional advances under either credit facility during waiver period
• Applicable margin increased to Level 5 pricing (5.00% for Bankers' Acceptances/Letters of Credit/LIBOR Loans)
• Restricted from making certain distributions and acquisitions during waiver period
• New event of default triggered if OLG ceases financial relief for more than 5 business days
