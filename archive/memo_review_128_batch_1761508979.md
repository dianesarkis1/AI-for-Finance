**Transaction Overview:** This is an incremental commitment agreement whereby an increasing lender provides additional capacity to OCSL's existing senior secured revolving credit facility. **Transaction Structure:** The agreement involves OCSL as borrower, an increasing lender party, and ING Capital LLC as administrative agent and issuing bank. Key counterparties include JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. and Bank of America N.A. as syndication agents, with ING Capital LLC, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., and Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated serving as joint lead arrangers and joint bookrunners.

**Purpose of Financing:** N/A - specific use of proceeds not disclosed in the incremental commitment agreement.

**Company Background:** OCSL operates with subsidiary guarantors including OCSL SRNE, LLC and FSFC Holdings, Inc. The company maintains a secured credit structure with established guarantee and security agreements.
