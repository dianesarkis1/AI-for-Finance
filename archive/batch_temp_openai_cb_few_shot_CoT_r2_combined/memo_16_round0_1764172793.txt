# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 29, 2024, MarineMax, Inc. and its lending syndicate executed a fourth amendment to their credit agreement originally dated August 8, 2022.
- MarineMax, Inc., a Florida corporation serving as Borrower Representative, operates in the marine industry with extensive subsidiary operations including boat dealerships, yacht services, charter operations, and marina management across multiple jurisdictions including the U.S., Caribbean, and Europe.
- The transaction involves a syndicated credit facility with Manufacturers and Traders Trust Company as Administrative Agent and Wells Fargo Commercial Distribution Finance, LLC as Floor Plan Agent, alongside multiple participating lenders including Bank of America, PNC Bank, BMO Bank, and others.
- The amendment specifically modifies Section 2.01(a)(iii) of the existing credit agreement to adjust limits on various categories of floor plan financing for marine vessels, including boats over 80 feet, foreign OEM units, used units, and high-value vessels exceeding $1,000,000.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong, diversified lender syndicate including major financial institutions (M&T Bank, Wells Fargo, Bank of America, PNC, BMO, Huntington, and others), demonstrating broad lender confidence
- Comprehensive guarantee structure with extensive subsidiary guarantor network spanning multiple business lines and geographies
- Reaffirmation of all existing guarantees, pledges, and collateral arrangements, maintaining security structure
- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default exist, indicating current compliance

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Amendment increases certain floor plan financing limits which may indicate higher inventory financing needs or expansion plans requiring enhanced monitoring
- Marine industry exposure to economic cycles and discretionary consumer spending patterns
- Complex multi-jurisdictional subsidiary structure may present operational and regulatory complexity
