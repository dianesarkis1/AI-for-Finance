# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-09-06

**Borrower / Company Overview:** The transaction involves existing borrowers (Tercel Oilfield Products USA L.L.C., Pride Energy Services, LLC, and Top-Co Inc.) and three new borrowers (Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC, Innovex International, Inc., and TIW Corporation) operating in the oilfield services and energy equipment sector, providing downhole solutions and related energy services.

**Transaction Description:** Joinder Agreement adding new borrowers to an existing syndicated credit facility administered by PNC Bank, National Association as Agent, with U.S. Bank National Association as participating lender. The new borrowers join a Second Amended and Restated Revolving Credit, Term Loan, Guaranty and Security Agreement originally dated June 10, 2022.

**Purpose of Financing:** The joinder stems from the "Dril-Quip Merger," where Innovex merged with DQ Merger Sub, LLC (now Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC), with Dril-Quip, Inc. changing its name to Innovex International, Inc. prior to the merger consummation.

**Company Background:** The credit facility has an established history dating to 2022 with four previous amendments, indicating ongoing lender engagement. The facility includes revolving credit, term loan, and swing loan components serving energy sector participants across multiple jurisdictions (Texas, Delaware, Alberta).

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• Established credit relationship with multiple amendments demonstrating ongoing lender engagement and borrower compliance
• Diversified institutional lender syndicate including PNC Bank and U.S. Bank National Association
• Comprehensive security package including pledges of equity interests, intellectual property, deposit account controls, and continuing security interests in all collateral
• Joint and several liability structure across all borrowers provides multiple sources of repayment
• Structured post-closing integration process with specific deliverable timelines demonstrates organized approach to merger integration

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• Integration risk from merger activities and addition of new borrowers to existing credit structure, mitigated by comprehensive joinder provisions and post-closing requirements
• Cyclical energy sector exposure with inherent commodity price volatility affecting oilfield services demand
• Multiple amendments to original agreement (four amendments since 2022) may indicate ongoing covenant management requirements or operational complexities
• Limited financial performance metrics available in joinder documentation to assess current credit quality
