# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 6, 2024, multiple parties executed a Joinder Agreement to add new borrowers to an existing credit facility administered by PNC Bank, National Association.
- The transaction involves existing borrowers (Tercel Oilfield Products USA L.L.C., Pride Energy Services, LLC, and Top-Co Inc.) and three new borrowers (Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC, Innovex International, Inc., and TIW Corporation) joining a Second Amended and Restated Revolving Credit, Term Loan, Guaranty and Security Agreement originally dated June 10, 2022.
- The joinder stems from the "Dril-Quip Merger," where Innovex merged with DQ Merger Sub, LLC (now Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC), with Dril-Quip, Inc. changing its name to Innovex International, Inc. prior to the merger.
- The credit facility includes revolving credit, term loan, and swing loan components with PNC Bank as Administrative Agent and U.S. Bank National Association as a participating lender.
- All parties operate in the oilfield services and energy equipment sector, providing downhole solutions and related energy services.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established credit relationship dating to 2022 with multiple amendments indicating ongoing lender engagement and borrower compliance
- Diversified lender syndicate including major institutions (PNC Bank, U.S. Bank National Association)
- Comprehensive security package including pledges of equity interests, intellectual property, and deposit account controls
- Joint and several liability structure across all borrowers provides multiple sources of repayment
- Post-closing covenant structure demonstrates organized integration process with specific deliverable timelines

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Integration risk from merger activities and addition of new borrowers to existing credit structure
- Cyclical energy sector exposure with inherent commodity price volatility
- Multiple amendments to original agreement (four amendments since 2022) may indicate ongoing covenant or operational challenges
- Limited financial information available to assess overall credit quality and performance metrics
