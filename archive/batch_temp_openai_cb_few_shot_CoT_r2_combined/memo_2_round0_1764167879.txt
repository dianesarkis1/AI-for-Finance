# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 30, 2020, Bank of Montreal (as Administrative Agent) and MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. entered into a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver related to their original credit agreement dated June 10, 2019.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, serves as the Borrower with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor. The company operates Casino Facilities under oversight from OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation).
- This waiver extends previous COVID-19 related relief measures through March 31, 2021 (the "Extended Waiver Period"), addressing the temporary closure of casino facilities mandated by applicable law during the pandemic.
- The purpose of this waiver is to provide continued covenant relief due to the COVID-19 pandemic's impact on casino operations, including suspension of financial covenant compliance requirements and reporting obligations, while implementing enhanced liquidity monitoring and operational restrictions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) is providing financial relief to the borrower, including relief from threshold payments and continued payment of Fixed Component Per Diem under the COSA agreement
- Enhanced liquidity monitoring through mandatory weekly reporting ensures close oversight of the borrower's financial position
- Minimum liquidity requirement of $15,000,000 provides a financial cushion during the challenging period
- The borrower confirmed no Default or Event of Default exists as of the waiver date after accounting for the relief provided

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Extended casino closure due to COVID-19 creates significant operational and financial stress on the borrower's core business
- Suspension of financial covenants through March 31, 2021 indicates potential ongoing compliance challenges
- Pricing moves to highest Level 5 margin (5.00% for Bankers' Acceptances/Letters of Credit/LIBOR Loans), reflecting increased credit risk
- New advances under either credit facility are prohibited during the Extended Waiver Period, limiting additional liquidity access
- Restrictions on distributions and acquisitions limit financial flexibility
