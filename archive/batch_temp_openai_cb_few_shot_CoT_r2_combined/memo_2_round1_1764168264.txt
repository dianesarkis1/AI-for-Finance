## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. serves as the borrower, with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor. The company operates Casino Facilities under oversight from OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) and is subject to the COSA agreement with OLG.

**Transaction Description:** Bank of Montreal, as Administrative Agent, granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver on behalf of the Required Lenders. This waiver amends and restates the original credit agreement dated June 10, 2019 (as amended July 17, 2019), extending previous COVID-19 relief measures through the Extended Waiver Period ending March 31, 2021.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver provides continued covenant relief addressing the COVID-19 pandemic's impact on casino operations, including temporary closure of Casino Facilities mandated by applicable law. The waiver suspends financial covenant compliance requirements and reporting obligations while implementing enhanced liquidity monitoring and operational restrictions.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure exceeding 60 consecutive days due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions. OLG has provided financial relief through threshold payment relief and continued Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA agreement.
