# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 17, 2022, Spectrum Brands, Inc. entered into a Fourth Amendment to its Amended and Restated Credit Agreement with Royal Bank of Canada as Administrative Agent and participating Revolving Lenders.
- Spectrum Brands, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Lead Borrower under this revolving credit facility originally established in June 2015 and amended/restated in June 2020.
- The amendment provides additional covenant flexibility in connection with Spectrum Brands' pending Hardware and Home Improvement (HHI) segment sale to ASSA ABLOY AB for $4.3 billion in cash proceeds, as outlined in the Asset and Stock Purchase Agreement dated September 8, 2021.
- The primary purpose is to temporarily relax the Total Net Leverage Ratio covenant to accommodate operational needs during the completion of the HHI Sale transaction, providing increased flexibility from 6.00:1.00 to 7.00:1.00 until the earlier of September 29, 2023 or completion/termination of the HHI Sale.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Substantial asset sale proceeds of $4.3 billion expected from the HHI Sale should significantly improve the company's financial position and provide deleveraging capacity.
- Royal Bank of Canada serves as Administrative Agent with participation from Required Revolving Lenders, indicating continued lender support.
- Amendment includes specific trigger mechanisms and timeline limitations (September 29, 2023) that provide defined parameters for covenant relief.
- All existing collateral documents and security interests remain in full force and effect.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Temporary covenant relaxation from 6.00:1.00 to 7.00:1.00 Total Net Leverage Ratio increases credit risk during the amendment period.
- Dependency on successful completion of the HHI Sale transaction, with covenant relief tied to this strategic initiative.
- Limited visibility into current leverage levels and overall financial performance from the amendment document alone.
- Amendment fees of 0.20% of revolving commitments indicate lenders require compensation for increased risk.
