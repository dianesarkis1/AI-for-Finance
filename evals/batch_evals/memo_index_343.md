## Executive Summary

**Date:** September 4, 2024

**Borrower:** Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II

**Transaction Overview:** This is a commitment increase request under an existing senior secured revolving credit facility. The borrower is requesting to increase the aggregate revolving commitments from $455,000,000 to $480,000,000, representing a $25,000,000 increase. The increase will be effectuated by expanding BNP Paribas's Multicurrency Commitment from $25,000,000 to $50,000,000.

**Purpose of Financing:** N/A - specific use of proceeds not disclosed in the provided documentation.
