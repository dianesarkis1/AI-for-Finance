# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 12, 2021, Emerson Electric Co. entered into a Suspension of Rights Agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent under their existing Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018.
- Emerson Electric Co. serves as the primary Company/Borrower, with subsidiary borrowers also party to the underlying credit facility. JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. acts as Administrative Agent for the lending syndicate.
- This agreement temporarily suspends certain borrowing rights related to LIBOR transition, specifically removing access to Non-USD currencies (GBP, EUR, JPY) and eliminating 1-week and 2-month USD LIBOR interest period options, effective until LIBOR replacement amendments are implemented.
- The purpose is to manage the transition away from LIBOR rates ahead of the December 31, 2021 cessation date for certain LIBOR tenors, while deferring costs of formal credit agreement amendments until alternative benchmarks are established.
- Emerson Electric Co. is a multinational manufacturing and technology company with established banking relationships and access to multi-currency credit facilities.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- **Established Banking Relationship**: Long-standing credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, indicating strong institutional confidence
- **Proactive LIBOR Management**: Company is taking proactive steps to address LIBOR cessation in an orderly fashion
- **Flexibility Maintained**: Suspension can be reversed once proper LIBOR replacement amendments are in place
- **Strong Corporate Governance**: Comprehensive indemnification provisions and proper legal documentation demonstrate sophisticated credit management

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- **Reduced Financing Flexibility**: Temporary loss of Non-USD currency borrowing options and certain interest period selections may limit optimal funding strategies
- **Transition Period Uncertainty**: Potential operational complexities during the LIBOR replacement implementation phase
- **Limited Visibility**: This document provides minimal insight into overall credit facility size, pricing, or financial covenants
