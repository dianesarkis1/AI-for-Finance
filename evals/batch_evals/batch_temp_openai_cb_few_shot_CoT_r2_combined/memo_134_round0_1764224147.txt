# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In June 2025, OFSCC-FS, LLC and its lending syndicate executed a third amendment to their revolving credit and security agreement, originally dated June 20, 2019.
- OFSCC-FS, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the Borrower, with OFS Capital Corporation acting as Servicer and OFSCC-FS Holdings, LLC as Equityholder.
- The lending syndicate includes BNP Paribas as Administrative Agent and Lender, and Mitsubishi HC Capital America, Inc. as Lender, with Citibank, N.A. serving as Collateral Agent and Virtus Group, LP as Collateral Administrator.
- The amendment extends the Final Maturity Date to June 20, 2027 and modifies the Reinvestment Period to terminate on August 31, 2025, providing the borrower with additional time for both facility maturity and reinvestment activities.
