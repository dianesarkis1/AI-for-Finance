## 1. Executive Summary

**Borrower / Company Overview:** ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP is a Delaware limited partnership that serves as the borrower, with ExchangeRight Income Fund (d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT), a Maryland REIT, as the parent company.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction is a Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement that adds Renasant Bank as a new lender with a $15,000,000 incremental revolving commitment to an existing revolving credit facility. **Purpose of Financing:** The financing increases the borrower's available revolving credit capacity under the existing credit agreement dated May 30, 2024, providing additional liquidity for general corporate purposes.

**Company Background:** ExchangeRight operates as a real estate investment trust focused on net leased portfolio properties. The company maintains a diversified structure through multiple subsidiary entities, including ExchangeRight Essential Income Strategy Properties 6, LLC and various Delaware statutory trusts (ExchangeRight Net Leased Portfolio 3-9 DST entities) that hold portfolio properties.
