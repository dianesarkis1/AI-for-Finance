## 1. Executive Summary

- In May 2022, Texas-New Mexico Power Company executed an increase supplement to expand its existing credit facility by adding a new lender.
- Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation, serves as the Borrower under a Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated March 11, 2022, with KeyBank National Association as Administrative Agent.
- The transaction involves adding CoBank, ACB as a new lender with a $25,000,000 commitment to the existing credit facility pursuant to Section 2.1(f) of the Credit Agreement.
- The purpose of this increase supplement is to expand the borrowing capacity under the existing credit facility. Concurrently, the Borrower issued Replacement First Mortgage Bonds under a Sixteenth Supplemental Indenture dated May 13, 2022, which are secured by mortgage bonds in accordance with the FMB Mortgage Documents.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established banking relationship with KeyBank National Association serving as Administrative Agent and existing lender
- Addition of CoBank, ACB (a cooperative bank specializing in agribusiness and rural infrastructure) demonstrates diversified lender appeal and confidence in the credit
- Texas-New Mexico Power Company operates as a regulated utility, typically providing stable cash flows and regulatory oversight
- Secured credit structure with First Mortgage Bonds providing collateral protection to lenders

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited financial information available in the supplement to assess overall credit profile and covenant compliance
- Utility sector exposure to regulatory changes and capital intensity requirements
- Increased debt capacity could potentially increase leverage if fully utilized
