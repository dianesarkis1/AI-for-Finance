## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation, operates as a regulated electric utility company.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents an increase supplement to the Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated March 11, 2022. The supplement adds CoBank, ACB as a new lender to the existing credit facility with KeyBank National Association serving as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The financing expands the borrowing capacity under the existing credit facility through the addition of a new $25,000,000 commitment from CoBank, ACB.

**Company Background:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company operates under a secured credit structure backed by first mortgage bonds. The company maintains an established banking relationship and has issued Replacement First Mortgage Bonds under a Sixteenth Supplemental Indenture dated May 13, 2022, which provides collateral security in accordance with the FMB Mortgage Documents.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established banking relationship with KeyBank National Association as Administrative Agent and existing lender
• Addition of CoBank, ACB demonstrates lender confidence and diversification of funding sources
• Regulated utility operations typically provide stable, predictable cash flows
• Secured credit structure with First Mortgage Bond collateral protection
• Borrower confirmed compliance with all covenant requirements under Section 2.1(f)

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Limited financial performance data available in supplement document
• Utility sector regulatory risk mitigated by regulated rate-setting framework
• Potential leverage increase if additional capacity is fully utilized
• Capital-intensive nature of utility operations requiring ongoing investment
