# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America

**Transaction Overview:** This is a consent agreement between Oil-Dri Corporation of America and BMO Harris Bank N.A. to modify an existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006. The transaction involves a one-time covenant modification rather than new financing.

**Purpose:** The modification allows Oil-Dri to add back a $5,644,000 non-cash goodwill impairment charge related to its Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment to the definitions of Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR for covenant calculation purposes. This ensures continued compliance with financial maintenance covenants following the impairment charge taken in Q1 2022.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates with multiple domestic subsidiaries serving as guarantors, including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company. The company has maintained a credit relationship with BMO Harris Bank since 2006.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established 16-year banking relationship demonstrates credit stability and lender confidence
• Proactive covenant management with borrower seeking modification before potential breach
• Strong guarantee structure with multiple subsidiary guarantors enhancing credit support
• No existing defaults or events of default per management representations
• One-time nature of impairment with clear scope and timeline limitations

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Goodwill impairment suggests underperformance or strategic challenges in Retail and Wholesale segment
• Covenant modification may indicate tight compliance headroom on financial metrics
• Limited operational visibility from amendment document alone regarding underlying business performance
• Risk mitigated by specific nature of adjustment and maintenance of all other covenant terms
