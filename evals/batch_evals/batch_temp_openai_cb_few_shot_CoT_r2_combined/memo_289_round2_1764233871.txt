# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-09-15

**Borrower/Company Overview:** F45 Training Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation, is the borrower under an existing credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and Australian Security Trustee.

**Transaction Description:** This is a consent agreement executed among F45 Training Holdings Inc. (Borrower), JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. (Administrative Agent and Lender), and Goldman Sachs Bank USA (Lender) to modify reporting requirements under the existing credit facility.

**Purpose of Financing:** The consent extends F45 Training Holdings' deadline to deliver its 2022 fiscal year audited consolidated financial statements and related compliance certificate from the original March 31, 2023 due date to September 30, 2023.

**Company Background:** F45 Training Holdings operates under an Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated August 13, 2021. The credit facility has undergone four subsequent amendments (December 2021, May 2022, February 2023, and June 2023), demonstrating an active and evolving banking relationship with institutional lenders.
