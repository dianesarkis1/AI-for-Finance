# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 30, 2020, Bank of Montreal as administrative agent granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver to MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, serves as the borrower under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019) with Bank of Montreal as administrative agent and various lenders.
- This waiver extends previous COVID-19 related relief through March 31, 2021, addressing casino facility closures mandated by Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) due to the pandemic.
- The purpose is to waive financial covenant breaches, closure-related defaults, and compliance certificate delivery requirements that resulted from government-mandated casino closures exceeding 60 consecutive days. The waiver extends the relief period until March 31, 2021 (the "Extended Waiver Period").
- MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities under agreements with OLG, which has provided financial relief including threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during the closure period.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Government support through OLG providing financial relief including threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during closure
- Liquidity maintenance requirement of minimum $15,000,000 provides downside protection
- Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
- Applicable margin increases to Level 5 pricing (5.00% for Bankers' Acceptances/Letters of Credit/LIBOR Loans) providing additional compensation for risk

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Casino operations remain highly vulnerable to government-mandated closures and regulatory restrictions
- Extended waiver period through March 31, 2021 indicates prolonged financial distress
- Borrower prohibited from requesting new advances under either credit facility during waiver period
- New event of default triggers if OLG ceases relief payments for more than 5 business days
- Distribution restrictions limit financial flexibility during recovery period
