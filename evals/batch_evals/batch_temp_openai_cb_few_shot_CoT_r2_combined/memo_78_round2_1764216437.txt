# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation in the transportation sector, with multiple subsidiary loan parties including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to existing Credit Agreement originally dated February 3, 2023. The transaction involves Saia Inc. as Borrower, subsidiary Loan Parties, and a banking syndicate with JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent, along with Bank of America N.A., PNC Bank National Association, TD Bank N.A., and BOKF NA dba Bank of Oklahoma as Lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** To amend Section 6.09 (Restrictive Agreements) of the existing Credit Agreement, specifically modifying clause (vi) to allow increased flexibility for unsecured indebtedness arrangements while preserving lender protection mechanisms.

**Company Background:** Saia Inc. operates in the transportation industry with an established syndicated credit facility. The company maintains operations through multiple subsidiary entities that serve as guarantors under the credit arrangement.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established relationship with high-quality institutional lender group including major U.S. banks
• No defaults or events of default reported; borrower maintains covenant compliance
• Multiple subsidiary guarantors provide enhanced credit protection
• Amendment preserves all existing loan documents, security interests, and lender rights
• Modification reflects normal course covenant adjustment rather than financial distress

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Covenant modification permits potentially higher leverage through additional unsecured debt capacity
• "Materiality" determination for restrictiveness comparison assigned to Borrower's discretion
• Insufficient visibility into current financial metrics, facility utilization, or debt service capacity
• Mitigating factors include mandatory pari passu lien provisions and equal and ratable treatment requirements for new unsecured debt
