# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2021-10-12

**Borrower/Company:** Emerson Electric Co. serves as the primary borrower under a syndicated Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018, with subsidiary borrowers also party to the facility and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This is a Suspension of Rights Agreement that temporarily modifies specific borrowing privileges under the existing credit facility in preparation for LIBOR cessation. The agreement suspends access to certain Non-USD currencies and interest rate tenors.

**Purpose:** To facilitate an orderly transition away from LIBOR ahead of the December 31, 2021 cessation of panel submissions for Non-USD Currency LIBOR tenors and 1-week/2-month USD LIBOR tenors, while deferring costs associated with formal credit agreement amendments.

**Company Background:** Emerson Electric Co. maintains an established multi-currency syndicated credit facility with institutional lenders, demonstrating access to sophisticated financing arrangements across multiple jurisdictions including USD, GBP, EUR, and JPY markets.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• **Institutional Support:** Established syndicated facility with JPMorgan Chase as Administrative Agent reflects strong banking relationships
• **Regulatory Compliance:** Proactive approach to LIBOR transition demonstrates sophisticated treasury management
• **Operational Continuity:** Automatic conversion mechanisms ensure uninterrupted facility access during transition
• **Reversible Framework:** Rights can be restored upon implementation of proper LIBOR replacement amendments

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
