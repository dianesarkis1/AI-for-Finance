# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2022-06-06

Oil-Dri Corporation of America entered into a consent agreement with BMO Harris Bank N.A. to modify their existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006. The borrower is Oil-Dri Corporation of America, with multiple domestic subsidiaries acting as guarantors, including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Mounds Management Inc., Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.

This transaction represents a one-time modification to accommodate a specific non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to the Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment taken during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022. The purpose is to modify the definitions of "Consolidated EBITDA" and "Consolidated EBITR" to add back this one-time impairment charge, ensuring the company remains in compliance with its financial covenants. The modifications are effective for the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022 and thereafter, applying to that quarter and any subsequent period that includes such fiscal quarter.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Long-standing credit relationship dating back to 2006, demonstrating stability and lender confidence
• Proactive covenant management approach, addressing compliance issues before potential default
• No current defaults or events of default exist under the credit agreement per company representations
• Multiple subsidiary guarantors provide enhanced credit support and security

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Goodwill impairment indicates potential operational challenges or asset value deterioration in the Retail and Wholesale segment
• EBITDA definition modifications could mask underlying business performance issues, though limited to this specific one-time charge
• Limited financial visibility from this document alone regarding overall liquidity position or business trends
