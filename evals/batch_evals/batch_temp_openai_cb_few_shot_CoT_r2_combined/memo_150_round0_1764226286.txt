# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On May 10, 2022, Sight Sciences, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) entered into a Third Amendment to its Amended and Restated Credit and Security Agreement (Revolving Loan) with MidCap Funding IV Trust as Agent and Lender.
- The original credit agreement was dated November 23, 2020, and has been previously amended in October 2021 and November 2021.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify terms related to the Lockbox Account, specifically introducing a "Lockbox Activation Date" that occurs five business days prior to the initial borrowing of Revolving Loans.
- The amendment establishes enhanced collection procedures requiring Account Debtors to pay directly into lockbox accounts following the Lockbox Activation Date, with daily transfers to the Payment Account.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established credit relationship with MidCap Funding IV Trust managed by Apollo Capital Management, indicating institutional lender confidence
- Enhanced cash collection controls through lockbox arrangements provide improved visibility and control over receivables for lenders
- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default exist under the financing documents
- All existing collateral liens and security interests remain in full force and effect

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- The amendment includes a broad lender release provision, which may limit borrower recourse against lenders
- Enhanced lockbox controls suggest lenders may be seeking tighter oversight of cash collections, potentially indicating credit concerns
- Limited financial information available to assess overall credit quality and performance metrics
