# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In December 2023, Security Life of Denver Insurance Company provided a Class A Note to APA Finance III Borrower, LLC in the principal amount of $19,195,000.
- APA Finance III Borrower, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company that is ultimately owned by Altus Power, Inc. through a multi-tier holding company structure (APA Finance III Borrower Holdings, LLC → APA Finance III, LLC → APA Generation, LLC → Altus Power, Inc.).
- This Class A Note is part of a broader credit facility governed by a Credit Agreement originally dated February 15, 2023, which has been amended three times (June 15, 2023, July 21, 2023, and December 20, 2023).
- The note evidences Class A Loans made under the Credit Agreement, with U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association serving as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, and U.S. Bank National Association as Document Custodian.
