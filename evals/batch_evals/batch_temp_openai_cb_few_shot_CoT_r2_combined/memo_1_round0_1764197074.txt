# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 30, 2020, Bank of Montreal (as Administrative Agent) and MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. entered into a Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver agreement.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, serves as the Borrower with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor under the original credit agreement dated June 10, 2019.
- This waiver extends COVID-19 related relief through November 30, 2020, addressing casino closure defaults and financial covenant breaches caused by government-mandated shutdowns.
- The financing structure includes revolving credit facilities with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent. The waiver provides temporary relief from various defaults while imposing enhanced liquidity monitoring and operational restrictions.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were temporarily closed due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions, with Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) providing certain financial relief measures.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Government support through OLG financial relief packages, including threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments
- Enhanced liquidity monitoring with mandatory minimum liquidity requirement of $15 million
- Borrower remains current on representations and warranties with no additional defaults beyond COVID-related issues
- Structured waiver period with clear expiration (November 30, 2020) and defined conditions

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Casino closure exceeding 60 consecutive days triggers automatic default under normal circumstances
- Financial covenant breaches in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicate deteriorating financial performance
- Prohibition on new advances under credit facilities during waiver period limits financial flexibility
- Applicable margin increased to Level 5 (highest pricing tier), indicating elevated credit risk perception
