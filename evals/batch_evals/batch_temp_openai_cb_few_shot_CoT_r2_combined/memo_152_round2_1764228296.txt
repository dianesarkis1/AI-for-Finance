# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-02-20

**Borrower/Company Overview:** CrossAmerica Partners LP, a Delaware limited partnership, and Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation, operate in the fuel distribution and convenience retail sector with comprehensive subsidiary operations across multiple states.

**Transaction Description:** First Amendment to Amended and Restated Credit Agreement executed with Citizens Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of 13 lenders including Atlantic Union Bank, Bank of America N.A., Capital One National Association, Fifth Third Bank National Association, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Manufacturers and Traders Trust Company, MUFG Bank Ltd., Raymond James Bank, TD Bank N.A., Truist Bank, Webster Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., and Citizens Bank N.A.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment provides covenant relief to facilitate the acquisition of assets from Applegreen Midwest LLC and Applegreen Florida, LLC under the Applegreen Purchase Agreement dated January 26, 2024, by modifying EBITDA calculation parameters to exclude lease termination fees from existing limitations.

**Company Background:** CrossAmerica Partners operates through multiple subsidiaries including LGP Operations LLC, Express Lane Inc., Petroleum Marketers Incorporated, and CAPL Retail LLC, maintaining a diversified fuel wholesale and convenience store platform. The credit facility originates from an original credit agreement dated April 1, 2019, which was amended and restated on March 31, 2023.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong lender syndicate with 13 financial institutions including major money center banks demonstrates solid market confidence in credit quality
• Strategic acquisition-focused amendment indicates lender support for growth initiatives and platform expansion
• Comprehensive guarantee structure with multiple subsidiary guarantors enhances credit protection and recovery prospects
• All existing collateral, loan documents, and financial covenants remain fully enforceable
• Amendment tied to specific acquisition completion provides targeted covenant relief rather than broad modifications

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Covenant modification removes lease termination fee limitations for Applegreen acquisition, potentially increasing financial flexibility at lender expense
• Amendment effectiveness contingent on successful Applegreen acquisition completion creates execution risk
• Limited transparency on underlying facility terms including size, pricing, and maturity
• Risks mitigated by requirement for Required Lender consent and continued enforcement of all other existing credit terms
