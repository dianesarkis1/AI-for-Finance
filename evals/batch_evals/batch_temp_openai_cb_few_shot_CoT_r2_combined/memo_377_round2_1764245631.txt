# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Five entertainment library financing entities: LGTV Library Financing LLC, Artisan Library Financing LLC, Summit Library Financing LLC, LG Film Library Financing LLC, and LGIM Library UK Financing Limited, along with their respective parent holding companies.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to an Amended and Restated Credit, Security and Pledge Agreement, featuring a facility upsize that modifies outstanding loan amounts across a 14-member lender syndicate led by Fifth Third Bank as Administrative Agent and East West Bank as Reserve Deposit Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates an upsize to the existing credit facility supporting entertainment content library assets. The financing is structured through specialized vehicles holding content libraries across multiple entertainment brands.

**Company Background:** Multi-entity entertainment library financing structure originally established November 5, 2024, with first amendment in December 2024. The rapid amendment cycle indicates active capital deployment in the entertainment content sector.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Diversified 14-member lender syndicate including major regional and national banks
• Specialized entertainment library financing structure with asset segregation across content brands
• Recent facility upsize demonstrates lender confidence and borrower performance
• Comprehensive guarantee structure with five parent holding companies
• Reserve Deposit Agent structure provides additional security and operational oversight
• International diversification through UK financing entity

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Entertainment content libraries subject to valuation volatility based on performance cycles
• Complex multi-jurisdictional structure may create operational complexity
• Limited seasoning since November 2024 origination
• Content library assets exposed to technological disruption and changing distribution models
• Amendment references specific loan amounts in undisclosed Schedule 1.1, limiting transparency on exact facility size and lender allocations
