## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company is a Texas corporation operating as a regulated electric utility.

**Transaction Description:** This is an increase supplement to the Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated March 11, 2022. The supplement adds CoBank, ACB as a new lender with a $25,000,000 commitment to the existing syndicated credit facility administered by KeyBank National Association.

**Purpose of Financing:** The supplement increases the total available borrowing capacity under the existing credit facility by adding CoBank, ACB's $25,000,000 commitment to the lending syndicate.

**Company Background:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company maintains a secured credit facility backed by first mortgage bonds. Concurrent with this increase supplement, the company issued Replacement First Mortgage Bonds under a Sixteenth Supplemental Indenture dated May 13, 2022, which serve as collateral security under the credit facility structure.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Regulated utility operations provide stable, predictable cash flows and rate recovery mechanisms
• Secured credit structure with First Mortgage Bond collateral providing enhanced creditor protection
• Successful addition of CoBank, ACB demonstrates continued lender confidence and expands funding diversification
• Established administrative framework with KeyBank National Association as agent
• Borrower confirmed full compliance with all facility conditions and covenant requirements

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Regulatory oversight may limit operational flexibility, though provides earnings stability through rate-setting processes
• Capital-intensive utility operations require ongoing investment in infrastructure and equipment
• Increased borrowing capacity may lead to higher leverage if fully utilized
• Limited visibility into current financial metrics and performance trends from supplement documentation
