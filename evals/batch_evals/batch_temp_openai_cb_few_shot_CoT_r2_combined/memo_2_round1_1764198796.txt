# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator that manages casino facilities under agreements with Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG). The company operates gaming facilities including slot machines, table games, and lottery schemes.

**Transaction Structure:** Bank of Montreal, as administrative agent, granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver on behalf of the Required Lenders. This waiver amends and restates previous COVID-19 relief measures under a credit agreement originally executed on June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019) with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor.

**Purpose:** The waiver addresses financial covenant breaches and compliance defaults resulting from government-mandated casino closures exceeding 60 consecutive days due to COVID-19. It extends relief through March 31, 2021, waiving financial covenant breaches under Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2, closure-related defaults, and compliance certificate delivery requirements.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment operates under the COSA agreement with OLG, which has provided financial relief including threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during mandated closures.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government support through OLG providing financial relief including threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during closure
• Enhanced liquidity protection with minimum $15,000,000 requirement and weekly reporting
• Increased pricing compensation through Level 5 margin structure
• Structured monitoring through weekly liquidity reconciliation and management commentary

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extended waiver period indicates prolonged operational and financial distress from pandemic impacts
• Borrower prohibited from accessing new funding under either credit facility during waiver period
• New event of default triggers if OLG relief payments cease for more than 5 consecutive business days
• Operational restrictions including distribution limitations and acquisition prohibitions limit strategic flexibility
• Dependence on continued government support and regulatory approval for facility reopening
