# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On March 31, 2022, Vertical Horizons, Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company, entered into Amendment No. 1 to their Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated December 28, 2021.
- The borrower is part of the Frontier Airlines group structure, with Frontier Group Holdings, Inc., Frontier Airlines Holdings, Inc., and Frontier Airlines, Inc. serving as guarantors.
- The lending syndicate includes Citibank N.A. (as Facility Agent and Arranger), Barclays Bank PLC, Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc., with Bank of Utah serving as Security Trustee.
- This amendment relates to PDP (Pre-Delivery Payment) financing for a total of 59 Airbus aircraft: nine (9) Airbus A320NEO aircraft and fifty (50) Airbus A321NEO aircraft.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to extend certain deadline provisions and update related assignment and step-in agreements with Airbus.
