# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 26, 2020, PNM Resources, Inc., a New Mexico corporation, entered into an eighth amendment to its credit agreement with Wells Fargo Bank as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders including MUFG Union Bank, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase Bank, Morgan Stanley Bank, Royal Bank of Canada, KeyBank National Association, Bank of America, U.S. Bank National Association, The Bank of New York Mellon, and BOKF.
- The original credit agreement was established on July 30, 2018, and has been previously amended multiple times, most recently in December 2018.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the "Change of Control" definition and related merger provisions in connection with PNM Resources' entry into a merger agreement with Avangrid, Inc. and NM Green Holdings, Inc. dated October 20, 2020.
- The amendment provides specific waivers for potential covenant defaults that may have arisen from entering into the merger agreement, while clarifying that the actual closing of the merger will still constitute a prohibited change of control event.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong syndicate of major financial institutions including Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, and other established lenders, indicating broad lender confidence
- Proactive approach by borrower to address potential covenant violations through formal amendment process rather than allowing defaults to persist
- Limited scope of waivers that are specifically defined and do not create broader precedent for future covenant relief
- Borrower representations confirm no other defaults are occurring and all other credit agreement terms remain in full force

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- The pending merger transaction creates uncertainty around future ownership structure and potential acceleration of credit facilities upon closing
- Amendment acknowledges that actual merger closing will constitute a change of control and prohibited event, potentially triggering facility termination
- Limited financial information available to assess borrower's current credit profile or ability to refinance upon merger completion
- Cross-default provisions may be triggered by other indebtedness related to the merger agreement
