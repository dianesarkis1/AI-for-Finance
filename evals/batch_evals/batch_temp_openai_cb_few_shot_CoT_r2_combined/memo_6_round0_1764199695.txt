# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On July 25, 2025, Universal Electronics Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into a tenth amendment to its Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement with U.S. Bank National Association as Administrative Agent and Lender.
- The original credit agreement was dated October 27, 2017 and has been extensively amended nine times previously, with amendments occurring regularly from 2018 through 2024.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the liens provision in Section 6.16(i) of the Credit Agreement, specifically regarding liens in favor of Bank of China securing indebtedness up to $21,000,000, provided such liens do not extend to any property located in the United States.
- Universal Electronics Inc. operates with a comprehensive security package including security agreements, trademark and patent grants, and is supported by Universal Electronics BV as guarantor under a continuing guaranty dated October 25, 2021.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:

- Established banking relationship with U.S. Bank National Association serving multiple roles as Lender, LC Issuer, and Administrative Agent, indicating consolidated relationship management
- Comprehensive collateral package including security agreements, intellectual property grants (trademarks and patents), and continuing guaranty from Universal Electronics BV
- Long-term credit relationship with original agreement dating to 2017, suggesting lender confidence and borrower stability
- Borrower reaffirms compliance with all existing covenants and represents no defaults exist under the credit agreement

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:

- Frequent amendment history with ten amendments since 2017 may indicate ongoing covenant management or changing business needs
- Amendment allows for liens in favor of Bank of China up to $21,000,000, introducing additional secured creditor but limited to non-U.S. property
- Limited financial information available to assess overall credit profile and borrowing capacity utilization
