# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation operating in the transportation sector with multiple subsidiary entities including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Credit Agreement between Saia Inc. as Borrower, subsidiary Loan Parties, a syndicate of lenders including JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Bank of America N.A., PNC Bank National Association, TD Bank N.A., and BOKF NA dba Bank of Oklahoma, with JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. serving as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies restrictive covenant provisions to provide increased financial flexibility for unsecured indebtedness transactions while maintaining appropriate lender protections.

**Company Background:** Saia Inc. operates as a transportation company with an established credit facility originally executed on February 3, 2023, supported by multiple subsidiary guarantors across its business operations.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender syndicate comprising tier-1 U.S. banks demonstrates market confidence
• Borrower maintains full covenant compliance with no existing defaults or events of default
• Multiple subsidiary guarantors provide enhanced credit support through cross-guarantees
• All existing loan documentation, security interests, and collateral remain fully enforceable
• Amendment represents negotiated modification rather than distressed restructuring

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Covenant relaxation allows potentially increased leverage through unsecured debt permitted under Section 6.01
• Amendment language includes subjective standard requiring restrictions be "not materially more restrictive" as determined by Borrower
• Limited transparency into current facility utilization, financial performance metrics, or total debt capacity
• Risk mitigation includes requirement for pari passu lien rights and "equal and ratable" treatment provisions
