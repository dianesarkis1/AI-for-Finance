# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 26, 2024, IPC Alternative Real Estate Operating Partnership, LP and Inland Private Capital Corporation executed a modification to their existing loan documents.
- IPC Alternative Real Estate Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership based in Oak Brook, IL, serves as the Borrower, while Inland Private Capital Corporation, a Delaware corporation also based in Oak Brook, IL, acts as the Lender.
- The transaction involves a revolving credit facility originally established on October 27, 2023, with a principal amount of $22,500,000.
- The primary purpose of this modification is to extend the maturity date of the credit agreement from November 30, 2024, to November 30, 2025, providing the borrower with an additional year of credit availability.
- Both entities appear to be related companies within the Inland Private Capital organization, as evidenced by their shared Oak Brook, Illinois address and corporate structure.
