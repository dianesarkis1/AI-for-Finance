# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On August 18, 2022, Halozyme Therapeutics, Inc. entered into Amendment No. 1 to its Credit Agreement originally dated May 24, 2022, with Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent.
- Halozyme Therapeutics is a Delaware corporation serving as the Borrower, with Halozyme, Inc. and Antares Pharma, Inc. as Guarantors.
- The amendment increases the Revolving Credit Commitments by $225,000,000 in Incremental Revolving Commitments, bringing the total aggregate Revolving Credit Commitments to $575,000,000.
- The facility involves a syndicate of major financial institutions including Bank of America, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Mizuho Bank, Silicon Valley Bank, Fifth Third Bank, MUFG Union Bank, and City National Bank as Increase Loan Lenders.
- The purpose includes general corporate purposes in accordance with Section 6.11 of the Credit Agreement, with proceeds from the facility to be used alongside convertible notes issued on the same date. The Borrower was required to repay all outstanding Term A Loans as part of the amendment.
