# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 30, 2021, Team, Inc., a Delaware corporation, executed Amendment No. 1 to its Unsecured Term Loan Credit Agreement with Corre fund entities (Corre Opportunities Qualified Master Fund LP, Corre Horizon Fund LP, and Corre Horizon Fund II LP).
- The original Unsecured Term Loan Credit Agreement was dated November 9, 2021, making this a very early amendment to a recently executed facility.
- The amendment provides extensions for three key obligations: (i) extends the payment date for PIK Interest on Initial Term Loans from December 1, 2021 to December 6, 2021, (ii) extends the deadline for delivering a fully executed ABL Consent to December 6, 2021, and (iii) extends the date for issuing Underlying Warrants to December 7, 2021.
- Team, Inc. appears to be seeking operational flexibility through these short-term extensions of critical covenant and payment obligations.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- The amendments represent relatively minor extensions of only a few days, suggesting temporary operational issues rather than fundamental credit deterioration
- The borrower proactively sought amendments rather than defaulting on obligations, demonstrating cooperative lender relationship management
- All existing loan documents and covenants remain in full force and effect except for the specific amendments

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- The need for an amendment within three weeks of the original agreement suggests potential execution or operational challenges
- PIK Interest payment extension indicates potential near-term cash flow constraints
- The involvement of warrant issuance and ABL Consent requirements suggests complex capital structure that may indicate financial stress
- Limited information available on overall credit profile, financial performance, or collateral structure
