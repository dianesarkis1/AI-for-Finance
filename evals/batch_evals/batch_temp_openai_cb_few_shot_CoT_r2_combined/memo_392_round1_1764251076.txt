# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2021-10-12

**Borrower/Company:** Emerson Electric Co. (the "Company") serves as the primary borrower, with subsidiary borrowers also party to the underlying credit facility established under a Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018.

**Transaction Description:** Suspension of Rights Agreement between Emerson Electric Co. and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent. This agreement temporarily suspends specific borrowing rights under the existing credit facility related to LIBOR transition.

**Purpose:** To manage the orderly transition away from LIBOR rates ahead of the December 31, 2021 cessation date for certain LIBOR tenors, while deferring amendment costs until alternative benchmark rates are established.

**Company Background:** Emerson Electric Co. is a multinational manufacturing and technology company with established institutional banking relationships and access to multi-currency credit facilities through a syndicated lending arrangement.
