# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-10-26

**Borrower/Company Overview:** PNM Resources, Inc., a New Mexico corporation, operating under a syndicated credit facility with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as Administrative Agent and MUFG Bank, Ltd. serving as sole bookrunner and lead arranger for this amendment.

**Transaction Description:** Eighth amendment to existing credit agreement originally established July 30, 2018. The amendment modifies the "Change of Control" definition and merger-related covenant provisions to address PNM Resources' entry into a merger agreement with Avangrid, Inc. and NM Green Holdings, Inc.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment provides limited waivers for potential covenant defaults arising from the borrower's entry into the merger agreement dated October 20, 2020, while maintaining existing credit facility terms and preserving lender rights upon actual merger completion.

**Company Background:** PNM Resources maintains relationships with a strong syndicate of established financial institutions, including Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Citibank, MUFG Union Bank, Morgan Stanley Bank, Royal Bank of Canada, KeyBank, U.S. Bank, The Bank of New York Mellon, and BOKF.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• Strong syndicate composition with major financial institutions demonstrates broad lender confidence
• Proactive covenant management through formal amendment process rather than allowing defaults to persist
• Limited and precisely defined waiver scope prevents precedent for future covenant relief
• Borrower representations confirm absence of other defaults and maintenance of all existing obligations
• Amendment requires payment of all fees and expenses to Administrative Agent and Lead Arranger as condition precedent

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• Pending merger creates ownership uncertainty and potential facility acceleration upon closing
• Amendment explicitly preserves lenders' rights to declare change of control upon merger completion
• Limited financial disclosure restricts assessment of current credit profile and refinancing capacity
• Potential cross-default exposure from other indebtedness related to merger transaction
• Amendment waiver limited to entering the merger agreement only, not the actual transaction closing
