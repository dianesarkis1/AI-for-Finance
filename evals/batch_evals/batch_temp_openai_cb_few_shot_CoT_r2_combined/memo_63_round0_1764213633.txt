# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On April 25, 2024, Accuray Incorporated entered into a Third Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Silicon Valley Bank (now a division of First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company) as Administrative Agent and other lenders including HSBC Bank US.
- Accuray Incorporated is a Delaware corporation that serves as the Borrower under a credit facility originally established on May 6, 2021, previously amended in October 2022 and November 2023.
- The amendment modifies the definition of "Consolidated EBITDA" to include specific addbacks related to restructuring charges and cost savings from headcount reductions that occurred after September 30, 2023 through the amendment effective date.
- The purpose of this amendment is to provide temporary EBITDA adjustments to accommodate recent workforce reductions and associated restructuring activities, with time-limited addbacks that phase out by December 31, 2024.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• Established banking relationships with reputable institutions including Silicon Valley Bank/First-Citizens and HSBC Bank US
• Amendment includes release of claims provisions, suggesting amicable lender-borrower relationship
• Temporary nature of EBITDA adjustments with specific sunset dates by December 31, 2024
• Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default continuing after the amendment

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• Amendment appears to be borrower-friendly, allowing significant EBITDA addbacks (up to $6.8M for certain periods) that may mask underlying operational challenges
• Restructuring activities and headcount reductions suggest potential operational difficulties requiring covenant relief
• Limited visibility into underlying credit facility terms, pricing, or broader financial performance metrics
