# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-29

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC is a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Vine Energy Operating LP, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, and various other energy-related entities.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to Second Lien Credit Agreement between Vine Energy Holdings LLC as borrower, Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, and multiple institutional lenders primarily managed by GoldenTree Asset Management, LP. This amendment modifies the original Second Lien Credit Agreement dated December 30, 2020.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies the minimum hedging requirements under the original credit agreement, establishing new covenant terms for natural gas production hedging with enhanced flexibility provisions.

**Company Background:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC operates an upstream energy business focused on natural gas production from Proved Developed Producing Reserves, with a comprehensive subsidiary structure providing operational diversification across the energy value chain.
