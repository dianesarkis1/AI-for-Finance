# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-12-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation, a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower under this incremental commitment agreement. The company operates as a specialty lending corporation and is supported by subsidiary guarantors OCSL SRNE, LLC (a Delaware limited liability company) and FSFC Holdings, Inc. (a Delaware corporation).

**Transaction Description:** This transaction constitutes an incremental commitment increase under an existing Amended and Restated Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated February 25, 2019. MUFG Union Bank, N.A. serves as the Increasing Lender, providing an additional $25,000,000 commitment that expands its total commitment from $50,000,000 to $75,000,000. ING Capital LLC acts as Administrative Agent and Issuing Bank.

**Purpose of Financing:** The incremental commitment provides additional revolving credit capacity under the existing senior secured credit facility. The new loans will carry terms identical to existing facility terms and conditions, maintaining structural consistency across the credit arrangement.

**Company Background:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation operates within an established credit structure dating to February 2019. The original facility was arranged by a high-quality banking syndicate including ING Capital LLC, JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., and Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated as Joint Lead Arrangers and Joint Bookrunners, with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and Bank of America, N.A. serving as Syndication Agents.
