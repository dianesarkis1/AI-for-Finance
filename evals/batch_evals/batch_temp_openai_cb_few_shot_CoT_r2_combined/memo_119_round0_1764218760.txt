# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On April 14, 2023, Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. executed a Guarantor Joinder Agreement to join as new guarantors under Apollo Management Holdings, L.P.'s existing credit agreement dated October 12, 2022.
- Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower of the Revolving Facility, with Citibank, N.A. acting as Administrative Agent.
- The original credit agreement includes multiple Initial Guarantors, primarily Cayman Islands exempted limited partnerships and related Apollo entities (Apollo Principal Holdings I through XII, AMH Holdings (Cayman), L.P., and others).
- The purpose of this joinder is to add Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. as guarantors to induce lenders to maintain and/or make additional loans and issuing banks to maintain and/or issue additional letters of credit.
- Apollo is a leading global alternative investment manager, and this transaction expands the guarantee support for the existing revolving credit facility by bringing in additional Apollo entities.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- **Strong Sponsor**: Apollo Global Management is a well-established, publicly-traded alternative investment manager with significant assets under management and diversified revenue streams.
- **Enhanced Credit Support**: Addition of new guarantors strengthens the overall credit profile by expanding the pool of entities backing the facility obligations.
- **Established Banking Relationship**: Citibank, N.A. serves as Administrative Agent, indicating a relationship with a major financial institution.
- **Operational Continuity**: The joinder maintains all existing terms of the original credit agreement while adding incremental support.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- **Limited Financial Visibility**: The joinder document does not provide specific financial metrics, covenants, or pricing information to assess current credit performance.
- **Complex Corporate Structure**: The multiple guarantor entities across different jurisdictions (Delaware, Cayman Islands, Anguilla) may present operational and legal complexity.
