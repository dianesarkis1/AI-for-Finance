# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On May 10, 2024, LifeStance Health Holdings, Inc. entered into a Second Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Capital One and other lenders.
- LifeStance Health Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower with Lynnwood Intermediate Holdings, Inc. as the parent holding company. Capital One, National Association acts as Administrative Agent, Revolver Agent, and Collateral Agent.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated May 4, 2022, with a First Amendment executed on November 7, 2023. The lending syndicate includes Capital One and Unitranche Loan Transaction II, LLC (managed by Capital One and HPS Investment Partners).
- The purpose of this Second Amendment is to extend the "Delayed Draw Term Loan Commitment Expiration Date" from its previous date to August 14, 2024, providing additional time for the borrower to draw down on its delayed draw term loan facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:

• Borrower reaffirms all payment and performance obligations and confirms no existing defaults or events of default are continuing
• All existing loan documents, security interests, and liens remain in full force and effect, maintaining collateral protection
• Amendment extends commitment period, providing operational flexibility without fundamental credit restructuring
