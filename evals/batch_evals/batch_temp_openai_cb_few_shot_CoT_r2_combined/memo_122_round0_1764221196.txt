# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On December 28, 2020, Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation entered into an incremental commitment agreement to increase its existing revolving credit facility.
- Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation, a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower, with OCSL SRNE, LLC and FSFC Holdings, Inc. as Subsidiary Guarantors.
- The transaction involves MUFG Union Bank, N.A. as the Increasing Lender providing an additional $25,000,000 commitment, bringing MUFG's total commitment from $50,000,000 to $75,000,000.
- This incremental commitment relates to an Amended and Restated Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated February 25, 2019, with ING Capital LLC as Administrative Agent and a syndicate including JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and Bank of America, N.A. as Syndication Agents.
- The purpose is to provide additional revolving credit capacity under the existing senior secured credit facility, with identical terms to existing loans and commitments.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong institutional sponsor backing with Oaktree, a well-established alternative investment manager
- Diversified lending syndicate including major financial institutions (ING Capital, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, MUFG Union Bank)
- MUFG's willingness to increase its commitment from $50mm to $75mm demonstrates continued lender confidence
- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default are continuing
- Senior secured structure with collateral protection and subsidiary guarantees

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited visibility into overall facility size, pricing, or financial performance metrics
- As a specialty lending corporation, Oaktree's credit profile is tied to the performance of its underlying loan portfolio
- Information constraints prevent assessment of broader covenant compliance or liquidity position
