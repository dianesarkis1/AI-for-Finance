# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 6, 2024, three new borrowers (Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC, Innovex International, Inc., and TIW Corporation) joined an existing credit facility through a joinder agreement with PNC Bank National Association as agent.
- The existing borrowers include Tercel Oilfield Products USA L.L.C., Pride Energy Services, LLC, and Top-Co Inc., all operating in the oilfield services sector.
- This transaction stems from the Dril-Quip Merger, where Innovex merged with DQ Merger Sub, LLC (now Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC), with the surviving entity becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Innovex International, Inc. (formerly Dril-Quip, Inc.).
- The original credit agreement is a Second Amended and Restated Revolving Credit, Term Loan, Guaranty and Security Agreement dated June 10, 2022, which has been amended multiple times through June 28, 2024.
- The purpose is to integrate the new entities resulting from the merger into the existing credit facility, with PNC Bank serving as administrative agent alongside U.S. Bank National Association as a participating lender.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established credit relationship with multiple amendments indicating ongoing lender support and active management of the facility since 2022
- Strong collateral package including continuing security interests, liens on all collateral, and pledge agreements covering equity interests
- Joint and several liability structure provides enhanced credit support across all borrowing entities
- Comprehensive post-closing covenant structure ensures proper integration and documentation of new borrowers
- Professional management with Adam Anderson serving as Chief Executive Officer across multiple entities

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Integration risk associated with merger transaction and addition of three new borrowers to existing credit structure
- Post-closing requirements create execution risk, including delivery of stock certificates, insurance documentation, and intellectual property assignments within specified timeframes (5-65 days)
- Limited visibility into financial performance metrics, pricing terms, or total facility size from this joinder document
- Multiple previous amendments suggest potential ongoing covenant or structural issues requiring frequent modifications
