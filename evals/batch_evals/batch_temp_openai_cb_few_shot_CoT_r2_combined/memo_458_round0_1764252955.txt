# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On February 10, 2022, SESI, L.L.C. and its lenders executed a Third Amendment to their Credit Agreement originally dated February 2, 2021.
- SESI, L.L.C., a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the Borrower, with SESI Holdings, Inc. as Parent company and multiple subsidiary guarantors in the energy services sector including Superior Energy Services entities, Wild Well Control, Inc., and Warrior Energy Services Corporation.
- JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. acts as Administrative Agent, with participating lenders including Bank of America, N.A. and Citibank, N.A.
- The amendment modifies asset disposition restrictions and lien release provisions, providing the borrower with greater operational flexibility for property sales and transfers.
- SESI operates in the energy services industry, with subsidiaries providing drilling, well services, inspection services, and related oilfield support activities.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong banking consortium led by JPMorgan Chase with participation from major institutions (Bank of America, Citibank), indicating continued lender support
- Borrower represents and warrants full compliance with all loan covenants and confirms no defaults are occurring
- Amendment includes borrower release of claims against lenders, suggesting cooperative relationship
- Liquidity and availability thresholds ($100 million and $25 million respectively) provide financial cushion for certain asset disposition activities

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Energy services sector exposure with inherent commodity price volatility and cyclical business risks
- Relaxed asset disposition covenants may indicate potential need for asset sales or restructuring activities
- Multiple prior amendments and waivers since original 2021 agreement suggest ongoing covenant management issues
- Limited financial information available to assess overall credit profile and performance metrics
