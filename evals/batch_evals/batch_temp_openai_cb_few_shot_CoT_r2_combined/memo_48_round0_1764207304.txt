# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 17, 2022, Spectrum Brands, Inc. and its lending syndicate led by Royal Bank of Canada executed a fourth amendment to their amended and restated credit agreement originally dated June 23, 2015 and restated June 30, 2020.
- Spectrum Brands, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Lead Borrower with SB/RH Holdings, LLC as Holdings company. Royal Bank of Canada acts as Administrative Agent with participating Revolving Lenders.
- The amendment provides covenant relief by temporarily increasing the Total Net Leverage Ratio limit from 6.00:1.00 to 7.00:1.00 until a "Trigger Event" occurs, giving the company additional operational flexibility.
- The purpose is to provide Spectrum Brands additional time and flexibility to complete the sale of its Hardware and Home Improvement segment to ASSA ABLOY AB for $4.3 billion in cash proceeds under an Asset and Stock Purchase Agreement dated September 8, 2021.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Pending asset sale of $4.3 billion provides significant deleveraging opportunity and strategic focus
- Strong banking relationship with Royal Bank of Canada as Administrative Agent and established revolving lender syndicate
- Amendment includes fee payments to lenders (0.20% of commitments), demonstrating borrower's commitment to maintaining lender relationships
- Temporary nature of covenant relief with clear trigger events and reversion to tighter covenant

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Leverage covenant relief indicates potential near-term financial stress or covenant proximity
- Dependency on asset sale completion creates execution risk, with covenant relief expiring by September 29, 2023 if sale doesn't close
- Limited visibility into overall credit facility size, pricing, or other financial maintenance covenants
