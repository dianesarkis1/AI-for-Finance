# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-09-15

**Borrower/Company Overview:** F45 Training Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation, operates under an existing credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent and Australian Security Trustee.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction involves a consent agreement between F45 Training Holdings Inc., JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. (as Administrative Agent), and Goldman Sachs Bank USA (as Lender) to extend financial reporting deadlines under the existing Amended and Restated Credit Agreement.

**Purpose of Financing:** The consent provides F45 Training Holdings with an extension to deliver its 2022 fiscal year audited financial statements and accompanying compliance certificate, with the delivery deadline extended from March 31, 2023 to September 30, 2023.

**Company Background:** F45 Training Holdings Inc. is the borrower under an Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally executed on August 13, 2021, which has been subsequently amended four times through June 30, 2023, indicating an evolving credit relationship with institutional lenders.
