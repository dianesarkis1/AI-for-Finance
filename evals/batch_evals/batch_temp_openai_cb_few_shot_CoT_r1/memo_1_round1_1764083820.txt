## 1. Executive Summary

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operating company with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor.

**Transaction Description:** Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver granted by Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent on behalf of Required Lenders, extending COVID-19 related accommodations through November 30, 2020.

**Purpose of Financing:** This waiver addresses casino facility closures mandated by Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) due to the COVID-19 pandemic, providing relief from closure defaults, financial covenant breaches, and compliance certificate delivery requirements.
