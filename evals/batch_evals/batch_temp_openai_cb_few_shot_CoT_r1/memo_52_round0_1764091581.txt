# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 26, 2020, PNM Resources, Inc., a New Mexico corporation, entered into an Eighth Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- The original Credit Agreement was a Sixth Amendment to and Restatement of Credit Agreement dated July 30, 2018, which was previously amended by a Seventh Amendment dated December 19, 2018.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the "Change of Control" definition and address potential covenant defaults arising from PNM Resources entering into a merger agreement with Avangrid, Inc. and NM Green Holdings, Inc. dated October 20, 2020.
- PNM Resources is a utility holding company that has entered into a merger agreement but requires amendments and waivers to avoid triggering defaults under its existing credit facility. The lending syndicate includes major financial institutions: Wells Fargo (Administrative Agent), MUFG Union Bank, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley Bank, Royal Bank of Canada, KeyBank, Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Bank of New York Mellon, and Bank of Albuquerque.
