## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-04-14

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit facility. The broader Apollo organization includes Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc., both of which are joining as additional guarantors.

**Transaction Description:** This is a guarantor joinder agreement adding two new guarantors to an existing revolving credit facility. The transaction involves Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. becoming additional guarantors under a credit agreement originally dated October 12, 2022, with Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The additional guarantors are joining to induce lenders to maintain and/or make additional loans and issuing banks to maintain and/or issue additional letters of credit, while also serving as consideration for loans previously made and letters of credit previously issued.

**Company Background:** Apollo operates through a complex structure with multiple holding entities across jurisdictions including Delaware, Cayman Islands, and Anguilla. The original credit facility includes thirteen initial guarantors, indicating a substantial organizational structure supporting the credit facility.
