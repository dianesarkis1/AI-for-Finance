# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower under a credit agreement with BMO Harris Bank N.A., with multiple domestic subsidiaries as guarantors including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Mounds Management Inc., Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.

**Transaction Description:** This is a consent agreement modifying an existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006. The transaction involves Oil-Dri Corporation of America as borrower and BMO Harris Bank N.A. as lender, with subsidiary guarantors providing continued support.

**Purpose of Financing:** The purpose is to obtain lender consent for a one-time modification to financial covenant definitions, specifically allowing the addition of a $5,644,000 non-cash goodwill impairment charge to Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations for covenant compliance purposes.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates with a Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment that experienced a significant goodwill impairment during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022. The company maintains a long-standing credit relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating back to 2006.
