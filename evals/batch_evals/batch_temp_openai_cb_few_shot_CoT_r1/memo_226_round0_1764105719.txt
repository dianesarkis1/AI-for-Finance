## 1. Executive Summary

- On March 31, 2022, Vertical Horizons, Ltd. (a Cayman Islands company) entered into Amendment No. 1 to their Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated December 28, 2021.
- The borrower is Vertical Horizons, Ltd., with Citibank, N.A. serving as both Facility Agent and Arranger, alongside a syndicate of lenders including Barclays Bank PLC, Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc.
- This amendment relates to PDP (Pre-Delivery Payment) financing for aircraft acquisition - specifically nine (9) Airbus A320NEO aircraft and fifty (50) Airbus A321NEO aircraft.
