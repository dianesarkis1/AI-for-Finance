# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-29

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector, serves as the Borrower with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Vine Oil & Gas GP LLC, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, Vine Oil & Gas Finance Corp., and other related entities.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to Second Lien Credit Agreement between Vine Energy Holdings LLC and its lender syndicate, with Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. serving as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies minimum hedging requirements under the existing second lien credit facility, specifically adjusting Section 6.17 to require 70% hedging of reasonably anticipated projected hydrocarbon production of natural gas from Proved Developed Producing Reserves over a 24-month period.

**Company Background:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC operates in the oil and gas exploration and production sector, with assets including Proved Developed Producing Reserves of natural gas and related hydrocarbon properties secured under comprehensive collateral arrangements.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established relationship with Morgan Stanley Senior Funding as Administrative Agent, providing institutional-grade oversight and energy sector expertise
• Diversified lender syndicate managed by experienced credit investment firm GoldenTree Asset Management, indicating sophisticated institutional backing
• Comprehensive collateral package with ratification of all security interests and liens securing borrower obligations
• Current compliance status with no defaults or events of default existing under the credit agreement
• Structured hedging requirements provide downside protection against commodity price volatility

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Energy sector exposure with inherent commodity price volatility risk, mitigated by mandatory 70% natural gas production hedging over 24-month periods
• Second lien structure indicates subordinated credit position with potentially higher loss severity in distress scenarios
• Hedging covenant flexibility allows compliance based on commercially reasonable market rates, potentially resulting in lower hedging coverage during adverse market conditions
• Concentration risk in natural gas production dependent on reserve base performance and operational execution
