# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 6, 2022, Oil-Dri Corporation of America executed a consent agreement with BMO Harris Bank N.A. to modify their existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006.
- Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the Company/Borrower, with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Mounds Management Inc., Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.
- The transaction is a covenant modification allowing Oil-Dri to add back a one-time non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to their Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment to both Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations.
- The purpose is to provide covenant relief for a significant goodwill impairment charge taken during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022, ensuring continued compliance with existing financial maintenance covenants.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Long-standing banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating back to 2006, indicating stability and continuity
- Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement
- Multiple subsidiary guarantors providing additional credit support and security
- One-time nature of the goodwill impairment suggests this is not indicative of ongoing operational issues

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Significant goodwill impairment of $5.644 million suggests potential overvaluation or underperformance in the Retail and Wholesale segment
- Covenant modification indicates the borrower may have been at risk of covenant breach without this relief
- Limited visibility into overall financial performance, leverage ratios, or other key credit metrics beyond the specific covenant calculations
