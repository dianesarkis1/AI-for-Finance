## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation, operates as a regulated utility company serving Texas and New Mexico markets.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a commitment increase under an existing credit facility, adding CoBank, ACB as a new participating lender with a $25,000,000 commitment.
