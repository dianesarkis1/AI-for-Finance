# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In September 2024, Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II executed a commitment increase request for its senior secured revolving credit facility with Truist Bank as Administrative Agent.
- Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II serves as the Borrower, with Truist Bank acting as Administrative Agent and BNP Paribas as a participating lender.
- The original Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement was dated February 28, 2023, and has been previously amended twice (July 31, 2023 and May 30, 2024).
- The purpose of this request is to increase the aggregate revolving commitments from $455,000,000 to $480,000,000, specifically by increasing BNP Paribas' Multicurrency Commitment from $25,000,000 to $50,000,000.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong sponsor affiliation with Goldman Sachs, a globally recognized investment banking and financial services firm
- Established credit facility with multiple amendments indicating ongoing lender support and relationship management
- Commitment increase demonstrates lender confidence and borrower's access to additional liquidity
- Multi-bank syndicate structure with reputable institutions (Truist Bank, BNP Paribas) providing diversified funding sources

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited financial information available to assess underlying credit quality or covenant compliance
- Increased leverage exposure through higher commitment levels may elevate credit risk
- Information constraints limit visibility into pricing, covenants, and collateral structure
