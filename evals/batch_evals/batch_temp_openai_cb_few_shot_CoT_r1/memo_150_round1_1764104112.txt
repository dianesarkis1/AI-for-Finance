# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-10

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Sight Sciences, Inc., a Delaware corporation, operating in the medical device sector.

**Transaction Description:** Third Amendment to Amended and Restated Credit and Security Agreement (Revolving Loan) between Sight Sciences, Inc. as Borrower and MidCap Funding IV Trust as both Agent and Lender. This amendment modifies the existing revolving credit facility originally established on November 23, 2020, which was previously amended in October 2021 and November 2021.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment restructures lockbox account management protocols to enhance cash collection controls and establish more defined operational requirements for the revolving credit facility.

**Company Background:** Sight Sciences maintains an established banking relationship with comprehensive asset-based lending arrangements, indicating an operating company with accounts receivable and inventory collateral supporting its credit facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
