# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-10-29

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MarineMax, Inc., a Florida corporation serving as Borrower Representative, along with numerous subsidiary guarantors including boat dealerships, marinas, yacht services companies, and related entities across multiple jurisdictions.

**Transaction Description:** Fourth Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated August 8, 2022. The credit facility is administered by Manufacturers and Traders Trust Company (M&T Bank) as Administrative Agent, with Wells Fargo Commercial Distribution Finance serving as Floor Plan Agent. The lender syndicate includes major financial institutions: Bank of America, PNC Bank, BMO Bank, The Huntington National Bank, BankUnited, Raymond James Bank, and several regional lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies Section 2.01(a)(iii) of the existing credit agreement to establish specific sublimits within the floor plan facility: $158,333,333 each for boats larger than 80 feet, foreign OEM units (excluding Azimut and Galeon brands), and used floor plan units, plus a $450,000,000 limit for individual units valued over $1,000,000.

**Company Background:** MarineMax operates as a boat and yacht dealer with an extensive network of subsidiary operations spanning dealerships, marinas, yacht services, and related marine businesses across multiple jurisdictions including the United States and international locations.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong lender consortium featuring established financial institutions with marine lending expertise, indicating continued confidence in MarineMax's credit profile
• Comprehensive guarantee structure with numerous subsidiary entities providing credit enhancement across MarineMax's diversified operations
• The amendment represents an operational enhancement rather than distressed restructuring, suggesting ongoing business optimization
• Reaffirmation of all existing collateral documents and security interests maintains strong creditor protections

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• The amendment provides more borrower-friendly sublimit structures for floor plan financing, potentially increasing credit exposure in specific boat categories
• Marine retail industry exposure to economic cycles and discretionary consumer spending patterns
• Limited visibility into overall facility size, pricing, and key financial maintenance covenants constrains comprehensive credit assessment
• Complex multi-jurisdictional subsidiary structure may present operational and legal complexity
