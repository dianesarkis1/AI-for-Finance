# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 12, 2021, Emerson Electric Co. executed a Suspension of Rights Agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent under their existing Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018.
- Emerson Electric Co. serves as the Company/Borrower, with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. acting as Administrative Agent for a syndicated credit facility that includes multiple subsidiary borrowers and lenders.
- This agreement temporarily suspends Emerson's rights to borrow in Non-USD currencies (British Pounds Sterling, Euros, Japanese Yen) and to select 1-week or 2-month USD LIBOR interest periods, effective in anticipation of the 2021 LIBOR cessation scheduled for December 31, 2021.
- The purpose is to manage the transition away from LIBOR benchmarks while delaying the costs of formally amending the underlying Credit Agreement, with the suspension remaining in effect until alternative benchmark amendments are implemented.
- Emerson Electric Co. is a multinational manufacturing and technology company operating across various industrial sectors.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Proactive approach to LIBOR transition demonstrates strong operational risk management and coordination with lenders
- Relationship with JPMorgan Chase as Administrative Agent suggests access to sophisticated banking services and expertise
- Voluntary agreement to temporarily limit borrowing flexibility indicates cooperative relationship with lender group
- Structured approach allows for orderly transition while maintaining credit facility availability in USD

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Reduced borrowing flexibility through suspension of multi-currency options may limit natural hedging capabilities for international operations
- Temporary restriction to longer-term interest periods (minimum 1-month) may impact interest rate risk management
- Indemnification obligations expose Emerson to potential costs related to breaches or operational issues during transition period
- Limited information available on underlying credit facility terms, financial covenants, or overall credit profile
