# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2022-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Vertical Horizons, Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company, serves as the borrower in this aircraft financing arrangement. The transaction involves Frontier Airlines entities as guarantors, indicating this financing supports Frontier Airlines' fleet expansion program.

**Transaction Description:** This is Amendment No. 1 to a Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated December 28, 2021. The lending syndicate includes Citibank, N.A. (as Facility Agent and Arranger), Barclays Bank PLC, Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. Bank of Utah serves as Security Trustee.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment relates to PDP (Pre-Delivery Payment) financing for nine (9) Airbus A320NEO aircraft and fifty (50) Airbus A321NEO aircraft. The primary purpose is to extend certain compliance deadlines from July 1, 2022 to April 1, 2023, and update related assignment and step-in agreements with Airbus.

**Company Background:** The financing structure involves Frontier Airlines entities (Frontier Group Holdings, Inc., Frontier Airlines Holdings, Inc., and Frontier Airlines, Inc.) as guarantors, supporting Vertical Horizons' aircraft acquisition program for new-generation Airbus narrow-body aircraft.
