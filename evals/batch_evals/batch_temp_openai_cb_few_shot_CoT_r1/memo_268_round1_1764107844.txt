# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-07-07

**Borrower / Company Overview:** ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower. The parent entity is ExchangeRight Income Fund, d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT, a Maryland statutory trust operating as a real estate investment trust.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction constitutes a Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement executed among the borrower, Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as Administrative Agent, and Renasant Bank as the Additional Lender. The structure involves Renasant Bank joining an existing lending syndicate by providing an incremental revolving commitment.

**Purpose of Financing:** The transaction increases the aggregate revolving commitments under the existing credit facility from $135,000,000 to $150,000,000 through Renasant Bank's $15,000,000 incremental commitment, providing additional liquidity capacity to the borrower.

**Company Background:** The borrower operates as part of a REIT structure focused on commercial real estate investments, specifically managing multiple net leased portfolio properties through various subsidiary entities including Delaware statutory trusts. The original credit agreement was established on May 30, 2024, with prior amendments executed in January and April 2025.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks
