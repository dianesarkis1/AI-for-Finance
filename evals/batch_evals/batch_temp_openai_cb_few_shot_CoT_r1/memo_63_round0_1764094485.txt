# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On April 25, 2024, Accuray Incorporated, a Delaware corporation, entered into a third amendment to its existing credit agreement with Silicon Valley Bank (as Administrative Agent) and other lenders including HSBC Bank US.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated May 6, 2021, and had been previously amended in October 2022 and November 2023.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the definition of "Consolidated EBITDA" to provide additional covenant relief related to headcount reductions that occurred after September 30, 2023 through the amendment date.
- Accuray operates in the medical technology sector, providing radiation therapy solutions. The company appears to have undergone significant workforce restructuring requiring covenant modifications to maintain compliance.
