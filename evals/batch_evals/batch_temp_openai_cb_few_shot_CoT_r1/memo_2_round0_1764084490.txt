# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On 2020-11-30, Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver to MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. serves as the Borrower under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019, with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor.
- This waiver extends previous COVID-19 related relief through March 31, 2021, addressing casino closure impacts and financial covenant breaches.
- The transaction provides covenant relief due to government-mandated casino closures related to the COVID-19 pandemic, with OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) providing financial support to the borrower.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure by regulatory authorities, requiring ongoing financial accommodation from lenders.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Government support through OLG providing financial relief and covering fixed component payments during closure period
- Borrower maintains minimum liquidity requirement of $15,000,000
- Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
- Extension fee of 5 basis points demonstrates lender compensation for additional risk

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Extended waiver period through March 31, 2021 indicates prolonged financial stress
- No new advances available under either credit facility during waiver period
- Applicable margin increased to Level 5 (highest pricing tier at 5.00% for BA/LC/LIBOR loans)
- Restrictions on distributions, acquisitions, and material contract modifications
- Event of Default triggered if OLG ceases support payments for more than 5 business days
