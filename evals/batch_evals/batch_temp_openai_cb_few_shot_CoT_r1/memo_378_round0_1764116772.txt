# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On August 1, 2025, Millrose Properties, Inc., a Maryland corporation, entered into a First Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated June 24, 2025, establishing a credit facility with Goldman Sachs Bank USA serving as both Administrative Agent and lender, alongside Bank of America N.A., Citibank N.A., Citizens Bank N.A., JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., and Wells Fargo Bank N.A.
- The amendment introduces several key modifications: (1) adds a new "Designated Issuance" definition allowing the borrower to designate certain debt or equity issuances, (2) establishes a Stage 4 Funding Fee of 0.250% due 270 days after the Initial Draw Date, and (3) modifies mandatory prepayment provisions to allow retention of proceeds up to $500,000,000 in outstanding loans for designated issuances.
- The purpose appears to provide the borrower with greater flexibility for future capital raising activities while establishing additional fee income for lenders through the staged funding fee structure.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- High-quality lender syndicate including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, Wells Fargo, Citibank, and Citizens Bank, indicating strong institutional confidence
- Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default as of the amendment effective date
- Collateral and security interests remain in full force and effect, with borrower reaffirming all liens
- Amendment includes standard conditions precedent requiring borrower compliance and representations to remain true and correct

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Amendment terms are generally borrower-friendly, reducing mandatory prepayment requirements for designated issuances and allowing retention of proceeds
- Limited visibility into overall credit profile, financial performance, or business fundamentals of Millrose Properties
- The delayed draw term loan structure and staged funding fees suggest this may be a construction or development financing with inherent execution risks
