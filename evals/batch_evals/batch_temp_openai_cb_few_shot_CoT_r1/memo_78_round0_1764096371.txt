# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 31, 2023, Saia Inc. and its lenders entered into Amendment No. 1 to their Credit Agreement, originally dated February 3, 2023.
- Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower, with multiple subsidiary Loan Parties including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.
- The lending syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and Lender, along with Bank of America, N.A., PNC Bank National Association, TD Bank, N.A., and BOKF, NA dba Bank of Oklahoma as Lenders.
- The amendment specifically modifies Section 6.09 (Restrictive Agreements) to allow for more flexibility in unsecured debt agreements, permitting restrictions that are not materially more restrictive than the current Credit Agreement and that allow for pari passu security arrangements.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• Strong banking syndicate comprising major U.S. financial institutions (JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, PNC, TD Bank, BOKF), indicating continued lender confidence in Saia Inc.
• The Borrower represents that no Default or Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, suggesting current compliance with all credit terms.
• Amendment provides operational flexibility for future unsecured financing while maintaining lender protection through pari passu security provisions.
• All existing Loan Documents remain in full force and effect, preserving the integrity of the credit structure.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• The amendment loosens certain restrictive covenant provisions, potentially allowing for additional leverage through unsecured debt channels.
• Limited visibility into overall credit metrics, pricing, or financial performance based solely on this amendment document.
• Mitigation: The amendment includes safeguards requiring that new restrictions not be materially more restrictive than current terms and must permit equal security arrangements.
