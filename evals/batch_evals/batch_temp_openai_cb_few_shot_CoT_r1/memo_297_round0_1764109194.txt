# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 9, 2023, Blackstone Private Credit Fund executed Amendment No. 1 and Extension Agreement to its existing credit facility with Citibank N.A. as Administrative Agent.
- Blackstone Private Credit Fund, a Delaware statutory trust, serves as the Borrower under this senior secured credit facility that was originally established on May 6, 2022.
- The amendment extends the maturity date from May 6, 2027 to June 9, 2028 for consenting lenders, creating a dual-maturity structure where some lenders maintain the original 2027 maturity while others extend to 2028.
- The facility includes both revolving dollar commitments, revolving multicurrency commitments, and term loans, with the extension applying to both revolving and term facilities for participating lenders.
- This transaction represents a maturity extension for Blackstone's private credit investment vehicle, providing additional liquidity runway and refinancing flexibility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Blue-chip sponsor with Blackstone, one of the world's largest alternative asset managers with strong track record in private credit
- Extensive syndicate of premier financial institutions including Citibank (Administrative Agent), JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Deutsche Bank, BNP Paribas, and other major global banks
- Successful maturity extension demonstrates continued lender support and confidence in the credit
- Borrower represents compliance with all covenants and no defaults or events of default as of the amendment date
- Dual-maturity structure provides flexibility with some lenders extending while maintaining existing relationships

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Private credit fund structure creates exposure to underlying portfolio company performance and market conditions
- Dual-maturity structure (2027 vs 2028) could create refinancing pressure if non-extending lenders don't roll at the 2027 maturity
- Limited visibility into specific financial metrics, portfolio composition, or borrowing base requirements
- Interest rate and pricing terms not disclosed, limiting assessment of credit spread and cost structure
