# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On July 7, 2025, ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP executed a Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as Administrative Agent and Renasant Bank as the Additional Lender.
- The Borrower is ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, with ExchangeRight Income Fund d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT serving as the Parent. The entity operates as a real estate investment trust formed under Maryland law.
- This transaction represents an incremental increase to an existing revolving credit facility originally established on May 30, 2024. Renasant Bank is joining as an Additional Lender, providing a $15,000,000 incremental revolving commitment.
- The purpose is to increase the total revolving commitments from $135,000,000 to $150,000,000, with the lending syndicate now consisting of Wells Fargo Bank ($75,000,000), Fifth Third Bank ($60,000,000), and Renasant Bank ($15,000,000).
- The borrowing entity operates multiple subsidiary entities and Delaware statutory trusts focused on net leased portfolio properties, indicating a commercial real estate investment strategy.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks
