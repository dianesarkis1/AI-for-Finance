# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2021-06-29

**Borrower:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector with operations focused on hydrocarbon production from Proved Developed Producing Reserves.

**Transaction Structure:** Amendment No. 2 to Second Lien Credit Agreement originally dated December 30, 2020. The transaction involves Vine Energy Holdings LLC as Borrower, Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, and a syndicate of institutional lenders managed by GoldenTree Asset Management, LP.

**Purpose:** The amendment modifies minimum hedging requirements under Section 6.17 of the Credit Agreement, establishing new parameters for commodity hedging of natural gas production.

**Company Background:** Vine Energy Holdings operates through multiple subsidiaries including Vine Oil & Gas GP LLC, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, and others, all serving as guarantors under the facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established institutional framework with Morgan Stanley Senior Funding providing administrative oversight
• Diversified lender base managed by experienced credit specialist GoldenTree Asset Management
• Comprehensive security package with full ratification of collateral documents and liens
• Clean credit profile with borrower representations confirming no existing defaults
• Mandatory hedging provides downside protection against commodity price volatility

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Energy sector commodity price exposure, mitigated by 70% hedging requirement over 24-month periods
• Second lien credit structure indicates subordinated recovery position
• Hedging compliance flexibility may result in lower coverage during unfavorable market conditions
• Operational risks inherent in oil and gas production activities
