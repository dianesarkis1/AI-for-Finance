# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-06-16

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Nasdaq, Inc., a Delaware corporation, is a leading financial technology company providing trading, clearing, exchange technology, regulatory, and market data services globally.

**Transaction Description:** This Amendment No. 2 modifies Nasdaq's existing Amended and Restated Credit Agreement (originally dated December 16, 2022) among Nasdaq, Inc. as borrower, Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of institutional lenders including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Citibank N.A., JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Morgan Stanley Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank National Association, and other major financial institutions.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates Nasdaq's acquisition of Adenza Holdings, Inc. pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 10, 2023, by modifying the leverage ratio covenant structure to accommodate the acquisition financing requirements.

**Company Background:** Nasdaq operates as a premier financial market infrastructure provider, serving as both an exchange operator and technology solutions provider to financial institutions worldwide.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• High-quality borrower with established market position in financial technology sector
• Strong syndicate of premier financial institutions providing committed support
• Structured step-down leverage covenant provides clear deleveraging pathway over 2.5-year period
• Borrower maintains full compliance with all existing covenants and representations
• Amendment includes disciplined provisions for future acquisition capacity with appropriate leverage constraints

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Temporary increase in maximum leverage ratio to 4.75x immediately post-acquisition represents elevated near-term credit risk, mitigated by mandatory quarterly step-downs
• Acquisition integration risks inherent in the Adenza transaction, though specific business rationale and synergies not disclosed in amendment
• One-time "Subsequent Acquisition Holiday" provision allows additional leverage up to 4.50x for future acquisitions, mitigated by strict pro forma compliance requirements and reversion to 3.50x baseline
