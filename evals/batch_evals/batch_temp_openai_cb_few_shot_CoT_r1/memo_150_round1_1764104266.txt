# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-10

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Sight Sciences, Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into a Third Amendment to its Amended and Restated Credit and Security Agreement (Revolving Loan) with MidCap Funding IV Trust as Agent and Lender.

**Transaction Description:** This amendment modifies an existing revolving credit facility originally established on November 23, 2020, and previously amended in October 2021 and November 2021. The transaction involves MidCap Funding IV Trust serving as both Agent and Lender under the credit agreement.

**Purpose of Financing:** The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify terms related to the Lockbox Account operations, specifically introducing a "Lockbox Activation Date" framework and updating deposit account control agreement requirements to enhance cash collection procedures.

**Company Background and Context:** Sight Sciences operates in the medical device industry and maintains a revolving credit facility with asset-based lending characteristics, evidenced by the emphasis on accounts receivable collection through structured lockbox arrangements and collateral management requirements.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**

• Comprehensive collateral package with security interests and liens maintained in full force and effect

• Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default exist under the financing documents

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**

• Strict requirement that 80% of account collections be properly identified and reconciled within 10 business days, with advance suspension mechanism if compliance fails
• Limited financial performance information available to assess overall credit quality and operational metrics
