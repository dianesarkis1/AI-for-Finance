# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-12-19

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Box, Inc., a Delaware corporation, operates as a cloud content management and collaboration platform provider.

**Transaction Description:** This amendment represents a bilateral credit facility modification between Box, Inc. (Borrower) and Wells Fargo Bank, National Association (sole Lender) to the existing Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally executed on June 30, 2023.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment adjusts the credit facility commitment amount to $75,000,000 and modifies liquidity covenant requirements to align with the borrower's existing convertible note obligations.

**Company Background:** Box, Inc. maintains an established banking relationship with Wells Fargo through this revolving credit facility, demonstrating continued access to institutional credit markets.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
