# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On May 10, 2022, Sight Sciences, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) entered into a Third Amendment to its Amended and Restated Credit and Security Agreement (Revolving Loan) with MidCap Funding IV Trust as Agent and Lender.
- The original credit agreement was dated November 23, 2020, and had been previously amended in October 2021 and November 2021.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify terms related to the Lockbox Account, specifically introducing a "Lockbox Activation Date" that is five business days prior to the initial borrowing of revolving loans, and updating deposit account control agreement requirements.
- Sight Sciences operates in the medical device industry, and this appears to be a revolving credit facility with asset-based lending characteristics, given the focus on accounts receivable collection through lockbox arrangements.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established credit relationship with multiple amendments showing ongoing lender engagement and flexibility
- Comprehensive collateral package with security interests and liens maintained in full force and effect
- Strong collection mechanisms through required lockbox arrangements for accounts receivable
- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default exist
- Amendment includes detailed account reconciliation requirements providing transparency to cash flows

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Amendment introduces more restrictive cash management controls, potentially indicating lender concerns about collection efficiency
- Requirement that 80% of account collections be properly identified and reconciled within 10 business days, with advance suspension if not met
- Limited financial information available to assess overall credit quality and performance metrics
- Asset-based lending structure suggests potential reliance on working capital collateral rather than cash flow
