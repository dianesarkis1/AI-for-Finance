# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 27, 2023, Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP (formerly America First Multifamily Investors, L.P.) and Bankers Trust Company executed a Third Amendment to their Amended and Restated Credit Agreement.
- Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP, a Delaware limited partnership focused on multifamily housing investments, serves as the Borrower, with Bankers Trust Company as the sole lender.
- The original credit agreement was established on August 23, 2021, and has been previously amended twice (April 29, 2022 and July 29, 2022).
- This amendment primarily updates administrative details related to the Pledged Account and Securities Intermediary arrangements. Specifically, it updates the account number for the Pledged Account (to #164283-000) and designates Wilmington Trust, National Association as the Securities Intermediary maintaining the account.
- The transaction requires corresponding amendments to the Security Agreement and an updated Control Agreement with the Securities Intermediary.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established banking relationship with Bankers Trust Company spanning multiple years with documented amendment history showing ongoing engagement
- Borrower represents that no Event of Default or Unmatured Event of Default has occurred, indicating current compliance with credit terms
- Strong collateral framework with pledged accounts and security agreements maintained through Wilmington Trust as Securities Intermediary
- All existing loan documents, collateral agreements, and covenants remain ratified and in full force and effect

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited visibility into overall credit facility size, pricing, or financial performance metrics
- Administrative nature of amendments may indicate operational complexity in collateral management
- Single lender relationship with Bankers Trust Company concentrates counterparty exposure
