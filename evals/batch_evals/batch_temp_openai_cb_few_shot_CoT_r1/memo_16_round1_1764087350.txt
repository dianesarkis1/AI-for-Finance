# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2024-10-29

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MarineMax, Inc., a Florida corporation serving as Borrower Representative, along with numerous subsidiary loan parties including boat dealerships, marinas, yacht services companies, and related entities across multiple jurisdictions.

**Transaction Description:** Fourth Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated August 8, 2022. The transaction involves MarineMax and its subsidiaries as borrowers/guarantors, with Manufacturers and Traders Trust Company as Administrative Agent and Wells Fargo Commercial Distribution Finance as Floor Plan Agent. The syndicated lender group includes Bank of America, PNC Bank, BMO Bank, The Huntington National Bank, BankUnited, Raymond James Bank, and other financial institutions.

**Purpose:** The amendment modifies floor plan financing sublimits within the existing credit facility, establishing specific borrowing capacity thresholds for different categories of marine vessels and equipment.

**Company Background:** MarineMax operates as a marine retailer with an extensive network of subsidiaries engaged in boat and yacht sales, marina operations, yacht services, and related marine activities across domestic and international markets.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong syndicated lender group with established marine industry expertise demonstrates continued institutional support
• Comprehensive subsidiary guarantee structure provides broad credit enhancement across MarineMax's diversified operations
• Amendment represents operational refinement rather than distressed restructuring, indicating proactive capital management
• Full reaffirmation of existing collateral documents and security interests maintains robust creditor protections

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Increased sublimit flexibility may expand credit exposure in premium vessel categories (units >$1M individual value)
• Marine retail sector sensitivity to economic cycles and discretionary consumer spending volatility
• Amendment document provides limited visibility into overall facility terms, pricing structure, and financial maintenance requirements
• Multi-jurisdictional subsidiary structure across domestic and international entities may present operational complexity
