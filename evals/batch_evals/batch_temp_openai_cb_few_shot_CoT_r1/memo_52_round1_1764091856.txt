# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-10-26

**Borrower/Company Overview:** PNM Resources, Inc. is a New Mexico corporation that serves as borrower under a syndicated credit facility with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This Eighth Amendment to Credit Agreement modifies the existing credit facility originally established through a Sixth Amendment to and Restatement of Credit Agreement dated July 30, 2018 (as previously amended by a Seventh Amendment dated December 19, 2018). The amendment involves Wells Fargo as Administrative Agent, MUFG Bank, Ltd. as sole bookrunner and lead arranger, and a syndicate of major financial institutions including MUFG Union Bank, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley Bank, Royal Bank of Canada, KeyBank, Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Bank of New York Mellon, and BOKF d/b/a Bank of Albuquerque.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment addresses potential covenant defaults arising from PNM Resources entering into a merger agreement with Avangrid, Inc. and NM Green Holdings, Inc. dated October 20, 2020. The primary purpose is to modify the "Change of Control" definition, update merger-related restrictions, and provide limited waivers for potential defaults related to entering the merger agreement.

**Company Background:** PNM Resources operates as a utility holding company. The company has entered into a strategic merger agreement but requires credit facility modifications to manage potential covenant violations during the transaction process.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong, diversified lending syndicate comprising eleven major financial institutions demonstrates broad market confidence
• Borrower maintains full compliance with credit agreement terms after giving effect to this amendment
• Supportive lender group willing to provide targeted waivers and accommodate strategic transaction needs
• Amendment preserves comprehensive lender protections for the actual merger closing

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Pending merger transaction will ultimately trigger prohibited "Change of Control" event requiring future resolution
• Limited scope of waivers covers only entering the merger agreement, not closing the transaction
• Potential cross-default exposure from other debt arrangements related to the merger agreement
• Amendment provides no visibility into overall credit metrics, financial performance, or covenant compliance levels
