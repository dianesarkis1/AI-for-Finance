## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• **Limited scope amendments** - Extensions are narrow in scope (5-6 days) and specific to administrative deadlines rather than fundamental credit terms
• **Proactive borrower management** - Team, Inc. sought amendments in advance of deadlines, demonstrating responsible covenant management
• **Full lender consent** - All participating lenders agreed to modifications, indicating continued confidence and support from the lending syndicate  
• **Preservation of lender rights** - Amendment explicitly maintains all existing loan document terms, covenants, and remedies
• **Recent facility establishment** - Original Credit Agreement dated November 9, 2021, suggests recent successful credit approval and due diligence process

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• **Early execution challenges** - Need for amendments within three weeks of facility origination raises concerns about borrower's operational execution capabilities and internal coordination
• **PIK interest structure** - Payment-in-kind arrangements may indicate cash flow constraints or preservation strategies, though this could be by design rather than necessity
• **Multi-layered debt complexity** - References to ABL facilities suggest complex capital structure that may create coordination challenges and potential conflicts
• **Warrant obligations** - Equity issuance requirements indicate dilutive instruments that may affect borrower's capital structure and future financing flexibility
• **Limited financial visibility** - Amendment provides minimal insight into underlying financial performance, collateral coverage, or maintenance covenant compliance
