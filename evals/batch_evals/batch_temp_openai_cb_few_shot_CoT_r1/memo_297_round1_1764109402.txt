# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2023-06-09

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Blackstone Private Credit Fund, a Delaware statutory trust, operates as a private credit investment vehicle under the Blackstone alternative asset management platform.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 and Extension Agreement to the Amended and Restated Senior Secured Credit Agreement originally dated May 6, 2022. The transaction involves Blackstone Private Credit Fund as Borrower, with Citibank N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of 29 major financial institutions as Lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment extends the maturity date from May 6, 2027 to June 9, 2028 for consenting lenders while making certain other modifications to the existing credit agreement. This creates a dual-maturity structure accommodating both extending and non-extending lenders.

**Company Background:** Blackstone Private Credit Fund represents Blackstone's private credit investment platform, utilizing both revolving credit facilities (dollar and multicurrency) and term loans to support its investment activities in the private credit markets.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Blue-chip sponsor with Blackstone, one of the world's largest and most established alternative asset managers
• Extensive syndicate of 29 premier global financial institutions demonstrating broad market support
• Successful voluntary maturity extension indicates strong lender confidence and relationship quality
• Borrower confirmed compliance with all financial covenants and absence of defaults as of amendment date
• Flexible dual-maturity structure provides refinancing optionality and maintains existing lender relationships

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Private credit fund structure creates inherent exposure to underlying portfolio performance and credit market volatility
• Dual-maturity framework (2027/2028) may create refinancing pressure at the 2027 maturity date for non-extending facilities
• Limited disclosure of financial metrics, portfolio composition, and borrowing base mechanics restricts comprehensive credit assessment
• Absence of pricing and interest rate terms prevents evaluation of credit spread adequacy and funding cost structure
