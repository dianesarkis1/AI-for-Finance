# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower under a credit facility with BMO Harris Bank N.A., with multiple subsidiary guarantors providing additional support.

**Transaction Description:** This is a covenant modification consent to an existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006. The transaction involves Oil-Dri Corporation of America as borrower and BMO Harris Bank N.A. as lender, with seven subsidiary guarantors including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Mounds Management Inc., Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.

**Purpose of Financing:** The modification provides covenant relief by allowing Oil-Dri to add back a one-time non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to their Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment to both Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations, ensuring continued compliance with financial maintenance covenants.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates with multiple subsidiaries and maintains a Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment that experienced goodwill impairment during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Long-standing banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating back to 2006, demonstrating stability and continuity
• Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement
• Multiple subsidiary guarantors providing additional credit support and security enhancement
• One-time nature of the goodwill impairment suggests this is not indicative of ongoing operational deterioration
• Proactive covenant management demonstrates prudent financial stewardship

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Significant goodwill impairment of $5.644 million indicates potential asset overvaluation or underperformance in the Retail and Wholesale segment
• Covenant modification requirement suggests the borrower may have been at risk of covenant breach without this relief
• Limited visibility into overall financial performance, leverage ratios, or other key credit metrics beyond the specific covenant calculations provided
• Concentration risk in Retail and Wholesale segment given the magnitude of impairment relative to the modification request
