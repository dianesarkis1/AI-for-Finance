# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-05-27

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Karman Holding Inc., a Delaware corporation, operates through various subsidiaries including Karman Space & Defense LLC, Aerospace Engineering LLC, Wolcott Design Services LLC, Amro Fabricating Corporation, American Automated Engineering Inc., and Systima Technologies Inc., indicating involvement in aerospace and defense sectors.

**Transaction Description:** This First Amendment to Credit Agreement provides for an incremental term loan facility of $75,000,000 with Citibank N.A. serving as Administrative Agent, Collateral Agent, and sole First Amendment Incremental Term Lender. The amendment modifies the original Credit Agreement dated April 1, 2025.

**Purpose of Financing:** The proceeds will be used to repay outstanding Revolving Credit Loans, for working capital and other general corporate purposes (including Permitted Investments), and to pay associated fees and expenses.

**Company Background and Context:** This represents a relatively quick follow-on financing within approximately two months of the initial facility, with the incremental term loans having the same terms as existing Initial Term Loans and Term Loans, creating a fungible tranche of debt.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong lender relationship with Citibank N.A. serving as both Administrative Agent and sole incremental term lender, indicating confidence in the credit
• Multiple subsidiary guarantors providing additional credit support and diversified business operations across aerospace and defense sectors
• SOFR-based pricing structure aligning with current market standards
• Quarterly amortization schedule of 0.25% provides modest debt reduction over time
• No events of default under key sections as a condition precedent
• Terms match existing facility, creating operational simplicity

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Single lender concentration risk with Citibank N.A. providing the entire $75 million incremental facility
• Recent vintage of original credit facility followed quickly by incremental financing may indicate higher capital needs than originally anticipated; mitigated by use of proceeds for refinancing existing revolving debt
• Limited visibility into specific financial maintenance covenants; mitigated by requirement that no events of default exist under key sections
