# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On August 28, 2020, Lilis Energy Inc., a Nevada corporation, executed a Third Amendment to its Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-In-Possession Credit Agreement with BMO Harris Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent and other lenders including Capital One, National Association and Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch.
- The original DIP Credit Agreement was dated June 30, 2020, indicating Lilis Energy is operating under Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings.
- The purpose of this Third Amendment is to extend certain Chapter 11 milestones, specifically amending Section 8.23(e) to extend a deadline from August 28, 2020 to September 3, 2020.
- Lilis Energy appears to be an energy company with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Brushy Resources Inc., Hurricane Resources LLC, Impetro Operating LLC, Lilis Operating Company LLC, and Impetro Resources LLC.
- This represents a debtor-in-possession financing arrangement providing liquidity during the company's Chapter 11 reorganization process.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Super-priority DIP structure provides enhanced creditor protection and recovery prospects in bankruptcy proceedings
- Strong banking consortium including BMO Harris Bank N.A., Capital One, and Credit Suisse provides institutional lender confidence
- Multiple subsidiary guarantors provide additional credit support and potential recovery sources
- Borrower representations confirm continued compliance with loan covenants and no defaults

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Company is operating under Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, indicating significant financial distress
- Need for Chapter 11 milestone extensions suggests potential delays in reorganization timeline
- Limited financial information available to assess underlying business performance and recovery prospects
- DIP financing inherently carries execution risk related to successful emergence from bankruptcy
