# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-08-18

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Halozyme Therapeutics, Inc. is a Delaware corporation operating in the biotechnology sector, with subsidiaries Halozyme, Inc. and Antares Pharma, Inc. serving as Guarantors under the credit facility.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to the Credit Agreement increases the existing revolving credit facility through Incremental Revolving Commitments of $225,000,000. The transaction involves multiple Increase Loan Lenders joining the syndicate, with Bank of America, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and Swing Line Lender. The amendment modifies the original Credit Agreement dated May 24, 2022.

**Purpose of Financing:** The increased facility provides additional liquidity for general corporate purposes, with all loan proceeds to be used in accordance with Section 6.11 of the Amended Credit Agreement. The amendment also facilitates the full repayment of outstanding Term A Loans.

**Company Background:** Halozyme operates as a biotechnology company with a subsidiary structure including Halozyme, Inc. and Antares Pharma, Inc., both providing guarantees under the credit facility and maintaining collateral support through existing Collateral Documents.
