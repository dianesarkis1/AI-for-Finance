# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower under a credit facility with BMO Harris Bank N.A., supported by seven subsidiary guarantors including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Mounds Management Inc., Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.

**Transaction Description:** This is a consent amendment to modify an existing credit agreement originally executed on January 27, 2006. The transaction involves Oil-Dri Corporation of America as borrower and BMO Harris Bank N.A. as lender.

**Purpose of Financing:** The modification provides covenant relief by allowing the company to add back a one-time non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 to its Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations, ensuring continued compliance with financial maintenance covenants.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates with multiple business segments, including a Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment that experienced goodwill impairment during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022. The company maintains a long-standing credit relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating back to 2006.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established 16-year banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank demonstrates relationship stability and lender confidence
• No existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement as confirmed in borrower representations
• Strong guarantee structure with seven subsidiary guarantors providing enhanced credit support
• One-time nature of goodwill impairment suggests isolated rather than systemic operational issues
• Proactive covenant management demonstrates responsible financial stewardship

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Material goodwill impairment of $5.644 million indicates potential asset overvaluation or segment underperformance in Retail and Wholesale operations
• Need for covenant modification suggests borrower was approaching or potentially breaching financial maintenance requirements
• Limited financial disclosure restricts comprehensive credit assessment of overall leverage and liquidity position
• Segment-specific impairment may signal competitive pressures or market challenges in retail/wholesale operations
