# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-08-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Lilis Energy Inc., a Nevada corporation, serving as the borrower under a Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement. The company is supported by five subsidiary guarantors: Brushy Resources Inc., Hurricane Resources LLC, Impetro Operating LLC, Lilis Operating Company LLC, and Impetro Resources LLC.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents the Third Amendment to a Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement originally executed on June 30, 2020. The amendment involves Lilis Energy Inc. as borrower, BMO Harris Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of lenders including Capital One National Association and Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment extends certain Chapter 11 milestone deadlines to provide additional time for the borrower's bankruptcy restructuring process. Specifically, the amendment grants an extension of certain Chapter 11 milestones as requested by the borrower.

**Company Background and Context:** Lilis Energy Inc. is an energy sector company currently operating under Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection. The company is utilizing debtor-in-possession financing to maintain operations during its restructuring process, with the original DIP facility established approximately two months prior to this amendment.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Super-priority DIP structure provides enhanced creditor protections and recovery prospects compared to traditional unsecured claims
• Diverse institutional lender syndicate including BMO Harris Bank N.A., Capital One National Association, and Credit Suisse AG demonstrates institutional confidence
• Borrower maintains compliance with all representations, warranties, and covenants as certified on the effective date
• All existing loan documents and security interests remain in full force and effect post-amendment

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Chapter 11 bankruptcy context presents elevated credit risk and potential for significant principal impairment
• Request for milestone extensions indicates potential delays in restructuring timeline, increasing execution risk
• Limited financial information available in amendment restricts comprehensive credit assessment capability
• Energy sector exposure during restructuring period presents additional commodity and operational risks
