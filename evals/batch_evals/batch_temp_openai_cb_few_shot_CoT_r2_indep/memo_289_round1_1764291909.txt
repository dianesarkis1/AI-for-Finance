# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-09-15

**Borrower/Company Overview:** F45 Training Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation engaged in the fitness training industry.

**Transaction Description:** This is a consent agreement under an existing Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated August 13, 2021. The transaction involves F45 Training Holdings Inc. as borrower, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent and Australian Security Trustee, and a lending syndicate including Goldman Sachs Bank USA.

**Purpose of Financing:** The consent provides an extension for the delivery of required financial reporting obligations under the existing credit facility, specifically extending the deadline for audited consolidated financial statements for fiscal year 2022 and accompanying Compliance Certificate.

**Company Background:** F45 Training Holdings Inc. has an established credit relationship dating to 2021, with the original credit agreement having been amended four times (December 2021, May 2022, February 2023, and June 2023), indicating an active and evolving credit relationship with its banking partners.
