# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2022-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Vertical Horizons, Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company, serves as the financing vehicle for Frontier Airlines' aircraft acquisition program.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement for PDP (Pre-Delivery Payment) financing of 59 Airbus aircraft (9 A320neo and 50 A321neo). The transaction involves Citibank N.A. as Facility Agent and Arranger, with a syndicate of lenders including Barclays Bank PLC, Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding.

**Purpose of Financing:** PDP financing to support pre-delivery payments for Frontier Airlines' fleet expansion with modern Airbus narrow-body aircraft.

**Company Background:** The financing structure involves Vertical Horizons as borrower with full guarantees from Frontier Group Holdings, Inc., Frontier Airlines Holdings, Inc., and Frontier Airlines, Inc., providing multiple layers of credit support for this aviation industry financing.
