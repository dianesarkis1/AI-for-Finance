# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-09-02

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation, is a leading multinational tobacco and consumer products company.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to a 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement involves PMI as borrower, with Citibank Europe PLC (UK Branch) serving as Facility Agent, and a syndicate of 16 international lenders including Citibank N.A. Jersey Branch, Citicorp North America Inc., Bank of America N.A. London Branch, BBVA New York Branch, Banco Santander S.A., Barclays Bank PLC, Credit Suisse entities, Deutsche Bank AG Cayman Islands Branch, HSBC entities, Mizuho Bank Ltd., Standard Chartered Bank, Sumitomo Banking Corporation, Wells Fargo Bank N.A. London Branch, Bank of China (Europe) S.A., Commerzbank AG New York Branch, and MUFG Bank Ltd.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies the "Minimum Acceptance Condition" definition related to an acquisition offer, establishing a 50% threshold for Target shareholder acceptances. This suggests the bridge facility supports PMI's acquisition activities.

**Company Background:** PMI operates as a major player in the global tobacco and consumer goods sector, with sufficient scale and creditworthiness to attract a prestigious international banking syndicate for acquisition financing.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• **Investment-grade borrower profile:** PMI represents a globally recognized multinational corporation with established market presence
• **Premier banking syndicate:** 16-bank consortium includes top-tier international institutions across multiple jurisdictions, indicating strong lender confidence
• **Short-term bridge structure:** 364-day facility term suggests interim acquisition financing with planned permanent refinancing
• **Constructive lender relationship:** Unanimous lender agreement to amendment demonstrates cooperative syndicate management

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• **Regulatory headwinds:** Tobacco industry faces ongoing regulatory challenges globally, though mitigated by PMI's product diversification efforts
• **Acquisition execution risk:** Bridge financing carries inherent transaction completion risks, though mitigated by experienced borrower and lender group
• **Limited financial transparency:** Amendment document provides minimal visibility into facility economics and borrower financial metrics
