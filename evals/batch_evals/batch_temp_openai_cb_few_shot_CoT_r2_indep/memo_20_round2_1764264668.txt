## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-03-04

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP (formerly America First Multifamily Investors, L.P.) is a Delaware limited partnership focused on multifamily housing investments.

**Transaction Description:** This Fifth Amendment to Credit Agreement represents the latest modification to a revolving credit facility originally established on June 11, 2021. The transaction involves BankUnited N.A. serving as Administrative Agent for a three-lender syndicate that includes Bankers Trust Company and NexBank. This amendment formally adds NexBank as a lender through a joinder agreement and rebalances the commitment allocations among the lending group.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment serves to expand the lender syndicate by incorporating NexBank and restructuring the proportionate commitment shares under the existing credit facility, while maintaining the total facility size.

**Company Background:** The borrower is a Delaware limited partnership operating in the multifamily housing investment sector. The credit relationship demonstrates an active amendment history, with four prior modifications since origination: November 2021, June 2023, July 2023, and September 2023, indicating ongoing engagement between borrower and lenders.
