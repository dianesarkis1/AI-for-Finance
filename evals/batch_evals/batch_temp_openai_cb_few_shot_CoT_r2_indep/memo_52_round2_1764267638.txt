# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-10-26

**Borrower/Company Overview:** PNM Resources, Inc., a New Mexico corporation operating as a utility holding company.

**Transaction Description:** Eighth Amendment to Credit Agreement among PNM Resources, Inc., Wells Fargo Bank, National Association (as Administrative Agent), and a syndicate of lenders including MUFG Union Bank, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley Bank, Royal Bank of Canada, KeyBank, Bank of America, U.S. Bank, Bank of New York Mellon, and BOKF/Bank of Albuquerque. MUFG Bank, Ltd. serves as sole bookrunner and lead arranger for this amendment.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates PNM Resources' pending merger with Avangrid, Inc. through NM Green Holdings, Inc. by modifying covenant terms and providing specific waivers related to the merger agreement execution.

**Company Background:** PNM Resources operates regulated electric and gas utilities serving customers in New Mexico and Texas, providing stable utility services through its regulated subsidiary operations.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong, diversified lender syndicate comprising top-tier financial institutions demonstrates significant lender confidence
• Regulated utility business model provides stable, predictable cash flows and regulatory cost recovery mechanisms
• Borrower maintains compliance with all existing covenants and financial representations
• Proactive covenant management during M&A process shows sophisticated financial management
• MUFG Bank's continued lead arranger role indicates strong ongoing banking relationships

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Pending merger creates change of control event that will trigger facility termination upon closing, despite current waivers
• Amendment provides borrower-friendly modifications including higher change of control threshold (25% vs. prior undisclosed level)
• Limited covenant relief is temporary and specific to merger agreement signing only
• Refinancing risk exists upon merger consummation as current facility will be prohibited under modified terms
• Amendment explicitly preserves lender rights regarding future defaults from merger closing
