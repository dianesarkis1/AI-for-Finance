## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2025-07-25

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Universal Electronics Inc. is a Delaware corporation with an existing credit facility with U.S. Bank National Association as administrative agent and lender.

**Transaction Description:** This Tenth Amendment modifies Universal Electronics Inc.'s Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated October 27, 2017. The transaction involves Universal Electronics Inc. as borrower and U.S. Bank National Association as lender and administrative agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment permits Universal Electronics Inc. to grant liens to Bank of China securing indebtedness up to $21,000,000, provided such liens do not extend to any U.S.-based property.

**Company Background and Context:** Universal Electronics Inc. operates under a credit facility that has been amended nine times previously since 2017. The company maintains security arrangements including intellectual property assets and has Universal Electronics BV as a guarantor.
