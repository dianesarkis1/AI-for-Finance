## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-01-17

**Borrower/Company Overview:** HPS Corporate Lending Fund serves as the borrower under a Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This is a commitment increase agreement executed pursuant to Section 2.08(e) of the existing credit facility. Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch joins as an "Increasing Lender" to provide additional lending capacity of $25 million under the revolving credit structure. The transaction involves HPS Corporate Lending Fund (Borrower), Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch (Increasing Lender), and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. (Administrative Agent).

**Purpose of Financing:** To increase the aggregate commitment amount under the existing Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement, expanding HPS Corporate Lending Fund's available borrowing capacity by $25 million.

**Company Background:** HPS Corporate Lending Fund operates under a credit facility originally established June 23, 2022, and amended October 30, 2023. The fund maintains established banking relationships with premier financial institutions.
