## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-07-07

**Borrower / Company Overview:** ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the Borrower, with ExchangeRight Income Fund (d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT), a Maryland REIT, as the Parent company.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement adds Renasant Bank as an Additional Lender to an existing revolving credit facility. Wells Fargo Bank, National Association serves as Administrative Agent, with existing lenders including Fifth Third Bank, National Association.

**Purpose of Financing:** To increase the aggregate revolving credit commitments by $15,000,000 through the addition of Renasant Bank, bringing total facility capacity to $150,000,000.

**Company Background:** ExchangeRight operates as a real estate investment trust focused on essential income-generating properties. The corporate structure includes multiple subsidiary entities and Delaware statutory trusts that serve as guarantors, indicating a diversified real estate portfolio. The original credit facility was established on May 30, 2024, with this representing the third incremental increase to the facility.
