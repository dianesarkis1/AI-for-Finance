# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** America First Multifamily Investors, L.P. is a limited partnership operating in the multifamily real estate investment sector. The entity is structured with America First Capital Associates Limited Partnership Two as its general partner, which in turn is managed by Greystone AF Manager LLC as general partner.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a First Amendment to an existing Credit Agreement originally executed on June 11, 2021. The amendment involves America First Multifamily Investors, L.P. as Borrower, with BankUnited, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and lead lender alongside Bankers Trust Company as participating lender.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment primarily serves to restructure the guarantor arrangements by formally designating Greystone Select Incorporated as the guarantor under new documentation. Additionally, it updates certain administrative provisions regarding lender consent requirements for releases under the credit facility.

**Company Background:** America First Multifamily Investors, L.P. operates as part of a structured entity arrangement with institutional backing through Greystone-affiliated management companies, suggesting focus on multifamily real estate investments with professional asset management oversight.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

• Borrower represents full compliance with existing covenants and confirms no Events of Default exist as of the amendment date
• Professional management structure through Greystone-affiliated entities suggests institutional-quality oversight and operational expertise

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**

• Reliance on guarantor support concentrates credit risk on Greystone Select Incorporated's financial strength and performance
