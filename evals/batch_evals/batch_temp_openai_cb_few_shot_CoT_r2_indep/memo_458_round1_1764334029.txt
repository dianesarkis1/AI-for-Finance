# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-02-10

**Borrower / Company Overview:** SESI, L.L.C., a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the borrower with SESI Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, as the parent company. The credit facility includes multiple subsidiary guarantors operating in the energy services sector.

**Transaction Description:** This is a third amendment to an existing credit agreement originally dated February 2, 2021. The transaction involves SESI, L.L.C. as borrower, with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. serving as administrative agent, and includes Bank of America, N.A. and Citibank, N.A. as lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies existing credit agreement terms to provide enhanced flexibility for asset dispositions and establishes automatic lien release mechanisms.

**Company Background:** The borrower operates in the energy services industry through multiple subsidiaries including Superior Energy Services, Wild Well Control, International Snubbing Services, CSI Technologies, and Warrior Energy Services Corporation, among others.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender group including JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, and Citibank demonstrates continued banking support
• Borrower representation of no existing defaults indicates current covenant compliance
• Amendment includes specific liquidity ($100 million) and availability ($25 million) maintenance requirements for enhanced dispositions
• Cash consideration requirement for borrowing base asset sales provides collateral protection

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Relaxed asset disposition covenants may reduce collateral coverage over time, though mitigated by substantial portion limitations and cash consideration requirements
• Multiple prior amendments since 2021 origination suggests potential covenant pressure, though current amendment appears proactive rather than distressed
• Energy services sector exposure presents commodity and cyclical risks inherent to the industry
