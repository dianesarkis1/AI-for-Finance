**Date of Agreement:** 2024-01-31

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Fathom Manufacturing, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company serving as Borrower, with Fathom Guarantor, LLC (Delaware LLC) as Holdings. The company is located at 1050 Walnut Ridge Drive, Hartland, WI 53029.

**Transaction Description:** Extension consent letter executed between Fathom Manufacturing, LLC and its lender syndicate, modifying specific covenant deadlines under the existing Credit Agreement dated December 23, 2021. The lending syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and Lender, CIBC Bank USA, BMO Bank N.A., and Stifel Bank & Trust as Lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The extension provides additional time for compliance with two critical obligations: (1) execution of a Binding Agreement involving the Ultimate Parent under Section 5.15, and (2) completion of a USD 50,000,000 term loan prepayment to Third Amendment Consenting Term Lenders under Section 2.11(h).

**Company Background:** Fathom Manufacturing operates under a credit facility established in December 2021, with Mark Frost serving as Chief Financial Officer. The company has undergone previous amendments to its credit agreement and maintains relationships with major institutional lenders.
