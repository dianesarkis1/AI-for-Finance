**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation, a Delaware corporation operating as a business development company focused on specialty lending activities, with subsidiary guarantors OCSL SRNE, LLC and FSFC Holdings, Inc.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents an incremental commitment agreement that increases an existing senior secured revolving credit facility.
