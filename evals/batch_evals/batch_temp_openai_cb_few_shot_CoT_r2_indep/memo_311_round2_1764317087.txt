# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-12-12

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower in this syndicated credit facility.

**Transaction Description:** First Amendment to existing First Lien Credit Agreement originally dated March 4, 2021, executed between Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc. (Borrower), multiple lenders, and Citibank, N.A. (Administrative Agent).

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment implements a comprehensive benchmark rate transition replacing LIBOR-based interest rates with SOFR-based rates for USD denominated loans and replacing LIBOR for Alternative Currencies with the applicable risk-free reference rate (RFR). This addresses the discontinuation of LIBOR publication by ICE Benchmark Administration Limited after December 31, 2021.

**Company Background and Context:** Petco operates as a health and wellness company with an established syndicated credit facility managed by Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, indicating institutional-grade credit profile.
