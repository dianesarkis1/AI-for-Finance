# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the primary borrower under a credit facility with multiple subsidiary loan parties including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Credit Agreement originally executed February 3, 2023. The amendment involves Saia Inc. and a banking syndicate with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent. The lender group includes Bank of America, N.A., PNC Bank, National Association, TD Bank, N.A., and BOKF, NA dba Bank of Oklahoma.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically modifies Section 6.09 (Restrictive Agreements) to provide Saia Inc. with enhanced flexibility for future unsecured debt arrangements while preserving lender protections through mandatory pari passu security provisions.

**Company Background:** The subsidiary structure indicates Saia Inc. operates in the transportation and logistics sector, with entities focused on motor freight services, sales operations, and logistics solutions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
