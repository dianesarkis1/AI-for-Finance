# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** The borrowing group consists of five entertainment library financing entities: LGTV Library Financing LLC, Artisan Library Financing LLC, Summit Library Financing LLC, LG Film Library Financing LLC, and LGIM Library UK Financing Limited. Each borrower is supported by a corresponding parent holding company.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents Amendment No. 2 to an Amended and Restated Credit, Security and Pledge Agreement. The original credit agreement was executed on November 5, 2024, with Amendment No. 1 following on December 9, 2024. The current amendment involves the borrowers, their parent companies, thirteen institutional lenders, and Fifth Third Bank as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment serves to modify existing credit agreement terms and implement a facility upsize ("Upsize"). The amendment reallocates outstanding principal amounts among the lender group as specified in Schedule 1.1 of Annex A.

**Company Background:** The borrowing entities operate as financing vehicles in the entertainment content library sector. The structure incorporates parent holding companies that provide additional credit support through guarantor arrangements. The companies appear focused on film and television content library monetization.
