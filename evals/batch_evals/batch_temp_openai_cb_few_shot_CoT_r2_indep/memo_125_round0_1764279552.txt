# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On August 28, 2020, Lilis Energy Inc. (a Nevada corporation) and its lenders executed a third amendment to their Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement.
- The original DIP credit agreement was dated June 30, 2020, with BMO Harris Bank N.A. serving as Administrative Agent, alongside lenders Capital One National Association and Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch.
- The borrower is supported by multiple subsidiary guarantors including Brushy Resources Inc., Hurricane Resources LLC, Impetro Operating LLC, Lilis Operating Company LLC, and Impetro Resources LLC.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to extend certain Chapter 11 milestones, specifically extending a deadline from August 28, 2020 to September 3, 2020 under Section 8.23(e) of the original credit agreement.
- Lilis Energy Inc. appears to be an energy company operating under Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection, utilizing debtor-in-possession financing to maintain operations during restructuring.
