# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-06-15

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Sigmatron International, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower in this credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as the sole Lender.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to an existing Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated July 18, 2022. This represents the second modification to the facility, following Amendment No. 1 executed April 28, 2023.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically modifies Section 5.14 regarding collateral requirements for material asset acquisitions and establishes procedures for potential corporate restructuring scenarios. Key changes include refined notification requirements to the Lender for material asset acquisitions and mandatory execution of New Holdco Pledge Documents within ten business days if corporate restructuring occurs under the related Term Loan Agreement.

**Company Background:** Sigmatron International operates under a multi-lender capital structure, maintaining this JPMorgan Chase credit facility alongside a separate Term Loan Agreement with other lenders. The presence of an Intercreditor Agreement indicates coordinated debt management across multiple financing sources.
