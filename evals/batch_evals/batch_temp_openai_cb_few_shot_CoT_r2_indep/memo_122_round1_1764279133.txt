# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-12-28

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation, a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower with subsidiary guarantors OCSL SRNE, LLC (Delaware limited liability company) and FSFC Holdings, Inc. (Delaware corporation).

**Transaction Description:** This incremental commitment agreement increases the borrowing capacity under the existing Amended and Restated Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement dated February 25, 2019. MUFG Union Bank, N.A. serves as the Increasing Lender, with ING Capital LLC acting as Administrative Agent and Issuing Bank. The syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and Bank of America, N.A. as Syndication Agents, with joint lead arrangers ING Capital LLC, JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., and Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated.

**Purpose of Financing:** To provide additional borrowing capacity of $25,000,000, increasing MUFG Union Bank's commitment from $50,000,000 to $75,000,000 under the existing revolving credit facility.

**Company Background:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation operates as a business development company focused on specialty lending activities.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong sponsor backing through Oaktree Capital Management, a well-established alternative investment manager with significant expertise in credit markets
• Diversified lender syndicate including major financial institutions (ING Capital, JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, MUFG Union Bank), demonstrating broad market confidence
• Transaction represents an upsizing of existing credit facilities rather than emergency refinancing, indicating healthy demand for additional capacity
• Comprehensive security package with guarantee and security agreements covering subsidiary guarantors and collateral

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**

• Incremental commitment terms mirror existing facility terms, maintaining consistent risk profile
