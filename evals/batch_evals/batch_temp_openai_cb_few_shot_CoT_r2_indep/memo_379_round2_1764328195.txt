# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-05-27

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Karman Holding Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Karman Parent LLC, Karman Space & Defense LLC, Aerospace Engineering LLC, Wolcott Design Services LLC, Amro Fabricating Corporation, American Automated Engineering Inc., and Systima Technologies Inc.

**Transaction Description:** This is a First Amendment to an existing Credit Agreement originally dated April 1, 2025, with CITIBANK, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent. The amendment establishes $75,000,000 in First Amendment Incremental Term Loans provided solely by Citibank.

**Purpose of Financing:** The proceeds will be used to repay outstanding Revolving Credit Loans, for working capital and general corporate purposes (including Permitted Investments), and to pay associated fees and expenses.

**Company Background:** Based on the subsidiary names, Karman Holding appears to be a holding company with operations in aerospace, defense, engineering services, and manufacturing sectors.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional support with CITIBANK, N.A. providing the full $75M incremental facility, demonstrating continued lender confidence
• Diversified business platform spanning aerospace, defense, engineering, and manufacturing through multiple operating subsidiaries
• Successful expansion of borrowing capacity within two months of original facility establishment
• Incremental loans form same tranche and class as existing Term Loans, are fungible for U.S. federal income tax purposes, and share the same CUSIP
• Quarterly amortization schedule provides modest deleveraging starting September 2025

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Incremental borrowing shortly after facility establishment may indicate higher than anticipated capital needs
• SOFR-based floating rate exposure creates interest rate risk in rising rate environment
• Single lender concentration risk with Citibank providing the full incremental facility
• Limited visibility into specific financial maintenance covenants beyond standard compliance requirements
