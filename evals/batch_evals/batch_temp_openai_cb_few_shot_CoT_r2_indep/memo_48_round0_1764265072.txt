## 1. Executive Summary

- In November 2022, Spectrum Brands, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) entered into a Fourth Amendment to their Amended and Restated Credit Agreement with Royal Bank of Canada as Administrative Agent and participating Revolving Lenders.

- The purpose of this amendment is to provide Spectrum Brands additional time and flexibility to complete the sale of its Hardware and Home Improvement segment to ASSA ABLOY AB for $4,300,000,000 in cash proceeds (subject to customary purchase price adjustments) under an Asset and Stock Purchase Agreement dated September 8, 2021.
