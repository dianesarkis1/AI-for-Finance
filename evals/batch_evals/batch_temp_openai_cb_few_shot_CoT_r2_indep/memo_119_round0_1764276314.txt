# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On April 14, 2023, Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. executed a Guarantor Joinder Agreement to join as additional guarantors under Apollo Management Holdings, L.P.'s existing credit agreement dated October 12, 2022.
- Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit facility with Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.
- The original credit agreement includes multiple Initial Guarantors comprising various Apollo Principal Holdings entities organized in the Cayman Islands and Anguilla, demonstrating Apollo's global asset management structure.
- The purpose of this joinder is to add Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. as new guarantors to induce lenders to maintain and/or make additional loans and issuing banks to maintain and/or issue additional letters of credit, strengthening the credit support structure for the facility.
- Apollo operates as a leading global alternative investment manager with a diversified platform spanning private equity, credit, and real assets.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks
