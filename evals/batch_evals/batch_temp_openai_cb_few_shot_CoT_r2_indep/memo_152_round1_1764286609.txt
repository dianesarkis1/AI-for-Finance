# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-02-20

**Borrower / Company Overview:** CrossAmerica Partners LP, a Delaware limited partnership, and Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serve as co-borrowers under this credit facility. CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel wholesale and retail convenience store business with multiple subsidiary entities.

**Transaction Description:** This is a first amendment to an existing amended and restated credit agreement with Citizens Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of thirteen lenders. The amendment modifies covenant definitions to facilitate an acquisition transaction.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment enables the acquisition of assets from Applegreen entities under the Applegreen Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 26, 2024, involving purchases from Applegreen Midwest LLC and Applegreen Florida, LLC by CAPL Retail LLC.

**Company Background:** CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel distribution and convenience store sector through a comprehensive network of subsidiary entities. The original credit agreement was established in April 2019 and previously amended and restated in March 2023, demonstrating an established banking relationship.
