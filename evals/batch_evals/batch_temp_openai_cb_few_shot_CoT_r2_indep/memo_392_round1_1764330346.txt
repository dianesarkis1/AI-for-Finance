# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-10-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Emerson Electric Co. serves as the primary borrower, with subsidiary borrowers also party to the underlying credit facility established under a Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018.

**Transaction Description:** Emerson Electric Co. entered into a Suspension of Rights Agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent. This agreement temporarily suspends certain borrowing rights under the existing syndicated credit facility in anticipation of the 2021 LIBOR cessation.

**Purpose of Financing:** The agreement provides an interim solution to manage LIBOR transition impacts while delaying costs associated with formal credit agreement amendments. It specifically eliminates borrowing in non-USD currencies (British Pounds Sterling, Euros, Japanese Yen) and restricts certain USD LIBOR interest period selections.

**Company Background:** Emerson Electric Co. is an established industrial technology company with access to a syndicated credit facility through major financial institutions, demonstrating strong institutional banking relationships.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong banking relationship with JPMorgan Chase Bank as Administrative Agent, indicating institutional confidence in Emerson Electric's credit profile
• Proactive approach to LIBOR transition management, demonstrating operational sophistication and regulatory compliance focus
• Temporary nature of restrictions with clear path to restoration of full borrowing rights upon proper amendment implementation
• Borrower remains in good standing with continued access to USD borrowing under modified terms

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Reduced operational flexibility through elimination of non-USD currency borrowing options and restricted interest period selections
• Potential increased borrowing costs if USD rates become less favorable than previously available non-USD alternatives
• Indemnification obligations expose borrower to potential costs related to LIBOR transition implementation and any breaches
• Limited visibility into broader credit facility terms, pricing, and financial covenants from this supplementary agreement
