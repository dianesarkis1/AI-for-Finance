# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-06-16

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Nasdaq, Inc. is a Delaware corporation operating as a leading provider of trading, clearing, exchange technology, listing, information and public company services.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to Amended and Restated Credit Agreement among Nasdaq, Inc. as borrower, Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of premier lenders including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Citibank, HSBC, Industrial and Commercial Bank of China, JPMorgan Chase, Mizuho Bank, Morgan Stanley Bank, Nordea Bank, TD Bank, and Wells Fargo.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates Nasdaq's acquisition of Adenza Holdings, Inc. pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 10, 2023, by modifying leverage covenant requirements to accommodate the "Specified Acquisition" transaction.

**Company Background:** Nasdaq operates as a globally recognized financial services and technology company with an established track record in strategic acquisitions and market leadership position.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Blue-chip borrower with Nasdaq being a globally recognized financial services and technology company
• Premier banking syndicate comprising top-tier financial institutions, indicating strong lender confidence and relationship strength
• Structured leverage covenant step-downs provide a clear deleveraging path from 4.75x to 3.50x over 10 fiscal quarters post-acquisition
• Inclusion of "Subsequent Acquisition Holiday" provision allows for one additional acquisition with temporary covenant relief, demonstrating lender support for growth strategy

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Increased leverage allowance from existing levels to accommodate acquisition, with peak leverage of 4.75x permitted initially
• Limited visibility into underlying credit agreement terms such as pricing, maturity, and other financial maintenance covenants
• Acquisition integration risk associated with Adenza Holdings transaction, though mitigated by Nasdaq's track record and structured covenant step-downs
