# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-02-20

**Borrower/Company Overview:** CrossAmerica Partners LP is a Delaware limited partnership operating in the fuel distribution and convenience store sector. Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as co-borrower alongside the Partnership.

**Transaction Description:** This first amendment to the amended and restated credit agreement involves CrossAmerica Partners LP and Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc. as borrowers, with Citizens Bank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent. The syndicate includes multiple lenders and L/C issuers.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates the acquisition of assets from Applegreen entities under the Applegreen Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 26, 2024, involving CAPL Retail LLC's purchase of assets from Applegreen Midwest LLC and Applegreen Florida, LLC.

**Company Background:** CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel wholesale and retail convenience store business through multiple subsidiary entities. The credit facility features a comprehensive guarantee structure with numerous subsidiary guarantors, building upon an established banking relationship dating to April 2019 with the original credit agreement.
