# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MSCC Funding I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit and security agreement. The entity is managed by Main Street Capital Corporation, a Maryland corporation acting as collateral manager.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a lender joinder whereby EverBank N.A. joins an existing syndicated revolving credit facility as an additional lender. The syndicate includes Truist Bank (administrative agent and swingline lender), Citibank N.A. (collateral agent, custodian, and document custodian), and Virtus Group LP (collateral administrator).

**Purpose of Financing:** The joinder expands the lending syndicate by adding EverBank N.A. with a $75,000,000 commitment to the existing revolving credit facility, thereby increasing total available funding capacity.

**Company Background:** Main Street Capital Corporation operates as the collateral manager and sole member of the borrowing entity. The structure requires lenders to maintain Qualified Purchaser status under the Investment Company Act and Qualified Institutional Buyer status under the Securities Act, indicating a sophisticated investment-grade financing arrangement.
