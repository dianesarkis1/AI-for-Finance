## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-29

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC is a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector. The company operates through a comprehensive structure of oil and gas entities including Vine Oil & Gas GP LLC, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, Vine Energy Operating LP, and multiple royalty holdings entities.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to Second Lien Credit Agreement among Vine Energy Holdings LLC as Borrower, Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, and institutional lenders including entities managed by GoldenTree Asset Management. This amendment modifies the original Second Lien Credit Agreement dated December 30, 2020.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically revises minimum hedging requirements under Section 6.17 of the original credit agreement and introduces new definitions for "Proved Developed Producing Reserves" to provide operational flexibility in commodity risk management.

**Company Background:** Vine Energy Holdings operates in hydrocarbon production with focus on natural gas from proved developed producing reserves. The company maintains operations through multiple subsidiaries including operating partnerships, mineral holding companies, finance corporations, and royalty entities across the oil and gas value chain.
