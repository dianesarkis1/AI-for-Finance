# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-01-31

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Fathom Manufacturing, LLC is a Delaware limited liability company serving as the Borrower under a credit facility, with Fathom Guarantor, LLC acting as Holdings. The company operates from Hartland, Wisconsin, with Mark T. Frost serving as Chief Financial Officer.

**Transaction Description:** This extension consent letter modifies specific covenant deadlines under the existing Credit Agreement dated December 23, 2021. The transaction involves a syndicated lending group led by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, with CIBC Bank USA, BMO Bank N.A., and Stifel Bank & Trust as participating Lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The extension provides the Borrower additional time to satisfy two critical covenant obligations: execution of a Binding Agreement involving the Ultimate Parent per Section 5.15, and completion of a USD 50,000,000 mandatory term loan prepayment to Third Amendment Consenting Term Lenders under Section 2.11(h).

**Company Background:** Fathom Manufacturing maintains an institutional credit facility established in December 2021 that has been subject to previous amendments. The company operates within a structured holding company framework and maintains established banking relationships with major financial institutions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Majority Third Amendment Consenting Term Lenders approved the extension, demonstrating continued lender support and cooperation
• High-quality syndicate composition with JPMorgan Chase as Administrative Agent provides institutional credibility
• Extension is narrowly scoped and time-limited, maintaining strict covenant discipline

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extension request indicates potential execution challenges in meeting original covenant deadlines
• Substantial USD 50,000,000 mandatory prepayment obligation remains due by March 31, 2024
