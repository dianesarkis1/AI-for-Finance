# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation, operating through multiple subsidiaries including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Credit Agreement between Saia Inc. as Borrower and a banking syndicate led by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, with participating lenders including Bank of America, N.A., PNC Bank, TD Bank, N.A., and BOKF, NA dba Bank of Oklahoma.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies covenant terms in the original Credit Agreement dated February 3, 2023, specifically revising Section 6.09 (Restrictive Agreements) to provide enhanced flexibility for the borrower regarding unsecured debt arrangements.

**Company Background:** Saia Inc. is a transportation and logistics company with multiple operating subsidiaries, all serving as Loan Parties under the credit facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender support from major U.S. banks demonstrates continued market confidence
• Borrower confirms no Default or Event of Default exists, indicating strong covenant compliance
• Comprehensive guarantee structure maintained across all subsidiary Loan Parties
• Amendment preserves lender protections through mandatory pari passu security arrangements

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Covenant modification provides borrower-friendly terms that may allow less restrictive future indebtedness
• Risk mitigated by requirement that future unsecured debt terms cannot be materially more restrictive than current Agreement
• Lender security interests protected through mandatory equal and ratable treatment provisions
