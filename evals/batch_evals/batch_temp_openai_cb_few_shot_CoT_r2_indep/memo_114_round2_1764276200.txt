# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MSCC Funding I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the borrower with Main Street Capital Corporation (a Maryland corporation) acting as collateral manager and sole member.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction involves EverBank N.A. joining as an additional lender to an existing revolving credit and security agreement originally dated November 22, 2022. The lending syndicate includes Truist Bank as administrative agent and swingline lender, Citibank N.A. as collateral agent, custodian and document custodian, and Virtus Group LP as collateral administrator.

**Purpose of Financing:** The financing expands the existing revolving credit facility by adding EverBank as a new lender with a $75,000,000 commitment, increasing the total lending capacity available to the borrower.

**Company Background:** Main Street Capital Corporation operates as a business development company and utilizes this funding structure through its subsidiary MSCC Funding I, LLC for investment activities that require qualified purchaser and qualified institutional buyer status under applicable securities regulations.

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
