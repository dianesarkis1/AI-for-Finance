# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower under a credit facility with BMO Harris Bank N.A., supported by multiple domestic subsidiaries as guarantors.

**Transaction Description:** This is a consent agreement to modify an existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006. The modification involves amendments to financial covenant definitions to accommodate a one-time goodwill impairment charge.

**Purpose of Financing:** The consent provides covenant relief by allowing Oil-Dri to add back a $5,644,000 non-cash goodwill impairment charge from its Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations, preventing potential covenant violations.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates through multiple subsidiaries including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company. The company has a Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment that experienced goodwill impairment in Q1 2022.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Long-standing banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating to 2006, demonstrating lender confidence
• Proactive covenant management approach, addressing potential violations before default
• No existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement
• Strong guarantee structure with multiple operating subsidiaries providing credit support
• One-time nature of the goodwill impairment charge limits ongoing impact

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• $5.644 million goodwill impairment indicates underperformance in Retail and Wholesale segment
• Need for covenant modification suggests tight financial covenant compliance
• Limited financial transparency beyond the specific covenant metrics disclosed
• Potential ongoing operational challenges in the impaired business segment
