**Date of Agreement:** 2024-03-04

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP (formerly America First Multifamily Investors, L.P.) is a Delaware limited partnership focused on multifamily housing investments.

**Transaction Description:** Fifth Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated June 11, 2021. The transaction involves BankUnited N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate including Bankers Trust Company and NexBank as lenders.
