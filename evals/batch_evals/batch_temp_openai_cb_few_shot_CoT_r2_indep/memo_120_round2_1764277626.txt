# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-09-02

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation and major multinational tobacco and consumer goods company.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to PMI's 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement dated May 11, 2022. The amendment is executed between PMI and an international banking syndicate with Citibank Europe PLC (UK Branch) serving as Facility Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** Bridge financing facility supporting PMI's acquisition activities, with this amendment specifically modifying the "Minimum Acceptance Condition" related to the target acquisition.

**Company Background:** PMI is a globally recognized multinational corporation with established market positions in tobacco and consumer goods sectors, actively diversifying its product portfolio through strategic acquisitions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Blue-chip borrower with investment-grade profile and global market presence
• Strong international banking syndicate comprising 16 premier financial institutions across multiple jurisdictions
• Short-term bridge structure (364-day term) indicating temporary acquisition financing with defined exit strategy
• Demonstrated lender cooperation through unanimous amendment execution

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Limited financial visibility as amendment does not disclose facility size, pricing, or financial covenants
• Tobacco industry regulatory pressures, partially mitigated by PMI's product diversification strategy
• Acquisition execution risk tied to achieving minimum acceptance threshold of 50% target shareholdings
