# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-12-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation, a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower with subsidiary guarantors OCSL SRNE, LLC and FSFC Holdings, Inc.

**Transaction Description:** This incremental commitment agreement increases an existing senior secured revolving credit facility. The transaction involves MUFG Union Bank, N.A. as the Increasing Lender, with ING Capital LLC serving as Administrative Agent and Issuing Bank. The syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and Bank of America, N.A. as Syndication Agents, with joint lead arrangers ING Capital LLC, JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., and Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated.

**Purpose of Financing:** To provide additional borrowing capacity through an incremental commitment, expanding the existing revolving credit facility to meet operational funding needs.

**Company Background:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation operates as a business development company focused on specialty lending activities, backed by the Oaktree Capital Management platform.
