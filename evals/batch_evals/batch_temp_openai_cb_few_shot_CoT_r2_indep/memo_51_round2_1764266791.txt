# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation.

**Transaction Description:** Credit facility expansion adding CoBank, ACB as a new lender with a $25,000,000 commitment to an existing syndicated credit facility under a Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated March 11, 2022, with KeyBank National Association as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** Expand available credit capacity through addition of new syndicate member.

**Company Background and Context:** Electric utility corporation operating under an established credit facility. Concurrent execution of Sixteenth Supplemental Indenture issuing Replacement First Mortgage Bonds as facility security.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Regulated utility with stable cash flow profile
• Established banking relationships with institutional lenders
• Secured structure via First Mortgage Bonds
• Current covenant compliance confirmed
• Diversified lender syndicate

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Limited current financial disclosure in supplement document
• Regulatory environment exposure typical of utility sector
• Capital-intensive industry requirements
