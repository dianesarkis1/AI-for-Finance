# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In September 2024, three new borrowers (Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC, Innovex International, Inc., and TIW Corporation) joined an existing credit facility through a joinder agreement dated 2024-09-06.
- The existing borrowers include Tercel Oilfield Products USA L.L.C., Pride Energy Services, LLC, and Top-Co Inc., all operating in the oilfield services sector.
- PNC Bank, National Association serves as the Administrative Agent, with U.S. Bank National Association participating as a lender in this syndicated credit facility.
- The joinder follows a corporate merger where Innovex merged with DQ Merger Sub, LLC (now Innovex Downhole Solutions, LLC), and Dril-Quip, Inc. changed its name to Innovex International, Inc.
- The original Second Amended and Restated Revolving Credit, Term Loan, Guaranty and Security Agreement was dated June 10, 2022, and has been amended multiple times through June 28, 2024.
- The purpose is to add the new borrowers as parties to the existing credit agreement following the completion of the Dril-Quip merger, expanding the borrowing base in the oilfield products and services industry.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established banking relationship with reputable institutions (PNC Bank as agent, U.S. Bank as participating lender)
- Cross-collateralization through joint and several liability among all borrowers strengthens credit support
- Comprehensive security package including pledges of equity interests, intellectual property, and deposit account controls
- Post-closing covenant structure ensures proper documentation and collateral perfection within specified timeframes

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Integration risk following merger activity, though mitigated by existing operational borrowers remaining in the facility
- Limited visibility into financial performance metrics, covenant compliance, or pricing terms due to joinder document format
- Concentration in oilfield services sector exposes credit to commodity price volatility and cyclical industry conditions
- Multiple prior amendments to the credit agreement may indicate previous covenant or operational challenges
