# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-11-17

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Spectrum Brands, Inc., a Delaware corporation serving as Lead Borrower under a revolving credit facility administered by Royal Bank of Canada.

**Transaction Description:** Fourth Amendment to Amended and Restated Credit Agreement modifying financial covenant terms in existing revolving credit facility.

**Purpose of Financing:** Provides operational flexibility to complete divestiture of Hardware and Home Improvement segment to ASSA ABLOY AB for $4.3 billion cash proceeds.

**Company Background:** Spectrum Brands entered Asset Purchase Agreement with ASSA ABLOY AB on September 8, 2021, requiring additional time for transaction completion.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• $4.3 billion asset sale proceeds provide substantial deleveraging opportunity
• Established relationship with Royal Bank of Canada as Administrative Agent
• Time-bound covenant relief with automatic reversion by September 29, 2023
• Amendment includes 0.20% fee compensation to lenders

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Leverage covenant relaxation to 7.00:1.00 indicates potential financial stress
• Transaction delays since September 2021 suggest execution complexity
• Limited facility detail constrains comprehensive risk assessment
• Binary reliance on HHI Sale completion mitigated by hard reversion timeline
