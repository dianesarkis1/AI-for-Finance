# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2021-10-12

**Borrower:** Emerson Electric Co. and its subsidiary borrowers under a syndicated credit facility with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction:** Suspension of Rights Agreement temporarily restricting certain borrowing capabilities under an existing Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018. The agreement suspends: (i) borrowing rights in non-USD currencies (GBP, EUR, JPY), and (ii) selection of 1-week and 2-month USD LIBOR interest periods.

**Purpose:** Interim solution to address 2021 LIBOR cessation scheduled for December 31, 2021, allowing delay of formal credit agreement amendments while maintaining operational continuity.

**Company Background:** Emerson Electric Co. maintains an established syndicated credit facility with institutional lenders, demonstrating access to capital markets through major financial institutions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional banking relationship with JPMorgan Chase as Administrative Agent
• Proactive LIBOR transition management demonstrating operational sophistication
• Temporary restrictions with defined restoration path upon formal amendment implementation
• Borrower maintains good standing with continued USD borrowing capability

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Reduced operational flexibility eliminates multi-currency borrowing options and interest period selection
• Potential cost implications if USD rates prove less favorable than restricted alternatives
• Indemnification exposure for LIBOR transition-related costs and potential compliance breaches
• Agreement provides interim solution pending comprehensive LIBOR replacement amendments
