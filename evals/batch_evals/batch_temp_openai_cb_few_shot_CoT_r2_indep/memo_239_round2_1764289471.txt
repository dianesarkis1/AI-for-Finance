# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-29

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Vine Energy Holdings LLC is a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector with an extensive subsidiary structure including Vine Oil & Gas GP LLC, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, Vine Oil & Gas Finance Corp., and other related entities focused on hydrocarbon exploration and production.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to Second Lien Credit Agreement between Vine Energy Holdings LLC as borrower and multiple institutional lenders including FS Credit Income Fund, GN3 SIP Limited, GoldenTree 2004 Trust, GoldenTree Credit Opportunities 2012-1 Financing Limited, GT NM L.P., GTAM 110 Designated Activity Company, Healthcare Employees' Pension Plan Manitoba, High Yield And Bank Loan Series Trust, Louisiana State Employees Retirement System, San Bernardino County Employees Retirement Association, Syncora Guarantee Inc., and Tolleson High Yield Credit LP. Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. serves as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies the original Second Lien Credit Agreement dated December 30, 2020, specifically to revise minimum hedging requirements under Section 6.17 and establish new definitions for oil and gas reserve classifications.

**Company Background:** Vine Energy operates through multiple subsidiaries engaged in oil and gas development, with operations supported by proved developed producing reserves requiring ongoing hedging of commodity price exposure.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender syndicate managed by reputable asset managers including GoldenTree Asset Management and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding
• Comprehensive collateral package with liens and security interests in oil and gas properties maintained and reaffirmed
• Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement
• Amendment provides operational flexibility with market-based exceptions to hedging requirements when commercially reasonable rates are unavailable

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Commodity price volatility inherent in oil and gas operations, mitigated by mandatory hedging covering 70% of projected hydrocarbon production over 24-month periods
• Second lien subordinated position increases recovery risk in potential distress scenarios
• Complex multi-entity structure with numerous subsidiary guarantors may complicate credit administration and enforcement
