# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On 2021-06-29, Vine Energy Holdings LLC executed Amendment No. 2 to its Second Lien Credit Agreement with Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, along with multiple institutional lenders managed by GoldenTree Asset Management.
- Vine Energy Holdings LLC is a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector, with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Vine Oil & Gas GP LLC, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, and other related entities focused on oil and gas operations.
- The amendment modifies the original Second Lien Credit Agreement dated December 30, 2020, primarily to adjust minimum hedging requirements and add definitions related to oil and gas reserves.
- The purpose is to modify Section 6.17 (Minimum Hedging) to require the Borrower to maintain commodity swap contracts covering 70% of reasonably anticipated projected hydrocarbon production from Proved Developed Producing Reserves over a 24-month period, with flexibility provisions for market conditions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong institutional lender base managed by reputable asset managers including Morgan Stanley Senior Funding and GoldenTree Asset Management, indicating sophisticated credit assessment and ongoing monitoring
- Comprehensive collateral package with liens and security interests in oil and gas properties maintained and reaffirmed through the amendment
- Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default, indicating current compliance with all credit terms
- Amendment includes flexibility provisions allowing reduced hedging requirements when commercially reasonable market rates are unavailable

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Commodity price exposure inherent in oil and gas operations, though partially mitigated by mandatory hedging requirements covering 70% of projected production
- Complex corporate structure with multiple subsidiary guarantors may complicate enforcement and administration
- Second lien position indicates subordinated credit ranking, increasing recovery risk in distress scenarios
