**Borrower/Company Overview:** America First Multifamily Investors, L.P. is a limited partnership with America First Capital Associates Limited Partnership Two as its general partner, which in turn is managed by Greystone AF Manager LLC.

**Transaction Description:** First Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated June 11, 2021, between America First Multifamily Investors, L.P. as Borrower and a lender group led by BankUnited, N.A. as Administrative Agent, with Bankers Trust Company as participating lender.
