The transaction involves Fathom Manufacturing, LLC as Borrower and a syndicate of institutional lenders led by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, with CIBC Bank USA, BMO Bank N.A., and Stifel Bank & Trust as participating Lenders.
