# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 2, 2022, Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation, executed Amendment No. 1 to its 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement originally dated May 11, 2022.
- The borrower is Philip Morris International Inc., a major multinational tobacco and consumer goods company.
- The amendment involves PMI and a substantial international banking syndicate including Citibank Europe PLC (UK Branch) as Facility Agent, along with multiple global lenders including Bank of America London Branch, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, Credit Suisse, Barclays, and others.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the "Minimum Acceptance Condition" definition, changing the threshold from an unspecified level to requiring that acceptances from Target shareholders exceed 50% of total issued and outstanding Target Shares (excluding treasury shares).
- This appears to be a bridge financing facility supporting PMI's acquisition activities, with the amendment facilitating more flexible deal execution parameters.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- **Blue-chip borrower**: Philip Morris International is a globally recognized, investment-grade multinational corporation with strong market positions
- **Robust banking syndicate**: The facility is supported by a diverse group of premier international financial institutions across multiple jurisdictions
- **Short-term bridge structure**: The 364-day term suggests this is temporary acquisition financing, likely to be replaced by permanent capital
- **Acquisition-supportive amendment**: The modification to acceptance conditions suggests active deal progression and lender cooperation

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- **Limited financial information**: This amendment document does not provide visibility into pricing, size, or financial covenants of the underlying facility
- **Regulatory environment**: Tobacco industry faces ongoing regulatory pressures globally, though PMI has been diversifying its product portfolio
- **Acquisition execution risk**: Bridge facilities carry inherent risks related to successful completion of the underlying transaction
