# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 10, 2021, Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG), a Delaware corporation, entered into a Second Amendment to Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement with Bank of America, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent.
- The original Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement was dated January 18, 2019, establishing the baseline credit facility structure.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the Applicable Margin contained in Annex I to the Credit Agreement, effectively adjusting the pricing grid for the credit facility.
- AMG is the borrower in this term credit agreement arrangement with multiple bank lenders participating in the facility.
- The amendment updates the pricing structure based on debt rating levels, with margins ranging from 0.725% to 1.225% for Eurodollar Loans depending on credit ratings from A+/A1/A+ down to BBB/Baa2/BBB levels.
