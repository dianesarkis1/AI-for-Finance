**Borrower/Company:** Team, Inc., a Delaware corporation, executed Amendment No. 1 to its Unsecured Term Loan Credit Agreement with multiple lenders including Corre Opportunities Qualified Master Fund, LP, Corre Horizon Fund, LP, and Corre Horizon Fund II, LP.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment provides administrative relief by extending: (i) the PIK Interest payment date for Initial Term Loans, (ii) the ABL Consent delivery deadline, and (iii) the Underlying Warrants issuance requirement.
