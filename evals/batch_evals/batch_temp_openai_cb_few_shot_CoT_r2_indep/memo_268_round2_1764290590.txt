# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-07-07

**Borrower/Company Overview:** ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower, with ExchangeRight Income Fund (d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT), a Maryland real estate investment trust, as the parent entity.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a third incremental increase to an existing revolving credit facility through the addition of Renasant Bank as an Additional Lender. Wells Fargo Bank, National Association serves as Administrative Agent, with the original credit agreement dated May 30, 2024.

**Purpose of Financing:** The financing adds $15,000,000 in incremental revolving commitments from Renasant Bank, expanding the borrower's available liquidity under the existing credit facility.

**Company Background:** ExchangeRight operates as a real estate investment trust focused on income-generating properties. The organization maintains a complex structure with multiple subsidiary entities including ExchangeRight Essential Income Strategy Properties 6, LLC and various Delaware statutory trusts (ExchangeRight Net Leased Portfolio 3-9 DST) that serve as guarantors under the facility.
