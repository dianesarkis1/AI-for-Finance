# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-09-04

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II, a specialized lending entity within the Goldman Sachs organization, operates under a senior secured revolving credit facility administered by Truist Bank.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction constitutes a commitment increase request under an existing syndicated senior secured revolving credit agreement. The facility structure includes multiple lenders and issuing banks, with Truist Bank serving as Administrative Agent and BNP Paribas as a significant multicurrency commitment provider.

**Purpose of Financing:** The Company seeks to increase aggregate revolving commitments from $455,000,000 to $480,000,000, providing an additional $25,000,000 in liquidity capacity. This increase will be effectuated through BNP Paribas doubling its multicurrency commitment from $25,000,000 to $50,000,000.

**Company Background:** Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II represents Goldman Sachs' middle market lending platform. The underlying credit agreement was originally executed on February 28, 2023, with subsequent amendments on July 31, 2023 (First Amendment) and May 30, 2024 (Second Amendment), demonstrating active facility optimization and strong lender relationships.
