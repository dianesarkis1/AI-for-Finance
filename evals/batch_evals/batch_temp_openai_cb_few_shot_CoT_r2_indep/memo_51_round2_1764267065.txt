# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation, operating under a Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated March 11, 2022.

**Transaction Description:** Credit facility expansion through an Increase Supplement adding CoBank, ACB as a new lender with a $25,000,000 commitment under the existing credit agreement administered by KeyBank National Association.

**Purpose of Financing:** Syndicate expansion to increase total credit facility capacity under the established framework.

**Company Background:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company is a utility corporation with mortgage bond financing structure. The company issued Replacement First Mortgage Bonds under a Sixteenth Supplemental Indenture dated May 13, 2022.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Utility sector provides regulated revenue streams and operational stability
• Established banking relationships with tier-one financial institutions
• Secured credit structure backed by First Mortgage Bonds
• Borrower confirms full compliance with existing credit agreement terms
• Administrative Agent KeyBank National Association provides experienced facility management

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Regulatory exposure typical of utility sector, mitigated by rate recovery mechanisms
• Capital-intensive industry requirements, offset by regulated return structure
• Limited detailed financial metrics available in this supplement document
