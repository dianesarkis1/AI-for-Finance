# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 30, 2020, Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver to MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. serves as the Borrower with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019).
- This transaction represents an extension of COVID-19 related waivers due to government-mandated casino closures. The waiver extends relief from financial covenant breaches and closure defaults through March 31, 2021 (the "Extended Waiver Period").
- The financing accommodation addresses liquidity pressures from casino facility closures mandated by Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under regulatory oversight, with OLG providing certain financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during the closure period.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• Government Support: OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) is providing financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during closure
• Liquidity Requirements: Borrower must maintain minimum liquidity of $15,000,000 at all times, providing downside protection
• Weekly Monitoring: Required weekly liquidity reporting ensures close oversight of financial condition
• Regulated Industry: Casino operations are highly regulated, providing some operational stability framework

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• COVID-19 Impact: Extended closure of casino facilities creates significant operational and financial stress
• Financial Covenant Breaches: Multiple breaches of financial covenants in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicate deteriorating financial performance
• Increased Pricing: Applicable margin moved to Level 5 (highest pricing tier at 5.00% for BA/LC/LIBOR loans)
• Restricted Operations: Prohibitions on new advances, distributions, acquisitions, and contract amendments limit financial flexibility
• Government Dependency: Critical dependence on continued OLG support payments creates concentration risk
