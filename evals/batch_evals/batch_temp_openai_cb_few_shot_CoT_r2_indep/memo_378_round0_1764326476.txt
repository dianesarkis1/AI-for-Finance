## 1. Executive Summary

- On August 1, 2025, Millrose Properties, Inc., a Maryland corporation, entered into a First Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of major financial institutions.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated June 24, 2025, indicating this is a relatively new credit facility that required early modification within approximately two months of origination.
- The amendment introduces several key changes: (1) adds a new "Designated Issuance" definition allowing the borrower to designate certain debt or equity issuances for preferential treatment, (2) establishes a Stage 4 Funding Fee of 0.250% of outstanding loan principal payable 270 days after the Initial Draw Date, and (3) modifies prepayment mechanics to allow the borrower to retain proceeds from designated issuances above $500,000,000 in outstanding loans.
