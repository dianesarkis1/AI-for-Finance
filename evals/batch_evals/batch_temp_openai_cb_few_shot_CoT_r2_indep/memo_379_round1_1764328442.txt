# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2025-05-27

**Borrower:** Karman Holding Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the primary borrower under this First Amendment to Credit Agreement with CITIBANK, N.A. as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent.

**Transaction Structure:** This amendment establishes $75,000,000 in First Amendment Incremental Term Loans, expanding the borrower's existing credit facility originally executed on April 1, 2025. The incremental facility is provided solely by CITIBANK, N.A. as both agent and lender.

**Financing Purpose:** Proceeds will be deployed to repay outstanding Revolving Credit Loans, fund working capital requirements, support general corporate purposes including Permitted Investments, and cover associated transaction fees and expenses.

**Company Background:** Karman Holding operates as a diversified holding company with subsidiaries spanning aerospace and defense (Karman Space & Defense LLC, Aerospace Engineering LLC), manufacturing (Amro Fabricating Corporation), engineering services (Wolcott Design Services LLC), and automation technology (American Automated Engineering Inc., Systima Technologies Inc.).

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong lender relationship with CITIBANK, N.A. providing additional $75M capacity within two months of original facility establishment
• Diversified revenue base across aerospace, defense, manufacturing, and engineering services sectors
• Comprehensive guarantee structure with multiple operating subsidiaries providing credit support
• Conservative amortization schedule requiring only 0.25% quarterly principal payments starting September 2025
• Robust documentation including solvency certificates, legal opinions, and standard compliance representations

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Single lender concentration risk with Citibank as sole provider of incremental facility
• SOFR-based floating rate structure exposes borrower to interest rate volatility beyond initial period ending June 30, 2025
• Rapid incremental borrowing request suggests potential working capital pressures or higher than anticipated financing needs
• Limited covenant disclosure beyond standard representations, warranties, and no-default requirements
