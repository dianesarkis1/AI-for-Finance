# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-04-25

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Accuray Incorporated, a Delaware corporation, operates as the borrower under a syndicated credit facility with Silicon Valley Bank (now a division of First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company) serving as Administrative Agent, Issuing Lender, and Swingline Lender, alongside other institutional lenders including HSBC Bank US.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Amendment to Credit Agreement modifies the existing credit facility originally established on May 6, 2021 (previously amended in October 2022 and November 2023). The amendment involves the borrower, lender syndicate, and administrative agent making specific modifications to covenant definitions.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment provides covenant relief by expanding the definition of "Consolidated EBITDA" to permit additional addbacks related to restructuring charges and projected cost savings from workforce reductions, facilitating improved financial covenant compliance.

**Company Background:** Accuray Incorporated maintains an established banking relationship with reputable financial institutions and has operated under this credit facility structure since 2021, with periodic modifications to accommodate changing business conditions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established relationships with tier-one financial institutions including First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company and HSBC Bank US
• Borrower maintains covenant compliance and satisfactory representations and warranties
• Amendment includes comprehensive release provisions indicating cooperative lender-borrower relationship
• Time-limited covenant modifications provide temporary relief rather than permanent structural changes
• Amendment fee payment demonstrates borrower's commitment to maintaining lender relationships

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• EBITDA addbacks suggest operational challenges requiring significant workforce reductions and restructuring activities
• Borrower-favorable covenant modifications may obscure underlying financial performance deterioration
• Third amendment since 2021 indicates ongoing need for covenant relief and potential financial stress
• Specific dollar limits on addbacks ($2.6M restructuring charges, up to $6.8M cost savings) suggest material impact on financial metrics
• Limited visibility into overall facility terms constrains comprehensive credit risk assessment
