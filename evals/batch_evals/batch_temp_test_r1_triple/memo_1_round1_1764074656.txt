# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-09-30

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. is a casino operator that operates casino facilities under agreements with OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation). Complex Services Inc. serves as guarantor under the credit agreement.

**Transaction Description:** This is a Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver granted by Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent on behalf of Required Lenders. The waiver extends previous COVID-19 related relief measures through an Extended Waiver Period until November 30, 2020.

**Purpose of Financing:** The transaction provides continued covenant relief during government-mandated casino closures due to COVID-19. The waiver addresses financial covenant breaches, operational defaults from facility closures exceeding 60 consecutive days, and defers compliance certificate delivery requirements.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment operates under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019). The company has been significantly impacted by mandatory casino closures authorized by OLG due to COVID-19 restrictions, which would normally constitute Events of Default under the credit agreement.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) provides financial relief including threshold payment relief and continued Fixed Component Per Diem payments
• Strong liquidity maintenance requirements of minimum $15,000,000 throughout waiver period
• Comprehensive monitoring with mandatory weekly liquidity reporting and forecasting
• No new credit facility advances permitted, limiting additional lender exposure
• Pricing adjusted upward to Level 5 to reflect elevated risk profile

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Casino operations subject to ongoing government closure mandates with uncertain reopening timeline
• Multiple financial covenant breaches under Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 requiring continued waivers
• Extended waiver period creates uncertainty regarding long-term covenant compliance
• Operational restrictions including prohibited distributions and acquisitions during waiver period
• Dependency on continued OLG financial support for operational viability
