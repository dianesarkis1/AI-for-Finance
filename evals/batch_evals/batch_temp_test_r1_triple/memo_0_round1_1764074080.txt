## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-10

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG) is a Delaware corporation operating as an investment management company.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction is a Second Amendment to AMG's Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement originally dated January 18, 2019. The amendment involves AMG, Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent, and the existing lender syndicate.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies the pricing structure by replacing the "Applicable Margin" contained in Annex I of the original credit agreement with updated margin rates tied to debt ratings.

**Company Background:** AMG operates in the investment management industry. The company maintains investment-grade credit ratings and has an established credit facility with Bank of America, N.A. serving as both Administrative Agent and Lender.
