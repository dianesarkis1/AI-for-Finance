## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-05-10

**Borrower/Company Overview:** LifeStance Health Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower with Lynnwood Intermediate Holdings, Inc. as the parent holding company. The company operates within the healthcare services sector.

**Transaction Description:** This is a Second Amendment to the original Credit Agreement dated May 4, 2022 (previously amended November 7, 2023). The transaction involves LifeStance Health Holdings, Inc. as Borrower and a lending syndicate led by Capital One, National Association serving as Administrative Agent, Revolver Agent, Collateral Agent, Issuing Bank, and Swing Line Lender, along with Unitranche Loan Transaction II, LLC as a key lender.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically extends the Delayed Draw Term Loan Commitment Expiration Date to August 14, 2024, providing the Borrower additional time to access its existing delayed draw term loan facility. This represents a technical modification to preserve borrowing capacity under the existing credit structure.

**Company Background:** LifeStance Health Holdings maintains an established credit facility originally structured in May 2022, with this representing the second amendment to the original agreement. The company operates through a holding company structure with Lynnwood Intermediate Holdings as the parent entity.
