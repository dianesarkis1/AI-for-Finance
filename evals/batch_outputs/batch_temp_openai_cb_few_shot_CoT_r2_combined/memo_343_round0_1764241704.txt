# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In September 2024, Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II executed a commitment increase request for its senior secured revolving credit facility with Truist Bank as Administrative Agent.
- Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II serves as the Borrower, with Truist Bank acting as Administrative Agent and BNP Paribas as a participating lender in the syndicate.
- The original Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement was dated February 28, 2023, and has been amended twice previously (July 31, 2023 and May 30, 2024).
- The purpose of this transaction is to increase the aggregate revolving commitments from $455,000,000 to $480,000,000, representing a $25,000,000 increase. This increase is being effectuated by expanding BNP Paribas's Multicurrency Commitment from $25,000,000 to $50,000,000.
