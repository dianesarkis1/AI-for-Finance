# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-06-16

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Nasdaq, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower in this credit facility amendment with Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to Nasdaq's Amended and Restated Credit Agreement (originally dated December 16, 2022, with Amendment No. 1 dated March 29, 2023) to facilitate the acquisition of Adenza Holdings, Inc. pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 10, 2023. The transaction involves Nasdaq acquiring Adenza through merger subsidiaries Argus Merger Sub 1, Inc. and Argus Merger Sub 2, LLC.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies leverage ratio covenants in Section 6.06 to accommodate the increased leverage from the Adenza acquisition, establishing a stepped-down covenant structure over time and providing for a future "Subsequent Acquisition Holiday" for one additional acquisition.

**Company Background:** Nasdaq operates as a leading financial technology and market infrastructure provider with an established market position in global capital markets.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong lender consortium including premier financial institutions (Bank of America, Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, Morgan Stanley Bank, Wells Fargo, and others), indicating broad market confidence
• Borrower representations confirm no defaults exist and all prior representations remain accurate
• Structured step-down in leverage covenant demonstrates commitment to deleveraging over time
• Nasdaq's established market position as a leading financial technology and market infrastructure provider

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Significant relaxation of leverage covenant from current levels to 4.75x initially, representing increased credit risk
• Amendment provides additional flexibility for future acquisitions through "Subsequent Acquisition Holiday" provision, potentially enabling further leverage increases to 4.50x with its own step-down schedule
• Limited visibility into deal size, pricing, or other financial maintenance covenants beyond leverage ratio
