# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 2, 2022, Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation, executed Amendment No. 1 to its 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement originally dated May 11, 2022.
- The borrower is Philip Morris International Inc., a major tobacco and consumer products company, with Citibank Europe PLC, UK Branch serving as Facility Agent alongside a substantial international banking syndicate.
- This amendment modifies the definition of "Minimum Acceptance Condition" related to an acquisition offer, lowering the acceptance threshold from what appears to be a higher percentage to "exceeds 50% of the total issued and outstanding Target Shares."
- The financing appears to support PMI's acquisition strategy, providing bridge financing contingent on achieving minimum shareholder acceptance levels in a takeover offer for an unspecified target company.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• **Strong Borrower Profile**: Philip Morris International is a well-established, globally recognized consumer products company with substantial operational scale and market presence.

• **High-Quality Banking Syndicate**: The facility includes premier international financial institutions such as Citibank, Bank of America, Barclays, Credit Suisse, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, and Wells Fargo, indicating strong lender confidence.

• **Short-Term Bridge Structure**: The 364-day maturity suggests this is acquisition bridge financing, typically replaced by permanent financing upon deal completion.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• **Acquisition-Related Risk**: The amendment's focus on minimum acceptance conditions indicates execution risk related to the success of PMI's acquisition strategy.

• **Limited Financial Information**: The document provides no visibility into pricing, financial covenants, or PMI's current financial performance metrics.

• **Amendment Favorable to Borrower**: Lowering the minimum acceptance threshold to 50% may indicate challenges in achieving the original acceptance levels for the target acquisition.
