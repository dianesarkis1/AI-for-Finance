# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-09-02

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation, is a major international tobacco and consumer products company with global operations and market presence.

**Transaction Description:** This Amendment No. 1 modifies PMI's existing 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement dated May 11, 2022. The amendment involves PMI as borrower, Citibank Europe PLC, UK Branch as Facility Agent, and a syndicate of 16 international lenders including major financial institutions across North America, Europe, and Asia.

**Purpose of Financing:** The bridge facility supports PMI's acquisition strategy, providing short-term financing for a takeover transaction. The amendment specifically modifies acceptance conditions to facilitate completion of an offer for target company shares.

**Company Background:** PMI operates as a leading international tobacco and consumer products company with established relationships with premier global financial institutions, as evidenced by the high-quality lender syndicate participation.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• **Established Investment-Grade Borrower**: PMI is a well-capitalized multinational corporation with diversified global operations and strong market positions
• **Premier Lender Syndicate**: Facility supported by 16 leading international banks including Citibank, Bank of America, Barclays, Credit Suisse, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, Mizuho, and Wells Fargo, demonstrating strong institutional confidence
• **Short-Term Bridge Structure**: 364-day maturity typical for acquisition financing, designed for replacement with permanent financing upon transaction completion
• **Unanimous Lender Support**: All existing lenders participated in the amendment, indicating continued credit confidence

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• **Acquisition Execution Risk**: Success depends on achieving minimum 50% shareholder acceptance in target acquisition, with potential for deal failure
• **Bridge Financing Refinancing Risk**: Facility requires replacement or repayment within 364-day term, though PMI's credit profile supports refinancing capability
• **Limited Covenant Visibility**: Amendment provides minimal insight into financial maintenance requirements or borrower restrictions beyond acceptance conditions
