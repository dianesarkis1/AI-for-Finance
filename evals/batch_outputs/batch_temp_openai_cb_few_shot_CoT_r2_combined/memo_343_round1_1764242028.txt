**Borrower/Company Overview:** Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II is the borrower under a senior secured revolving credit facility, operating as part of the Goldman Sachs organization focused on middle market lending activities.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a commitment increase request under an existing Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated February 28, 2023. The facility involves Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II as borrower, Truist Bank as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of lenders including BNP Paribas.

**Purpose of Financing:** The borrower is requesting an increase in the aggregate revolving commitments from $455,000,000 to $480,000,000, representing a $25,000,000 expansion of available credit capacity effective September 4, 2024.

The credit agreement has been previously amended twice (July 31, 2023 and May 30, 2024), indicating an active and evolving credit relationship.
