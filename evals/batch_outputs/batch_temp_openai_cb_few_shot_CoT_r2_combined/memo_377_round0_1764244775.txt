# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On March 31, 2025, multiple entertainment library financing entities and their parent companies entered into Amendment No. 2 to their Amended and Restated Credit, Security and Pledge Agreement with Fifth Third Bank as Administrative Agent.
- The borrowers include LGTV Library Financing LLC, Artisan Library Financing LLC, Summit Library Financing LLC, LG Film Library Financing LLC, and LGIM Library UK Financing Limited, along with their respective parent holding companies.
- This amendment modifies the original credit agreement dated November 5, 2024 (previously amended in December 2024) and includes an "Upsize" transaction that increases the outstanding loan amounts held by each lender.
- The financing appears to support entertainment content library assets, with multiple specialized financing vehicles structured to hold different content libraries.
- The lender group consists of 13 financial institutions led by Fifth Third Bank, with East West Bank serving as Reserve Deposit Agent.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
• Strong and diversified lender syndicate including major regional and national banks (Wells Fargo, Truist, City National Bank, Texas Capital Bank)
• Specialized entertainment library financing structure with segregated asset vehicles
• Recent facility upsize indicates lender confidence and borrower growth
• Multiple guarantors and cross-collateralized structure enhances credit support
• Reserve Deposit Agent structure provides additional security layer

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• Entertainment content libraries can be subject to valuation volatility based on market demand and content performance
• Complex multi-entity structure with international components (UK entity) may create operational complexity
• Limited visibility into specific financial covenants, pricing terms, and collateral details
• Relatively new credit facility (originated November 2024) with limited operating history
