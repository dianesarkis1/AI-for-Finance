# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-09-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor, operates casino facilities under agreement with Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG).

**Transaction Description:** Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver executed between Bank of Montreal (Administrative Agent) and MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., extending COVID-19 related relief measures through November 30, 2020. This waiver amends and restates previous limited waivers from the original credit agreement dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019).

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver provides temporary relief from casino closure defaults and financial covenant breaches caused by government-mandated COVID-19 shutdowns, while maintaining enhanced monitoring and operational restrictions.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were temporarily closed due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions. The company receives financial relief from OLG, including threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments under their Casino Operating Services Agreement (COSA).

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government support through OLG financial relief packages providing threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments
• Enhanced liquidity monitoring with mandatory minimum liquidity requirement of $15 million
• Borrower remains current on representations and warranties with no additional defaults beyond COVID-related issues
• Structured waiver period with clear expiration date and defined conditions
• Weekly liquidity reporting provides transparency into cash position

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Casino closure exceeding 60 consecutive days triggers automatic default, indicating operational vulnerability
• Financial covenant breaches in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 demonstrate deteriorating financial performance
• Prohibition on new credit facility advances during waiver period severely limits financial flexibility
• Applicable margin increased to Level 5 (highest pricing tier), reflecting elevated credit risk
• Default triggers if OLG ceases relief payments for more than five business days, creating dependency risk
