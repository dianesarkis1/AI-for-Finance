## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-07-25

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Universal Electronics Inc., a Delaware corporation, maintains a credit facility with U.S. Bank National Association acting as Administrative Agent, Lender, and LC Issuer.

**Transaction Description:** Tenth amendment to the Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated October 27, 2017. The facility has undergone nine previous amendments since inception, with U.S. Bank National Association serving in multiple capacities under the arrangement.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically modifies lien covenant provisions to accommodate new secured indebtedness with Bank of China, permitting liens securing up to $21,000,000 provided such liens are restricted to non-U.S. property.

**Company Background:** Universal Electronics Inc. operates under a mature credit relationship with comprehensive collateral arrangements including security agreements covering general assets, confirmatory grants on intellectual property (trademarks and patents), and subsidiary guaranty support from Universal Electronics BV established in 2021.
