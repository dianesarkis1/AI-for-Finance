# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On October 31, 2023, Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into Amendment No. 1 to its Credit Agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated February 3, 2023, among Saia Inc. as Borrower, other Loan Parties, various financial institutions as Lenders, and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.
- The amendment modifies Section 6.09 (Restrictive Agreements) of the Credit Agreement, specifically clause (vi), to allow for more flexible restrictions and conditions related to unsecured indebtedness.
- Saia Inc. operates with multiple subsidiaries as Loan Parties, including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.
- The lending syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., Bank of America, N.A., PNC Bank National Association, TD Bank, N.A., and BOKF, NA dba Bank of Oklahoma.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong banking consortium comprising major U.S. financial institutions (JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, PNC Bank, TD Bank, and BOKF), indicating lender confidence in Saia Inc.
- The borrower maintains compliance with all existing covenants and no Default or Event of Default has occurred.
- Multiple subsidiary guarantors provide additional credit support through cross-guarantees.
- All loan documents and collateral remain in full force and effect with liens and security interests preserved.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- The amendment provides more borrower-friendly terms by relaxing restrictions on unsecured indebtedness, potentially allowing for increased leverage.
- Limited visibility into overall financial metrics, pricing, or capacity utilization of the credit facility.
- The amendment allows restrictions that are "not materially more restrictive" than current agreement terms, which provides some subjectivity in interpretation.
