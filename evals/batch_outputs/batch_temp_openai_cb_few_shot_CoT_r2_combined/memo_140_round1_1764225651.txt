# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-01-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Square, Inc. is the borrower under this revolving credit facility with Goldman Sachs Bank USA serving as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Amendment modifies Square's existing Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated May 1, 2020. The transaction involves Square as borrower, Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and lender, and a syndicate including JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association as lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment updates definitions related to COVID-19 relief programs to reflect changes from the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021, and increases Square's capacity to incur Specified Indebtedness under the Paycheck Protection Program Liquidity Facility (PPPLF) to $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount outstanding.

**Company Background:** Square, Inc. operates as a financial services and technology company, with this amendment demonstrating their participation in federal COVID-19 relief programs during the pandemic period.
