# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 29, 2021, Vine Energy Holdings LLC, a Delaware-based oil and gas company, executed Amendment No. 2 to its Second Lien Credit Agreement with Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. as Administrative Agent and various lenders managed by GoldenTree Asset Management.
- The original Second Lien Credit Agreement was dated December 30, 2020, establishing a credit facility for this energy sector borrower.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the minimum hedging requirements under Section 6.17, specifically changing the hedging covenant to require 70% hedging coverage of reasonably anticipated projected natural gas production from Proved Developed Producing Reserves over a 24-month period.
- Vine Energy Holdings LLC operates in the oil and gas sector with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Vine Energy Operating LP, Brix Operating LLC, and various other energy-related entities, suggesting a diversified upstream energy operation.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established relationship with reputable institutional lender Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, indicating access to sophisticated capital markets
- Comprehensive guarantee structure with multiple subsidiary guarantors providing credit enhancement
- Focus on natural gas production from Proved Developed Producing Reserves, which typically represent lower-risk, cash-flowing assets in the energy sector
- Collateral documents remain in full force and effect, maintaining security interests in borrower assets
- Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Energy sector exposure with inherent commodity price volatility and operational risks
- Second lien position indicates subordinated security relative to senior debt obligations
- Hedging covenant modifications may indicate previous compliance challenges or changing market conditions
- Limited visibility into financial metrics, pricing terms, or overall debt capacity constrains comprehensive credit assessment
