# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In December 2022, Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc. and Citibank N.A. (as Administrative Agent) executed a first amendment to their existing credit agreement originally dated March 4, 2021.
- Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower in this credit facility with Citibank N.A. acting as Administrative Agent for a syndicate of lenders.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to transition the credit facility's benchmark rate from USD LIBOR to Adjusted Term SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) in advance of LIBOR's discontinuation. This represents an "Early Opt-in Election" as defined in the original credit agreement.
- The amendment implements benchmark replacement conforming changes, replacing LIBOR-based rates with Adjusted Term SOFR plus credit spread adjustments (11.448 bps for one month, 26.161 bps for three months, and 42.826 bps for six months) as recommended by the Alternative Reference Rates Committee.

• Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement

• Limited visibility into overall credit facility terms, financial covenants, or borrower financial performance
