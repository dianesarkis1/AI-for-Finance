# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-04-14

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit facility. The borrower is part of the broader Apollo organization, a leading global alternative investment manager.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction involves a Guarantor Joinder Agreement adding Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. as new guarantors to an existing credit agreement dated October 12, 2022. The facility is a revolving credit structure with letter of credit capability, administered by Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The joinder agreement serves to strengthen the credit facility by adding guarantors to induce lenders to maintain and/or make additional loans and issuing banks to maintain and/or issue additional letters of credit. This provides enhanced security for both existing and future credit extensions.

**Company Background:** Apollo operates through a sophisticated corporate structure with the borrower supported by thirteen initial guarantors, primarily Cayman Islands exempted limited partnerships under the Apollo Principal Holdings series, plus AMH Holdings (Cayman), L.P. The addition of Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. further consolidates guarantee support within the Apollo organization.
