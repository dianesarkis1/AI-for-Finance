# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On July 7, 2025, ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP entered into a Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement to increase its revolving credit facility.
- ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP is a Delaware limited partnership that serves as the borrower, with ExchangeRight Income Fund (d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT), a Maryland REIT, as the parent company.
- The transaction adds Renasant Bank as a new lender with a $15,000,000 incremental revolving commitment, bringing the total aggregate revolving commitments to $150,000,000.
