# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-04-14

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit facility with Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This is a Guarantor Joinder Agreement adding Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. as new guarantors to an existing credit agreement originally dated October 12, 2022. The structure involves a revolving facility with letters of credit capability, supported by multiple guarantor entities across various jurisdictions.

**Purpose of Financing:** The joinder serves to induce lenders to maintain and/or make additional loans and issuing banks to maintain and/or issue additional letters of credit, while providing consideration for loans previously made and letters of credit previously issued.

**Company Background:** Apollo operates through a complex structure of related entities, with initial guarantors including thirteen Apollo Principal Holdings entities (primarily Cayman Islands exempted limited partnerships) and AMH Holdings (Cayman), L.P. The addition of Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. expands the guarantee pool supporting the credit facility.
