**Date of Agreement:** 2021-01-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Square, Inc. serves as borrower under a revolving credit facility with Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of institutional lenders.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Amendment to Square's Revolving Credit Agreement (originally dated May 1, 2020) involves Square as borrower, Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association as lenders.
