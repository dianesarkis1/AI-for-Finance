# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-10

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG), a Delaware corporation, is a publicly-traded asset management holding company.

**Transaction Description:** This Second Amendment to Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement modifies the existing term credit facility originally established on January 18, 2019. The amendment involves AMG as borrower, Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent, and multiple institutional lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically updates the Applicable Margin pricing grid contained in Annex I of the original Credit Agreement, effectively repricing the interest rate spreads based on AMG's debt rating levels.

**Company Background:** AMG operates as an asset management holding company. The original Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement was dated January 18, 2019, establishing the foundational credit facility that this amendment now modifies.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Investment-grade rated borrower with comprehensive pricing grid spanning A+/A1/A+ to BBB/Baa2/BBB levels
• Strong institutional banking relationship with Bank of America N.A. as Administrative Agent
• Amendment represents a repricing opportunity, suggesting favorable market conditions for the borrower
• Borrower confirms no defaults or events of default are occurring or would result from this amendment
• Tri-agency rating recognition (S&P, Moody's, Fitch) provides comprehensive credit assessment framework

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Asset management business model creates exposure to market volatility and fluctuations in assets under management
• Pricing increases significantly at lower rating levels (up to 1.225% margin for Eurodollar loans at BBB level) indicate meaningful credit risk if ratings deteriorate
• Limited financial performance visibility from amendment document alone, requiring reliance on public company disclosures
• Interest rate structure tied to floating rates creates potential margin compression risk in rising rate environments
