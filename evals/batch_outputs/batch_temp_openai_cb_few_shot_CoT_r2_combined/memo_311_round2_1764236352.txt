# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-12-12

**Borrower Overview:** Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower under this syndicated credit facility.

**Transaction Description:** This First Amendment modifies an existing First Lien Credit Agreement originally executed on March 4, 2021, between Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc., a syndicate of lenders, and Citibank N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment implements a proactive transition from USD LIBOR to Adjusted Term SOFR as the benchmark rate through an "Early Opt-in Election" mechanism, ahead of LIBOR's regulatory discontinuation. The amendment also addresses alternative currency rate replacements, transitioning from LIBOR for Alternative Currencies (EUR, CHF, JPY, GBP) to the applicable RFR (risk-free reference rate) following ICE Benchmark Administration's March 5, 2021 announcement to cease LIBOR publication for these currencies after December 31, 2021.

**Company Background:** Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc. is a Delaware corporation with an established syndicated credit facility that maintains relationships with multiple financial institutions under Citibank N.A.'s administrative oversight.
