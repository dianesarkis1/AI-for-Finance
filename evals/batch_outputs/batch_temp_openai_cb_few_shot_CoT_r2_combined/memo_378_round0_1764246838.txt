## 1. Executive Summary

- On August 1, 2025, Millrose Properties, Inc., a Maryland corporation, entered into a First Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated June 24, 2025, establishing a credit facility for Millrose Properties with Goldman Sachs Bank USA serving as Administrative Agent alongside other major lenders including Bank of America, Citibank, Citizens Bank, JPMorgan Chase Bank, and Wells Fargo Bank.
- The amendment introduces several key modifications: (1) adds a new "Designated Issuance" definition allowing for debt or equity securities issuance, (2) introduces a Stage 4 Funding Fee of 0.250% payable 270 days after Initial Draw Date, and (3) modifies mandatory prepayment provisions to allow retention of proceeds from designated issuances up to $500 million in outstanding loans.
