## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Five entertainment library financing entities: LGTV Library Financing LLC, Artisan Library Financing LLC, Summit Library Financing LLC, LG Film Library Financing LLC, and LGIM Library UK Financing Limited, along with their respective parent holding companies (LGTV Library Holdco LLC, Artisan Library Holdco LLC, Summit Library Holdco LLC, LG Film Library Holdco LLC, and LGIM Library UK Holdco Limited).

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to an Amended and Restated Credit, Security and Pledge Agreement, featuring an "Upsize" transaction that increases outstanding loan amounts across the 14-member lender syndicate led by Fifth Third Bank as Administrative Agent and East West Bank as Reserve Deposit Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates an upsize to the existing credit facility supporting entertainment content library assets, with specialized financing vehicles structured to hold different content libraries across multiple entertainment brands.

**Company Background:** Multi-entity entertainment library financing structure originally established November 5, 2024, with previous amendment in December 2024, indicating active capital deployment and growth in the entertainment content sector.
