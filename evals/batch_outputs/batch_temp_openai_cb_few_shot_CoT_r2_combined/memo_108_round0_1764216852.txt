# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On December 19, 2024, Box, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) and Wells Fargo Bank, National Association entered into Amendment No. 1 to their Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated June 30, 2023.
- Box, Inc. serves as the Borrower in this bilateral credit facility with Wells Fargo Bank as the sole Lender.
- The amendment modifies the commitment amount and liquidity condition definitions within the existing credit agreement.
- The primary purpose is to amend the "Commitment" definition to establish the Lender's commitment at $75,000,000 and to revise the "Liquidity Condition" to require the Borrower's liquidity to be greater than or equal to the outstanding principal amount of existing convertible notes.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as the lender provides institutional credibility and banking relationship strength
- The Borrower reaffirms compliance with all representations, warranties, and covenants, with no Event of Default or Potential Event of Default occurring
- All existing security documents, guarantees, pledges, and security interests remain in full force and effect
- The amendment maintains existing collateral protection and lien priorities

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Bilateral facility structure concentrates credit risk with a single lender, potentially limiting refinancing options
- The revised "Liquidity Condition" ties borrowing capacity to convertible note obligations, which could constrain access to credit if convertible notes remain outstanding
- Limited visibility into pricing, maturity, financial covenants, and overall credit metrics constrains comprehensive risk assessment
