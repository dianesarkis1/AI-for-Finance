# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-08-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Lilis Energy Inc., a Nevada corporation, operates in the energy sector through multiple subsidiaries including Brushy Resources Inc., Hurricane Resources LLC, Impetro Operating LLC, Lilis Operating Company LLC, and Impetro Resources LLC.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents the third amendment to a Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement originally executed on June 30, 2020. The lending syndicate consists of BMO Harris Bank N.A. serving as Administrative Agent, along with Capital One National Association and Credit Suisse AG Cayman Islands Branch as lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment extends certain Chapter 11 bankruptcy milestone deadlines to provide additional time for the borrower's restructuring process. Specifically, the amendment modifies Section 8.23(e) of the original credit agreement, extending a critical milestone deadline from August 28, 2020 to September 3, 2020.

**Company Background:** Lilis Energy operates as an energy company in the oil and gas sector and is currently undergoing Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings. The company maintains operations through its various subsidiary entities, all of which serve as guarantors under the credit facility.
