# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 6, 2022, Oil-Dri Corporation of America entered into a consent agreement with BMO Harris Bank N.A. to modify their existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006.
- Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower, with multiple domestic subsidiaries acting as guarantors, including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Mounds Management Inc., Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.
- The transaction is a one-time modification to accommodate a specific non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to the Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment taken during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022.
- The purpose is to modify the definitions of "Consolidated EBITDA" and "Consolidated EBITR" to add back this one-time impairment charge, ensuring the company remains in compliance with its financial covenants.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Long-standing credit relationship dating back to 2006, indicating stability and lender confidence in the borrower
- Proactive approach by the borrower to address covenant compliance before any potential default
- Company representations confirm no current defaults or events of default exist under the credit agreement
- Multiple subsidiary guarantors provide additional credit support and security for the lender

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- The goodwill impairment suggests potential operational challenges or asset value deterioration in the Retail and Wholesale segment
- Modification of EBITDA definitions to exclude impairment charges could mask underlying business performance issues
- Limited visibility into overall financial performance, liquidity position, or other covenant requirements from this document alone
