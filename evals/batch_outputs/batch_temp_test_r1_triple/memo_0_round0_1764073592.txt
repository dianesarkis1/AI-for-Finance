# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 10, 2021, Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG), a Delaware corporation, executed a Second Amendment to its Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement with Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent and Lender.
- The original Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement was dated January 18, 2019.
- The amendment's primary purpose is to modify the "Applicable Margin" pricing structure contained in Annex I of the Credit Agreement, replacing the existing pricing grid with new rates based on debt ratings from S&P, Moody's, and Fitch.
- AMG operates as an investment management company that partners with boutique asset management firms globally, providing capital and strategic support to independent investment managers.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong credit profile evidenced by investment-grade debt rating requirements (A+ to BBB levels) in the pricing grid, indicating institutional-quality credit
- Established banking relationship with Bank of America, N.A. as both Administrative Agent and Lender
- The borrower represents that no Default or Event of Default has occurred and is continuing
- All existing loan covenants and terms remain in full force and effect, providing continued lender protection

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited disclosure of specific financial metrics, deal size, or maturity terms constrains comprehensive credit assessment
- The amendment appears to adjust pricing terms, but without comparison to previous rates, the directional impact on borrowing costs is unclear
- Investment management industry exposure creates sensitivity to market volatility and asset-based fee structures
