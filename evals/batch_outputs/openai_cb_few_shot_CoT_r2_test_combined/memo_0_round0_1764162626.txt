# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In June 2021, Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG), a Delaware corporation, executed a second amendment to its Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement with Bank of America N.A. as Administrative Agent.
- The original credit agreement was dated January 18, 2019, establishing AMG as the borrower with Bank of America serving dual roles as both Administrative Agent and Lender.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the Applicable Margin contained in Annex I of the Credit Agreement, replacing the existing pricing grid with new interest rate spreads based on AMG's debt ratings from S&P, Moody's, and Fitch.
- AMG is an asset management company that operates through a network of affiliated investment management firms, providing diversified investment strategies and solutions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established borrower with investment-grade credit profile, as evidenced by the pricing grid accommodating ratings from A+/A1/A+ down to BBB/Baa2/BBB
- Strong banking relationship with Bank of America N.A. as both Administrative Agent and Lender
- Rating-based pricing mechanism provides transparency and alignment with credit quality
- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default are occurring or continuing

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited visibility into overall deal size, maturity profile, and comprehensive covenant structure
- Amendment appears to potentially increase pricing spreads (though previous pricing grid not provided for comparison)
- Pricing tied to external ratings creates potential volatility in borrowing costs based on rating agency actions
