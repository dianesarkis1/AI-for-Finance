# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-09-30

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities and serves as the Borrower under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019). Complex Services Inc. serves as guarantor. The company operates under a Casino Operating Service Agreement (COSA) with the Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG).

**Transaction Description:** This Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver was granted by Bank of Montreal (as Administrative Agent) on behalf of the Required Lenders, extending temporary covenant relief through November 30, 2020. The waiver amends and restates previous limited waivers dating back to May 2020.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver provides continued relief from financial covenant breaches, closure defaults, and compliance certificate delivery requirements due to COVID-19 mandated casino closures. The original credit agreement appears to include revolving credit facilities to support casino operations.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities that were subject to government-mandated closure for more than 60 consecutive days due to the COVID-19 pandemic. OLG has provided financial support through relief from threshold payments and continued Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Regulatory support from OLG through continued Fixed Component Per Diem payments and relief from threshold payments during closure periods
• Borrower maintains minimum liquidity requirement of CAD 15,000,000 throughout the waiver period
• Enhanced monitoring through mandatory weekly liquidity reporting to the Administrative Agent
• Pricing grid adjusted to Level 5 (highest margin tier), improving lender compensation for increased risk
• Government entity (OLG) providing direct financial relief to support borrower operations

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extended operational disruption due to government-mandated casino closures lasting more than 60 consecutive days
• Financial covenant breaches under Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 of the Credit Agreement, indicating deteriorated financial performance
• Restricted access to additional credit facilities during the waiver period, limiting financial flexibility
• Prohibition on distributions and acquisitions during waiver period
• Event of default triggered if OLG ceases providing relief for more than five business days
