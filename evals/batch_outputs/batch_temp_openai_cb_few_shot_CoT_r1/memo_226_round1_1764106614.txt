# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Vertical Horizons, Ltd. is a Cayman Islands exempted company serving as the borrower in this aircraft financing structure.

**Transaction Description:** This is Amendment No. 1 to a Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated December 28, 2021. The transaction involves a syndicated credit facility with Citibank, N.A. serving as both Facility Agent and Arranger, alongside lenders including Barclays Bank PLC, Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. Bank of Utah serves as Security Trustee.

**Purpose of Financing:** The facility provides PDP (Pre-Delivery Payment) financing for the acquisition of nine (9) Airbus A320NEO aircraft and fifty (50) Airbus A321NEO aircraft. The amendment extends certain agreement deadlines and updates related documentation.

**Company Background:** The transaction is connected to Frontier Airlines' fleet expansion, with Frontier Group Holdings, Inc., Frontier Airlines Holdings, Inc., and Frontier Airlines, Inc. serving as guarantors. The amendment extends key deadlines from July 1, 2022 to April 1, 2023 and updates assignment agreements with Airbus and step-in agreements among the parties.
