**Transaction Description:** Suspension of Rights Agreement executed between Emerson Electric Co. and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, temporarily modifying terms under the existing Credit Agreement dated May 23, 2018. The transaction involves multiple parties including the Company, subsidiary borrowers, syndicate lenders, and LC issuers.
