# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On May 27, 2025, Karman Holding Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into a First Amendment to Credit Agreement with Citibank N.A. as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent.
- The amendment provides for an incremental term loan facility of $75,000,000 to be funded by Citibank N.A. as the sole First Amendment Incremental Term Lender.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated April 1, 2025, making this a relatively quick follow-on financing within approximately two months of the initial facility.
- The proceeds will be used to repay outstanding Revolving Credit Loans, for working capital and other general corporate purposes (including Permitted Investments), and to pay associated fees and expenses.
- Karman Holding Inc. operates through various subsidiaries including Karman Space & Defense LLC, Aerospace Engineering LLC, Wolcott Design Services LLC, Amro Fabricating Corporation, American Automated Engineering Inc., and Systima Technologies Inc., suggesting involvement in aerospace and defense sectors.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong lender relationship with Citibank N.A. serving as both Administrative Agent and sole incremental term lender, indicating confidence in the credit
- Multiple subsidiary guarantors providing additional credit support and diversified business operations
- SOFR-based pricing structure aligning with current market standards
- Quarterly amortization schedule of 0.25% provides modest debt reduction over time
- No events of default under key sections (8.01(a), (f) or (g)) as a condition precedent

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Single lender concentration risk with Citibank N.A. providing the entire $75 million incremental facility
- Recent vintage of original credit facility (April 2025) followed quickly by incremental financing may indicate higher capital needs than originally anticipated
- Limited visibility into specific financial covenants or overall debt capacity constraints
