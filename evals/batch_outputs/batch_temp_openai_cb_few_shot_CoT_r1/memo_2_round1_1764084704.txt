# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities and serves as the Borrower under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019, with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor and Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver granted by Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent on behalf of Required Lenders, extending previous COVID-19 related covenant relief through March 31, 2021.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver provides covenant relief due to government-mandated casino closures related to the COVID-19 pandemic, waiving financial covenant breaches and compliance certificate delivery requirements during the Extended Waiver Period.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure by OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation) due to COVID-19 regulations, requiring ongoing financial accommodation from lenders with OLG providing financial support during the closure period.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government support through OLG providing financial relief and covering fixed component payments during closure period
• Borrower maintains minimum liquidity requirement of $15,000,000
• Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
• Extension fee of 5 basis points demonstrates lender compensation for additional risk

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extended waiver period through March 31, 2021 indicates prolonged financial stress
• No new advances available under either credit facility during waiver period
• Applicable margin increased to Level 5 (highest pricing tier)
• Restrictions on distributions, acquisitions, and material contract modifications
• Event of Default triggered if OLG ceases support payments for more than 5 business days
