# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On April 14, 2023, Apollo Global Management and Apollo Asset Management joined as additional guarantors to an existing credit agreement dated October 12, 2022.
- The borrower is Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, with Citibank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent for the revolving credit facility.
- The transaction involves adding two new guarantors (Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc.) to strengthen the guarantee structure of the existing credit facility.
- The purpose is to induce lenders to maintain and/or make additional loans and issuing banks to maintain and/or issue additional letters of credit, serving as consideration for previously made loans and issued letters of credit.
- Apollo is a prominent alternative investment management company with multiple holding entities across various jurisdictions including Cayman Islands and Anguilla, indicating a global operational footprint.
