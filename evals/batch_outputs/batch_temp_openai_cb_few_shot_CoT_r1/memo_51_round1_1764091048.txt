# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation, operates as a regulated utility company serving Texas and New Mexico markets.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents an increase supplement to the Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated March 11, 2022. The structure involves adding CoBank, ACB as a new lender to the existing credit facility syndicate, with KeyBank National Association serving as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The financing serves to increase the existing credit facility commitment by $25,000,000 through the addition of CoBank, ACB as a new participating lender under Section 2.1(f) of the existing Credit Agreement.

**Company Background:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company operates as a regulated utility corporation incorporated in Texas, maintaining credit facilities secured by mortgage bond structures through various supplemental indentures with a First Mortgage Bond Trustee.
