# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-07-07

**Borrower / Company Overview:** ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the Borrower. The Parent entity is ExchangeRight Income Fund, d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT, a real estate investment trust formed under Maryland law.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction is a Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement that adds Renasant Bank as an Additional Lender to an existing revolving credit facility. The transaction increases the total revolving commitments by $15,000,000, bringing the aggregate facility size to $150,000,000. Wells Fargo Bank, National Association serves as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The financing provides additional revolving credit capacity to support the borrower's ongoing business operations and liquidity needs.

**Company Background:** The borrower operates as part of a REIT structure focused on commercial real estate investments, specifically net leased portfolio properties. The organization maintains multiple subsidiary entities including various Delaware statutory trusts (ExchangeRight Net Leased Portfolio 3-9 DST) and operates under established credit facilities dating back to May 30, 2024.
