# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-05-10

**Borrower Overview:** LifeStance Health Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, operates as the borrower under a credit facility administered by Capital One, National Association. The company is a subsidiary of Lynnwood Intermediate Holdings, Inc., also a Delaware corporation, and appears to operate in the healthcare services sector.

**Transaction Description:** This is a Second Amendment to Credit Agreement, modifying the original credit agreement dated May 4, 2022 (previously amended by a First Amendment dated November 7, 2023). The transaction involves LifeStance Health Holdings, Inc. as borrower, with Capital One, National Association serving as Administrative Agent, Revolver Agent, and Collateral Agent, and Unitranche Loan Transaction II, LLC as a Required Facility Lender (managed by both Capital One and HPS Investment Partners, LLC).

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment extends the Delayed Draw Term Loan Commitment Expiration Date to August 14, 2024, providing the borrower with additional time to access committed delayed draw term loan facilities under the existing credit structure.

**Company Background:** LifeStance Health Holdings operates through various subsidiary guarantors under its parent company structure. The company maintains an established credit relationship with institutional lenders including Capital One and HPS Investment Partners, suggesting it is a substantial healthcare services enterprise requiring sophisticated financing arrangements.
