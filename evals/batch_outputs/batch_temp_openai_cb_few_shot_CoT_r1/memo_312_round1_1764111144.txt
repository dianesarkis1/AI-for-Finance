**Borrower / Company Overview:** APA Finance III Borrower, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, operating through a multi-tiered ownership structure ultimately controlled by Altus Power, Inc.

**Transaction Description:** Issuance of a Class A Note in the amount of $19,195,000 to Security Life of Denver Insurance Company under an existing credit facility. The transaction involves U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, and U.S. Bank National Association as Document Custodian.

**Purpose of Financing:** The specific purpose of the financing is not disclosed in the available documentation.

**Company Background:** The borrower operates under a complex Delaware LLC structure with APA Finance III Borrower Holdings, LLC as its sole member, ultimately tracing ownership through APA Finance III, LLC and APA Generation, LLC to Altus Power, Inc. The note was executed by Gregg Felton, Co-CEO of Altus Power, Inc.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**

• Structured credit facility with collateral agent arrangement providing security
• Clear corporate governance with identifiable ultimate parent company (Altus Power, Inc.)
• Active credit management evidenced by multiple amendments demonstrating ongoing lender engagement

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Limited visibility into borrower financial performance and business operations
• Three amendments within eleven months of original agreement may signal evolving credit conditions or covenant adjustments
• Multi-layered LLC ownership structure could potentially complicate enforcement actions
• Default interest provisions create additional cost exposure for overdue amounts
