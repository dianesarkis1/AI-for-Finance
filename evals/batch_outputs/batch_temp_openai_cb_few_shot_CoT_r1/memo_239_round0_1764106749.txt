# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On 2021-06-29, Vine Energy Holdings LLC and its lenders entered into Amendment No. 2 to their Second Lien Credit Agreement, originally dated December 30, 2020.
- Vine Energy Holdings LLC, a Delaware limited liability company operating in the oil and gas sector, serves as the Borrower with multiple subsidiary guarantors including Vine Oil & Gas GP LLC, Brix Operating LLC, Vine Minerals LLC, and others.
- Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. acts as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, with a syndicate of lenders managed by GoldenTree Asset Management, LP including FS Credit Income Fund, GoldenTree 2004 Trust, and various institutional investors.
- The primary purpose of this amendment is to modify the minimum hedging requirements in Section 6.17 of the Credit Agreement, specifically adjusting the hedging covenant to require 70% hedging of reasonably anticipated projected hydrocarbon production of natural gas from Proved Developed Producing Reserves over a 24-month period.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established relationship with Morgan Stanley Senior Funding as Administrative Agent, providing institutional-grade oversight and expertise in energy sector financing
- Diversified lender syndicate managed by experienced credit investment firm GoldenTree Asset Management, indicating sophisticated institutional backing
- Amendment includes comprehensive ratification and reaffirmation of all collateral documents and liens, ensuring security interests remain fully enforceable
- Borrower representations confirm no current defaults or events of default exist under the credit agreement

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Energy sector exposure with commodity price volatility risk, partially mitigated by mandatory hedging requirements of 70% of natural gas production over 24 months
- Second lien structure indicates subordinated credit position with potentially higher risk profile
- Amendment provides flexibility allowing borrower to comply with hedging requirements based on commercially reasonable market rates, which could result in lower hedging levels in adverse market conditions
