## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-04-14

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as borrower under a revolving credit facility. The company is part of the Apollo Global Management ecosystem, a leading alternative investment management platform.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction involves a guarantor joinder agreement whereby Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. join as additional guarantors to an existing credit agreement originally dated October 12, 2022. The structure includes multiple existing guarantors across Cayman Islands and Anguilla jurisdictions, with Citibank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The guarantor addition is designed to induce lenders to maintain existing loans and make additional loans, while encouraging issuing banks to maintain current letters of credit and issue additional letters of credit. This serves as consideration for previously extended credit facilities.

**Company Background:** Apollo operates through a complex multi-jurisdictional structure with numerous principal holdings entities domiciled in Cayman Islands and Anguilla. The borrower entity focuses on the revolving facility component of the broader credit arrangement, supported by an extensive guarantee network.
