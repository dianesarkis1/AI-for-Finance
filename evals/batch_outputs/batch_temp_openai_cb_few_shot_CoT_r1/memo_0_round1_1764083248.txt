# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-10

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG) is a Delaware corporation operating as an asset management company with an established investment grade credit profile across major rating agencies.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a Second Amendment to AMG's existing Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement, originally executed on January 18, 2019. The amendment involves AMG as borrower, Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent, and multiple institutional lenders. The structure modifies pricing terms through an updated Annex I to the original credit agreement.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically revises the "Applicable Margin" pricing grid to establish new interest rate spreads tied to AMG's debt ratings from S&P, Moody's, and Fitch rating agencies, effective June 10, 2021.

**Company Background:** AMG maintains investment grade ratings ranging from A+ to BBB levels, indicating a solid credit foundation within the asset management sector and suggesting established market presence and operational stability.
