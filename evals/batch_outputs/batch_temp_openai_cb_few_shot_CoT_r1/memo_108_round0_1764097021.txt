# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On December 19, 2024, Box, Inc., a Delaware corporation, and Wells Fargo Bank, National Association executed Amendment No. 1 to their Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated June 30, 2023.
- Box, Inc. is a cloud content management and collaboration platform provider serving as the Borrower in this bilateral credit facility with Wells Fargo as the sole Lender.
- The amendment modifies the commitment amount to $75,000,000 and revises the "Liquidity Condition" definition to require that the Borrower's liquidity be greater than or equal to the outstanding principal amount of existing convertible notes.
- The purpose of this financing amendment is to adjust the credit facility terms, specifically the commitment size and liquidity covenant requirements.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established relationship with Wells Fargo, a major U.S. financial institution, indicating lender confidence in Box, Inc.
- Borrower represents that no Event of Default or Potential Event of Default has occurred and is continuing
- All security documents, guarantees, pledges, and security interests remain in full force and effect
- Amendment reaffirms all existing loan documents and maintains collateral protection

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Single lender concentration risk with Wells Fargo as the sole credit provider
- Liquidity condition covenant tied to convertible notes outstanding may create refinancing pressure
- Limited financial information available to assess overall credit profile and performance metrics
