# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-12-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc., a Delaware corporation, operates as the borrower under a syndicated credit facility with Citibank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This First Amendment to Credit Agreement modifies the existing First Lien Credit Agreement originally executed on March 4, 2021. The amendment involves Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc. as Borrower, multiple syndicated lenders, and Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates the transition from LIBOR-based interest rates to SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) benchmark rates in response to the cessation of LIBOR publication. The amendment replaces USD LIBOR with Adjusted Term SOFR and addresses the discontinuation of Alternative Currency LIBOR rates.

**Company Background:** Petco operates under a syndicated credit facility structure with multiple institutional lenders, demonstrating access to capital markets and established banking relationships with major financial institutions.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
