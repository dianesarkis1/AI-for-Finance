# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-09-02

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI) is a Virginia corporation and established multinational tobacco and consumer goods company with significant global market presence and operations.

**Transaction Description:** This amendment modifies PMI's existing 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement dated May 11, 2022. The transaction involves PMI as borrower, with Citibank Europe PLC, UK Branch serving as Facility Agent, and a syndicate of 16 major international financial institutions as lenders. The amendment specifically revises the "Minimum Acceptance Condition" definition to provide more flexible terms for PMI's acquisition activities.

**Purpose of Financing:** The bridge credit facility supports PMI's strategic acquisition activities, specifically facilitating an offer for Target Shares. The amendment reduces the minimum acceptance threshold to exceed 50% of total issued and outstanding Target Shares (excluding treasury shares), making the acquisition conditions more achievable.

**Company Background:** PMI operates as a major player in the global tobacco and consumer goods industry, with sufficient scale and creditworthiness to attract a high-quality international banking syndicate for acquisition financing needs.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• **Strong Credit Profile:** PMI's established position as a multinational corporation with global operations and market leadership in tobacco/consumer goods sector
• **Premier Banking Relationships:** Syndicate includes 16 top-tier international financial institutions across multiple jurisdictions, demonstrating strong bank confidence
• **Strategic Facility Structure:** 364-day bridge format provides interim financing flexibility while PMI arranges permanent capital structure
• **Lender Cooperation:** Unanimous lender agreement to amendment terms demonstrates strong relationship management and deal execution capability
• **Geographic Diversification:** International lender base spans major financial centers including US, UK, Europe, and Asia

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• **Relaxed Terms:** Amendment reduces acquisition success requirements, potentially increasing execution risk for underlying transaction; mitigated by PMI's experienced M&A execution track record
• **Bridge Financing Risk:** 364-day term creates refinancing pressure; mitigated by PMI's access to multiple capital markets and strong credit profile
• **Limited Covenant Visibility:** Amendment provides minimal insight into broader financial maintenance requirements; however, PMI's investment-grade profile suggests appropriate covenant structure
• **Acquisition Integration Risk:** Target acquisition success depends on achieving minimum acceptance thresholds; mitigated by more favorable 50% threshold established in amendment
