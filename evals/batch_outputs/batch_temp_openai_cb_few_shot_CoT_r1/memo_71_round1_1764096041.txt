# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2022-08-18

Halozyme Therapeutics, Inc., a Delaware biotechnology corporation, executed Amendment No. 1 to its existing credit agreement, increasing its revolving credit facility by $225,000,000. The amendment modifies the original Credit Agreement dated May 24, 2022, with Bank of America, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent and Swing Line Lender. The transaction increases total Revolving Credit Commitments from $350,000,000 to $575,000,000 through Incremental Revolving Commitments provided by eleven Increase Loan Lenders.

The financing proceeds will be used in accordance with Section 6.11 of the Amended Credit Agreement for general corporate purposes. Halozyme operates as a biotechnology company with subsidiaries Halozyme, Inc. and Antares Pharma, Inc. serving as Guarantors. The amendment requires full repayment of all outstanding Term A Loans and maintains the existing collateral structure.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Premier lender syndicate including Bank of America, Wells Fargo, JPMorgan Chase, Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Citibank, and six additional institutional lenders demonstrating strong market confidence

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
