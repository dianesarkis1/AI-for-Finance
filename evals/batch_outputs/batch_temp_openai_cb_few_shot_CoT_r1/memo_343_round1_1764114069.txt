# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-09-04

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II is the borrower under this senior secured revolving credit facility, operating as part of the Goldman Sachs organization.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction involves a commitment increase request under an existing Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated February 28, 2023. The facility includes multiple lenders and issuing banks with Truist Bank serving as Administrative Agent. The original agreement has been amended twice (July 31, 2023 and May 30, 2024).

**Purpose of Financing:** The purpose is to increase the aggregate revolving commitments from $455,000,000 to $480,000,000, providing additional liquidity capacity through an increase in BNP Paribas' Multicurrency Commitment from $25,000,000 to $50,000,000.

**Company Background:** Goldman Sachs Middle Market Lending Corp. II operates as a lending vehicle within the Goldman Sachs organization, focusing on middle market lending activities.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong sponsor affiliation with Goldman Sachs, a globally recognized investment banking and financial services firm
• Established credit facility with multiple amendments indicating ongoing lender support and relationship management
• Commitment increase demonstrates lender confidence and borrower's access to additional liquidity
• Multi-bank syndicate structure with reputable institutions (Truist Bank, BNP Paribas) providing diversified funding sources

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Limited financial information available to assess underlying credit quality or covenant compliance
• Increased leverage exposure through higher commitment levels may elevate credit risk
• Information constraints limit visibility into pricing, covenants, and collateral structure
