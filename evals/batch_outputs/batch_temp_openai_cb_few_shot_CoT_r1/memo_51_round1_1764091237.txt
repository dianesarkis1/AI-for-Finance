**Date of Agreement:** 2022-05-13

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Texas-New Mexico Power Company, a Texas corporation, operates as a regulated utility company serving Texas and New Mexico markets.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction constitutes a commitment increase supplement to the Borrower's existing Fourth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated March 11, 2022. **Purpose of Financing:** The financing represents an expansion of the existing credit facility through the addition of a new lender commitment of $25,000,000 under Section 2.1(f) of the base Credit Agreement.
