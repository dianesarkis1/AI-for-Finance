# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 16, 2023, Nasdaq, Inc. entered into Amendment No. 2 to its Amended and Restated Credit Agreement with Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- The original credit agreement was dated December 16, 2022, and was previously amended on March 29, 2023.
- The amendment facilitates Nasdaq's acquisition of Adenza Holdings, Inc. pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 10, 2023.
- The primary purpose is to modify leverage ratio covenants to accommodate the acquisition financing, establishing a stepped-down leverage covenant structure post-acquisition.
- Nasdaq, Inc. is a Delaware corporation operating as a financial technology company providing trading, clearing, exchange technology, regulatory, and market data services globally.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong syndicate of premier financial institutions including Bank of America, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan Chase, Citibank, Morgan Stanley, Wells Fargo, and other major banks
- Structured step-down leverage covenant provides clear deleveraging pathway over time
- Borrower maintains compliance with all existing covenants and representations
- Amendment includes provisions for additional acquisition capacity with appropriate leverage constraints

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Temporary increase in maximum leverage ratio to 4.75x immediately following the acquisition, representing elevated credit risk
- The acquisition may increase business concentration or integration risks, though specific details are not disclosed
- Covenant relief is time-limited with mandatory step-downs providing structural protection to lenders
