# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under regulatory oversight from Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG). The company is party to a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent and Complex Services Inc. as guarantor.

**Transaction Description:** This Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver extends COVID-19 related relief through March 31, 2021, superseding previous waivers granted since May 2020. The Administrative Agent, acting on behalf of Required Lenders, provides covenant relief while implementing enhanced monitoring and restrictions.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver addresses financial covenant breaches and operational defaults resulting from government-mandated casino closures exceeding 60 consecutive days during the COVID-19 pandemic.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure by regulatory authorities. OLG has provided financial relief through letters dated April 19, June 11, and June 12, 2020, including relief from threshold payments and continued fixed component per diem payments during the closure period.
