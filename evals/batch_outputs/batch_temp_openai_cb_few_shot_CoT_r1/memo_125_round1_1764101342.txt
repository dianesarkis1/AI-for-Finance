# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2020-08-28

**Borrower:** Lilis Energy Inc., a Nevada corporation operating under Chapter 11 bankruptcy proceedings since June 2020.

**Transaction Description:** Third Amendment to Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-In-Possession Credit Agreement between Lilis Energy Inc. as borrower, multiple subsidiary guarantors, BMO Harris Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent, and lenders including Capital One National Association and Credit Suisse AG Cayman Islands Branch.

**Purpose:** Extension of Chapter 11 milestone deadline from August 28, 2020 to September 3, 2020, providing additional time for bankruptcy reorganization process.

**Company Background:** Energy sector company with operations conducted through five subsidiary guarantors: Brushy Resources Inc., Hurricane Resources LLC, Impetro Operating LLC, Lilis Operating Company LLC, and Impetro Resources LLC. The company entered Chapter 11 proceedings and established the original DIP facility on June 30, 2020.
