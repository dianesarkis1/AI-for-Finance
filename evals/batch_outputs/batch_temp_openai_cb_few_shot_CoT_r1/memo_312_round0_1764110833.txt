# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On December 20, 2023, APA Finance III Borrower, LLC issued a Class A Note to Security Life of Denver Insurance Company for $19,195,000.
- The Borrower is APA Finance III Borrower, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, which is ultimately owned through a chain of entities by Altus Power, Inc. The transaction involves U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as Administrative Agent and Collateral Agent, and U.S. Bank National Association as Document Custodian.
- This Class A Note is issued under a Credit Agreement originally dated February 15, 2023, which has been amended three times (June 15, 2023, July 21, 2023, and December 20, 2023).
- The note represents one of multiple notes under a broader credit facility structure, with interest payable on Interest Payment Dates as defined in the underlying Credit Agreement.
