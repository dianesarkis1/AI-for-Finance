## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-08-18

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Halozyme Therapeutics, Inc. is a Delaware corporation serving as the borrower under this credit facility amendment. The company operates with guaranteed subsidiaries including Halozyme, Inc. and Antares Pharma, Inc.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents Amendment No. 1 to an existing Credit Agreement originally dated May 24, 2022. The amendment establishes Incremental Revolving Commitments totaling $225,000,000 provided by multiple Increase Loan Lenders. **Purpose of Financing:** The amendment increases the existing revolving credit facility to provide additional liquidity for general corporate purposes. All proceeds from loans incurred after the Amendment No. 1 Effective Date will be used in accordance with Section 6.11 of the Amended Credit Agreement.

**Company Background:** Halozyme operates as a biotechnology company with operations structured through multiple subsidiary entities that serve as guarantors under the credit facility, indicating an established corporate structure with diversified business operations.
