# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On November 30, 2021, Team Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into Amendment No. 1 to its Unsecured Term Loan Credit Agreement with its lenders.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated November 9, 2021, making this a very recent amendment to a newly established facility.
- Team Inc. serves as the Borrower, with lenders including Corre Opportunities Qualified Master Fund LP, Corre Horizon Fund LP, and Corre Horizon Fund II LP.
- The purpose of this amendment is to provide short-term extensions on three critical obligations: (i) extend the PIK Interest payment date for Initial Term Loans from December 1 to December 6, 2021, (ii) extend the ABL Consent delivery deadline to December 6, 2021, and (iii) extend the Underlying Warrants issuance deadline to December 7, 2021.
- Team Inc. appears to be an established corporation requiring additional time to meet near-term obligations under a recently executed unsecured term loan facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Short-term nature of extensions (5-6 days) suggests minor administrative delays rather than fundamental credit issues
- Borrower proactively sought amendments rather than defaulting on obligations
- All lenders agreed to the modifications, indicating continued lender support
- Amendment preserves all existing loan document terms and lender rights

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Need for extensions shortly after facility origination (within 3 weeks) raises questions about execution capabilities
- PIK Interest structure suggests potential cash flow constraints requiring payment-in-kind arrangements
- Multiple concurrent deadline extensions indicate possible organizational or operational challenges
- Limited visibility into underlying credit metrics, collateral, or financial covenants
