# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 2, 2022, Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation, entered into Amendment No. 1 to its 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement originally dated May 11, 2022.
- The borrower is Philip Morris International Inc., a multinational tobacco and consumer goods company. The lender consortium includes major international financial institutions with Citibank Europe PLC, UK Branch serving as Facility Agent.
- This amendment modifies the definition of "Minimum Acceptance Condition" in the original bridge credit agreement, lowering the acceptance threshold from an unspecified level to exceeding 50% of total issued and outstanding Target Shares (excluding treasury shares).
- The financing appears to be structured to support PMI's acquisition activities, specifically an offer for Target Shares, with the amendment providing more flexible conditions for the transaction to proceed.
- PMI is a well-established multinational corporation in the tobacco and consumer goods sector, suggesting this bridge facility supports strategic M&A activity.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
