# Investment Memorandum

## Executive Summary

**Date:** 2025-08-01

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Millrose Properties, Inc., a Maryland corporation, operates as the borrower under a credit facility with Goldman Sachs Bank USA serving as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** First Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated June 24, 2025, executed between Millrose Properties, Inc., Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of consenting lenders including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Bank of America N.A., Citibank N.A., Citizens Bank N.A., JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., and Wells Fargo Bank N.A.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment provides enhanced flexibility for future capital raising activities by introducing a "Designated Issuance" framework that allows the borrower to retain more proceeds from certain debt or equity issuances, while establishing additional fee revenue through a staged funding structure.

**Company Background:** Millrose Properties appears to be engaged in real estate operations based on the delayed draw term loan facility structure and references to property-related collateral, though specific business activities are not detailed in the amendment.

## Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• High-quality institutional lender syndicate demonstrates strong market confidence in the credit
• No existing defaults or events of default as confirmed by borrower representations
• Secured facility with collateral and security interests reaffirmed and maintained in full force
• Amendment includes standard compliance conditions and representation requirements
• Established relationship with original credit agreement dated less than two months prior

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Borrower-favorable amendment terms reduce mandatory prepayment obligations, potentially extending facility duration
• Limited financial and operational visibility due to amendment-only documentation
• Delayed draw structure with staged fees suggests development or construction risk profile
• Modified prepayment provisions may impact cash flow timing to lenders
