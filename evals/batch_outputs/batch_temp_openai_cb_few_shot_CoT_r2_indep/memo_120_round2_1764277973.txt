# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-09-02

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation, is a leading multinational tobacco and consumer products company.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to a 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement originally dated May 11, 2022, involves PMI as borrower, with Citibank Europe PLC (UK Branch) serving as Facility Agent, and a syndicate of 16 international lenders including Citibank N.A. Jersey Branch, Citicorp North America Inc., Bank of America N.A. London Branch, BBVA New York Branch, Banco Santander S.A., Barclays Bank PLC, Credit Suisse entities, Deutsche Bank AG Cayman Islands Branch, HSBC entities, Mizuho Bank Ltd., Standard Chartered Bank, Sumitomo Banking Corporation, Wells Fargo Bank N.A. London Branch, Bank of China (Europe) S.A., Commerzbank AG New York Branch, and MUFG Bank Ltd.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment modifies the "Minimum Acceptance Condition" definition related to an acquisition offer, establishing a threshold where acceptances from Target shareholders must exceed 50% of total issued and outstanding Target Shares (excluding treasury shares). The bridge facility appears to support PMI's acquisition activities, providing interim financing while the acquisition process proceeds.

**Company Background:** PMI operates as a major player in the global tobacco and consumer goods sector. The company's ability to secure a 364-day bridge facility from a prestigious international banking syndicate demonstrates its strong credit profile and established relationships within the global financial community.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• **Established investment-grade profile:** PMI represents a globally recognized multinational corporation with demonstrated ability to access capital markets and maintain banking relationships
• **High-quality banking syndicate:** 16-bank consortium includes premier international financial institutions across multiple jurisdictions (US, UK, Europe, Asia), indicating strong institutional confidence in the credit
• **Structured bridge financing:** 364-day facility provides appropriate interim financing structure for acquisition activities with clear timeline for permanent refinancing or repayment
• **Cooperative lender dynamics:** Unanimous lender participation in the amendment demonstrates effective syndicate management and aligned interests
• **Flexible acquisition terms:** Amendment provides borrower with reasonable acquisition execution flexibility through modified acceptance threshold

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• **Industry regulatory environment:** Global tobacco sector faces continuing regulatory pressures and litigation risks, though PMI has demonstrated resilience through product innovation and geographic diversification
• **Acquisition execution uncertainty:** Bridge financing inherently carries transaction completion and integration risks, mitigated by PMI's experienced management team and established M&A capabilities
• **Limited covenant visibility:** Amendment provides minimal detail on financial maintenance requirements or operational restrictions, though retention of original Credit Agreement terms suggests comprehensive covenant package
• **Short-term refinancing requirement:** 364-day maturity creates refinancing pressure, though mitigated by PMI's access to multiple funding sources and strong market position
