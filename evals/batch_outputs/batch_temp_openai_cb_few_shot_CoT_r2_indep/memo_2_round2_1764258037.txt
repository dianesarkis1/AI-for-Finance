# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. is a casino operator with Complex Services Inc. serving as guarantor under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019).

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver granted by Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent on behalf of Required Lenders. The waiver extends COVID-19 related relief from financial covenant breaches and closure defaults through March 31, 2021 (the "Extended Waiver Period").

**Purpose of Financing:** The financing accommodation addresses liquidity pressures from casino facility closures mandated by Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under regulatory oversight from OLG. The company has been significantly impacted by government-mandated closures lasting more than 60 consecutive days, which would constitute an Event of Default under the original credit agreement. OLG is providing certain financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during the closure period.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government Support: OLG is providing financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during closure
• Liquidity Requirements: Borrower must maintain minimum liquidity of $15,000,000 at all times, providing downside protection
• Weekly Monitoring: Required weekly liquidity reporting ensures close oversight of financial condition
• Regulated Industry: Casino operations are highly regulated, providing operational stability framework

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• COVID-19 Impact: Extended closure of casino facilities creates significant operational and financial stress
• Financial Covenant Breaches: Multiple breaches of financial covenants in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicate deteriorating financial performance
• Increased Pricing: Applicable margin moved to Level 5 (highest pricing tier)
• Restricted Operations: Prohibitions on new advances, distributions, acquisitions, and contract amendments limit financial flexibility
• Government Dependency: Critical dependence on continued OLG support payments creates concentration risk
