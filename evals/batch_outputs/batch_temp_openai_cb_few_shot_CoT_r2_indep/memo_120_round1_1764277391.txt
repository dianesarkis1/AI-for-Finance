## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-09-02

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Philip Morris International Inc. (PMI), a Virginia corporation and major multinational tobacco and consumer goods company.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to PMI's 364-Day Bridge Credit Agreement, executed between PMI and an international banking syndicate. Citibank Europe PLC (UK Branch) serves as Facility Agent, with participation from premier global lenders including Bank of America London Branch, Deutsche Bank, HSBC, Credit Suisse, Barclays, and others.

**Purpose of Financing:** Bridge financing facility supporting PMI's acquisition activities, with this amendment specifically modifying deal execution parameters.

**Company Background:** PMI is a globally recognized multinational corporation with established market positions in tobacco and consumer goods sectors, actively diversifying its product portfolio.
