# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-07-07

**Borrower / Company Overview:** ExchangeRight Income Fund Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower. The parent company is ExchangeRight Income Fund, d/b/a ExchangeRight Essential Income REIT, a Maryland real estate investment trust.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Incremental Revolving Commitment Assumption Agreement adds Renasant Bank as a new lender to an existing syndicated revolving credit facility originally dated May 30, 2024. The transaction increases the total facility size by $15,000,000 through an incremental revolving commitment from Renasant Bank, bringing the aggregate revolving commitments to $150,000,000. Wells Fargo Bank, National Association serves as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The incremental commitment provides additional revolving credit capacity to support the borrower's ongoing business operations and capital requirements. This represents the third incremental addition to the original credit facility.

**Company Background:** ExchangeRight operates as a real estate investment trust focused on income-generating properties. The organizational structure includes multiple subsidiary entities and Delaware statutory trusts (ExchangeRight Net Leased Portfolio 3-9 DST and ExchangeRight Essential Income Strategy Properties 6, LLC) that serve as loan parties, indicating a diversified real estate portfolio across various net-leased properties and investment strategies.
