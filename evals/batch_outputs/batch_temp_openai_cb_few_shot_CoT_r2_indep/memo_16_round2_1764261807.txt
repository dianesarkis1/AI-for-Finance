## 1. Executive Summary

**Date**: 2024-10-29

**Borrower**: MarineMax, Inc., a Florida corporation serving as Borrower Representative, is a prominent marine retail and services company operating through an extensive network of subsidiaries including boat dealerships, marinas, yacht brokerage services, and charter operations across the United States and internationally.

**Transaction**: Fourth Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated August 8, 2022. The amendment specifically modifies Section 2.01(a)(iii) to increase sublimits on floor plan loan advances across four distinct vessel categories, providing enhanced inventory financing capacity within the existing syndicated credit facility structure.

**Counterparties**: Manufacturers and Traders Trust Company acts as Administrative Agent, Swingline Lender, and Issuing Bank. Wells Fargo Commercial Distribution Finance, LLC serves as Floor Plan Agent. The syndicate includes major institutional lenders: Bank of America, PNC Bank, BMO Bank, The Huntington National Bank, Flagstar Bank, BankUnited, Raymond James Bank, Cadence Bank, Hancock Whitney Bank, Coastal States Bank, and NorthPoint Commercial Finance.

**Purpose**: Amendment increases floor plan financing sublimits to support expanded inventory management across vessels over 80 feet ($158.3M), foreign OEM units excluding Azimut and Galeon brands ($158.3M), used vessels ($158.3M), and high-value individual units exceeding $1M in value ($450M).

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• **Robust institutional syndicate**: Twelve-bank lending group with major financial institutions demonstrates strong market confidence and relationship depth
• **Clean credit profile**: Borrower representations confirm absence of defaults or events of default, indicating sound covenant compliance and financial management
