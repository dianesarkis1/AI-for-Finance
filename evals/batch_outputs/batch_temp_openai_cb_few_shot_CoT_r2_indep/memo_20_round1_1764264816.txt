## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-03-04

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP (formerly America First Multifamily Investors, L.P.) is a Delaware limited partnership focused on multifamily housing investments.

**Transaction Description:** Fifth Amendment to Credit Agreement originally dated June 11, 2021, executed among the Borrower, BankUnited N.A. as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of lenders including Bankers Trust Company and newly added NexBank.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates the addition of NexBank as a new lender through a Lender Joinder Agreement and restructures the existing lender commitment allocations within the credit facility.

**Company Background:** The borrower operates as a Delaware limited partnership in the multifamily housing investment sector.
