# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 15, 2023, F45 Training Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into a consent agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. (as Administrative Agent) and other lenders regarding their existing credit facility.
- The original Amended and Restated Credit Agreement was dated August 13, 2021, and has been subsequently amended four times (December 2021, May 2022, February 2023, and June 2023).
- The purpose of this consent is to extend the delivery date for F45's audited consolidated financial statements for fiscal year 2022 and accompanying Compliance Certificate from March 31, 2023 to September 30, 2023.
- F45 Training Holdings Inc. operates in the fitness training industry. The lending syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A. (as Administrative Agent, Australian Security Trustee, Swingline Lender and Issuing Bank) and Goldman Sachs Bank USA as lenders.
