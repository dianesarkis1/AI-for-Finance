# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-12-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serves as the Borrower in this syndicated credit facility with multiple lenders and Citibank, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** First Amendment to an existing First Lien Credit Agreement originally dated March 4, 2021, executed between Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc. and Citibank, N.A. (as Administrative Agent) along with several banks and financial institutions as lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The primary purpose of this amendment is to transition from LIBOR-based interest rates to SOFR-based rates (Secured Overnight Financing Rate). The amendment replaces USD LIBOR with Adjusted Term SOFR as the benchmark rate and addresses the discontinuation of various LIBOR rates after December 31, 2021.

**Company Background:** Petco Health and Wellness Company, Inc. maintains an established syndicated credit facility with institutional lenders, with Citibank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent.
