# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-01-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Square, Inc. serves as the Borrower under a revolving credit agreement with Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of major financial institutions as Lenders.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Amendment modifies Square's existing Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated May 1, 2020. The transaction involves Square, Inc. as Borrower, Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent, and multiple lenders including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment updates definitions related to COVID-19 relief programs and increases Square's permitted borrowing capacity under the Paycheck Protection Program Liquidity Facility (PPPLF) to support liquidity needs during the pandemic recovery period.

**Company Background:** Based on the credit agreement parties, Square, Inc. maintains banking relationships with premier financial institutions, indicating established market presence and creditworthiness sufficient to access institutional credit facilities.
