# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. serves as the Borrower with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor under a credit agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019).

**Transaction Description:** Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent granted a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver on behalf of the Required Lenders. This transaction represents an extension of COVID-19 related waivers due to government-mandated casino closures.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver extends relief from financial covenant breaches and closure defaults through March 31, 2021 (the "Extended Waiver Period") to address liquidity pressures from casino facility closures mandated by Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG) due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under regulatory oversight, with OLG providing certain financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during the closure period.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Government Support: OLG is providing financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during closure
• Liquidity Requirements: Borrower must maintain minimum liquidity of $15,000,000 at all times, providing downside protection
• Weekly Monitoring: Required weekly liquidity reporting ensures close oversight of financial condition
• Regulated Industry: Casino operations are highly regulated, providing operational stability framework

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• COVID-19 Impact: Extended closure of casino facilities creates significant operational and financial stress
• Financial Covenant Breaches: Multiple breaches of financial covenants in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicate deteriorating financial performance
• Increased Pricing: Applicable margin moved to Level 5 (highest pricing tier)
• Restricted Operations: Prohibitions on new advances, distributions, acquisitions, and contract amendments limit financial flexibility
• Government Dependency: Critical dependence on continued OLG support payments creates concentration risk
