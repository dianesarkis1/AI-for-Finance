# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On March 25, 2021, Crestwood Midstream Partners LP, a Delaware limited partnership in the midstream energy sector, entered into a Third Amendment to its Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated October 16, 2018.
- The borrower is supported by Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as Administrative Agent, leading a substantial syndicate including Bank of America, Barclays Bank PLC, Citibank, JPMorgan Chase, Royal Bank of Canada, Truist Bank, and numerous other major financial institutions.
- The amendment facilitates the "Third Amendment Transactions," whereby Crestwood Equity Partners and third-party investors will acquire equity interests and assets of Crestwood Holdings LLC and its subsidiaries through unit purchases and general partnership interest acquisitions.
- The financing purpose includes enabling Crestwood Equity Partners to acquire CEQP Units and GP Units, with the borrower authorized to make distributions up to $275.0 million (including proceeds from revolving facility borrowings) to support these transactions.
- Crestwood Midstream Partners LP operates in the midstream energy sector with extensive subsidiary guarantor coverage across pipeline, crude services, gas processing, and transportation operations.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Exceptionally strong and diverse lender syndicate featuring 23+ major financial institutions, indicating robust market confidence and relationship strength
- Comprehensive subsidiary guarantor structure with numerous operating entities across the midstream value chain providing extensive credit support
- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default are continuing, suggesting current covenant compliance
- Amendment includes specific carve-outs and provisions that appear measured and transaction-specific rather than broad covenant relaxation

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Complex ownership restructuring involving third-party investors may introduce new governance dynamics and potential control considerations
- Significant dividend capacity of $275 million could impact cash available for debt service, though this appears to be transaction-specific
- Change in control provisions have been modified, potentially affecting lender protections in future ownership transitions
- Limited visibility into underlying financial performance metrics, pricing terms, or specific financial maintenance covenants from this amendment document
