# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America

**Transaction:** This is a consent to credit agreement modification between Oil-Dri Corporation of America (borrower) and BMO Harris Bank N.A. (lender). The transaction modifies definitions in an existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006.

**Purpose:** The financing modification accommodates a one-time non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to Oil-Dri's Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment, taken during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates with multiple domestic subsidiaries serving as guarantors, including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Long-standing banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank since 2006 demonstrates institutional confidence
• Proactive covenant management through formal modification process
• No existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement
• Comprehensive guarantee structure across multiple subsidiary entities
• Company maintains active financial reporting and compliance monitoring

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• $5.644 million goodwill impairment indicates underperformance in Retail and Wholesale segment
• Covenant modification requirement suggests potential stress on financial maintenance ratios
• Limited financial transparency beyond covenant compliance metrics
