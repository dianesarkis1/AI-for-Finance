# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-11-30

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. is a casino operator that entered into a credit agreement dated June 10, 2019 (amended July 17, 2019) with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent and Complex Services Inc. as guarantor.

**Transaction Description:** This Fifth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver extends COVID-19 related relief from financial covenant breaches and closure defaults through March 31, 2021. The waiver is granted by Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent on behalf of Required Lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** The accommodation addresses liquidity pressures from government-mandated casino facility closures due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions. The waiver provides relief from financial covenant breaches in Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 of the underlying Credit Agreement and extends various compliance certificate delivery deferrals.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities under regulatory oversight by Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG). The company has been significantly impacted by COVID-19 related closures lasting more than 60 consecutive days, which would constitute an Event of Default under normal circumstances. OLG is providing financial relief through threshold payment relief and fixed component per diem payments during the closure period.
