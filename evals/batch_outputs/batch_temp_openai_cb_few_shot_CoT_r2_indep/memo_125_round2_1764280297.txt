**Borrower/Company Overview:** Lilis Energy Inc. is a Nevada-based energy company operating under Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection with multiple operating subsidiaries.

**Transaction Description:** Third amendment to a Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement dated June 30, 2020. The syndicated facility is led by BMO Harris Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent, with Capital One National Association and Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch as lenders.
