## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-01-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Fathom Manufacturing, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the Borrower under a credit facility with Fathom Guarantor, LLC as Holdings. The company is based in Hartland, Wisconsin.

**Transaction Description:** Extension consent letter executed between Fathom Manufacturing, LLC and its lending syndicate to modify certain covenant deadlines under the existing Credit Agreement dated December 23, 2021. The lending syndicate includes JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and Lender, along with CIBC Bank USA, BMO Bank N.A., and Stifel Bank & Trust as Lenders.

**Purpose of Financing:** To provide additional time for compliance with two key obligations: (1) executing a Binding Agreement involving the Ultimate Parent, and (2) completing a mandatory term loan prepayment of $50,000,000 to Third Amendment Consenting Term Lenders.

**Company Background:** Limited information available from the extension consent letter. The company operates under a credit facility structure with institutional lender support and maintains operations in Wisconsin.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender syndicate including JPMorgan Chase as Administrative Agent, CIBC, BMO, and Stifel Bank & Trust
• Majority Third Amendment Consenting Term Lenders agreed to the extension, demonstrating continued lender support and collaborative covenant management
• Limited scope extension with specific parameters maintains disciplined approach to covenant relief
• All other loan document terms remain in full force and effect, preserving existing lender protections

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**

• Substantial $50,000,000 mandatory prepayment requirement suggests significant deleveraging obligation
• Extension is narrowly defined and explicitly does not establish precedent for future modifications or create custom course of dealing
• Breach of extension terms would constitute an Event of Default under the Credit Agreement, maintaining strong lender enforcement rights
