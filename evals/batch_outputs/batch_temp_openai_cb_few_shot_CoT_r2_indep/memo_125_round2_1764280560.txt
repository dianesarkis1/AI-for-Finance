# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-08-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Lilis Energy Inc., a Nevada corporation operating in the energy sector, currently under Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection.

**Transaction Description:** Third amendment to a Senior Secured Super-Priority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement originally executed on June 30, 2020. The transaction involves BMO Harris Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent, with a lending syndicate comprising Capital One National Association and Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch.

**Purpose of Financing:** Extension of Chapter 11 milestone deadlines to provide additional time for bankruptcy restructuring proceedings, specifically extending a critical deadline from August 28, 2020 to September 3, 2020.

**Company Background:** Lilis Energy Inc. operates through multiple subsidiary entities including Brushy Resources Inc., Hurricane Resources LLC, Impetro Operating LLC, Lilis Operating Company LLC, and Impetro Resources LLC, all serving as guarantors under the facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
