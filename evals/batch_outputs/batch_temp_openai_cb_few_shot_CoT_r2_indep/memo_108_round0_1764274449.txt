# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On December 19, 2024, Box, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) and Wells Fargo Bank, National Association entered into Amendment No. 1 to their Amended and Restated Credit Agreement originally dated June 30, 2023.
- Box, Inc. serves as the Borrower in this bilateral credit facility with Wells Fargo Bank as the sole Lender.
- The amendment modifies key definitions in the credit agreement, most notably adjusting the "Commitment" amount to $75,000,000 and revising the "Liquidity Condition" to require that the Borrower's liquidity be greater than or equal to the outstanding principal amount of existing convertible notes.
- The purpose of this amendment is to update certain covenant definitions and maintain the credit facility structure between Box, Inc. and Wells Fargo Bank.
- Box, Inc. is a technology company that operates in the cloud content management and file sharing sector.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong banking relationship with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association, a major U.S. financial institution
- Borrower representations confirm no Event of Default or Potential Event of Default has occurred and is continuing
- All Security Documents, Loan Documents, guarantees, pledges, grants, and security interests remain in full force and effect
- Amendment maintains existing collateral structure and lien priority

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Bilateral facility structure concentrates credit exposure with a single lender
- Modified "Liquidity Condition" ties covenant compliance to outstanding convertible notes, creating potential complexity
- Limited visibility into pricing, maturity, and other key credit terms from this amendment document alone
