# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-02-20

**Borrower / Company Overview:** CrossAmerica Partners LP is a Delaware limited partnership operating in the fuel distribution and convenience store sector. Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that serves as a co-borrower alongside the Partnership.

**Transaction Description:** This is a first amendment to an amended and restated credit agreement between the borrowers and a syndicate of lenders led by Citizens Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent. The original credit agreement was dated April 1, 2019, and was previously amended and restated on March 31, 2023.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates the acquisition of assets from Applegreen under the Applegreen Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 26, 2024, involving purchases from Applegreen Midwest LLC and Applegreen Florida, LLC by CAPL Retail LLC.

**Company Background:** CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel wholesale and retail convenience store business, with multiple subsidiary entities providing guarantees under the credit facility. The partnership structure includes CrossAmerica GP LLC as the general partner of CrossAmerica Partners LP.
