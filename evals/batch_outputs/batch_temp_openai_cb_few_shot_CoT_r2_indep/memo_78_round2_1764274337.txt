# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Saia Inc., a Delaware corporation, operating through multiple subsidiaries including Saia Motor Freight Line, LLC, Saia Sales, LLC, Saia TL Plus, LLC, LinkEx, Inc., and Saia Logistics, LLC.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Credit Agreement between Saia Inc. as Borrower and a banking syndicate led by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent. The amendment modifies the original Credit Agreement dated February 3, 2023.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment revises Section 6.09 (Restrictive Agreements) to provide the borrower with enhanced flexibility regarding future unsecured debt arrangements while maintaining lender protections.

**Company Background:** Saia Inc. operates as a transportation and logistics company with multiple operating subsidiaries serving as guarantors under the credit facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender support from major U.S. banks including JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., Bank of America, N.A., PNC Bank, TD Bank, N.A., and BOKF, NA
• Borrower confirms no Default or Event of Default exists and all representations and warranties remain true and correct
• Comprehensive guarantee structure maintained across all subsidiary Loan Parties
• Amendment preserves lender protections through mandatory pari passu security arrangements for future unsecured debt

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Covenant modification provides increased borrower flexibility that could potentially allow for additional leverage
• Risk mitigated by requirement that future unsecured debt restrictions cannot be materially more restrictive than current Agreement terms
• Lender security interests protected through mandatory equal and ratable treatment provisions in any future unsecured debt arrangements
