## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2025-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** The borrowers comprise five entertainment library financing entities: LGTV Library Financing LLC, Artisan Library Financing LLC, Summit Library Financing LLC, LG Film Library Financing LLC, and LGIM Library UK Financing Limited, each supported by corresponding parent holding companies.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 2 to an Amended and Restated Credit, Security and Pledge Agreement among the borrowers, their parent companies, a consortium of thirteen lenders, and Fifth Third Bank as Administrative Agent. The amendment includes facility modifications and an upsize provision.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates changes to the existing credit agreement terms and implements a facility upsize, with new loan allocation schedules among the lender group.

**Company Background:** The borrowing entities operate as special purpose vehicles in the entertainment content library financing sector, structured with separate parent holding companies providing additional credit support through guarantor arrangements.
