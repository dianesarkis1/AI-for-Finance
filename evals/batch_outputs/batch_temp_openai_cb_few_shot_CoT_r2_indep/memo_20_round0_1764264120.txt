# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On March 4, 2024, Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP (formerly America First Multifamily Investors, L.P.) entered into a Fifth Amendment to their Credit Agreement with BankUnited N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated June 11, 2021, and has been previously amended four times (November 2021, June 2023, July 2023, and September 2023).
- The amendment facilitates the addition of NexBank as a new lender through a Lender Joinder Agreement and updates the lender commitment structure. The total facility commitment is $50,000,000 with BankUnited maintaining the largest share at 60% ($30MM), while Bankers Trust Company and NexBank each hold 20% ($10MM each).
- Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP is a Delaware limited partnership focused on multifamily housing investments. The amendment includes a comprehensive release provision where the borrower waives all claims against lenders and reaffirms all existing loan document obligations.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong banking relationships with established regional and national lenders including BankUnited N.A. and Bankers Trust Company
- Successful addition of a new lender (NexBank) demonstrates continued lender confidence and diversification of funding sources
- Borrower representations confirm no existing defaults or events of default, indicating current compliance with all covenants
- Comprehensive reaffirmation of all security interests, liens, and guarantees maintains strong collateral protection
- Multiple previous amendments suggest an active and cooperative borrower-lender relationship
