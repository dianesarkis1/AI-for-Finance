# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 6, 2022, Oil-Dri Corporation of America entered into a consent agreement with BMO Harris Bank N.A. to modify their existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006.
- Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower, with multiple domestic subsidiaries acting as guarantors including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company.
- The transaction is a covenant modification consent that allows Oil-Dri to add back a one-time non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to their Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment to both Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations.
- The purpose is to accommodate a significant goodwill impairment charge taken during the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022, preventing potential covenant violations under the existing credit agreement's financial maintenance requirements.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Long-standing banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating back to 2006, indicating stability and lender confidence
- Proactive covenant management demonstrates responsible financial stewardship by addressing potential covenant issues before they become defaults
- Company representation that no current default or event of default exists under the credit agreement
- Strong subsidiary guarantee structure with multiple operating entities providing additional credit support

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- The $5.644 million goodwill impairment suggests underperformance in the Retail and Wholesale segment, potentially indicating operational challenges
- Covenant modification indicates the company was at risk of breaching financial maintenance requirements without this accommodation
- Limited visibility into overall financial performance, leverage ratios, or broader credit profile beyond the specific covenant modification
