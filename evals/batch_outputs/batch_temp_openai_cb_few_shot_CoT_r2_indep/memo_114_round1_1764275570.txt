# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MSCC Funding I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit and security agreement. Main Street Capital Corporation, a Maryland corporation, acts as the sole member and collateral manager of the borrower entity.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction involves EverBank N.A. joining as an additional lender to an existing syndicated revolving credit facility originally established on November 22, 2022. The lending syndicate includes Truist Bank as administrative agent and swingline lender, Citibank N.A. as collateral agent/custodian/document custodian, and Virtus Group LP as collateral administrator.

**Purpose of Financing:** The joinder expands the existing revolving credit facility by adding EverBank N.A. as a new lender with a $75,000,000 commitment, increasing total available lending capacity for the borrower's operations.

**Company Background:** Main Street Capital Corporation operates as a business development company utilizing this structured lending facility through its subsidiary MSCC Funding I, LLC. The facility requires participants to maintain qualified purchaser and qualified institutional buyer status, indicating sophisticated institutional investment activities.

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
