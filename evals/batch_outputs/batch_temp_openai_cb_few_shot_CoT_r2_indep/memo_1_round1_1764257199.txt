# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-09-30

**Borrower / Company Overview:** MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc. operates casino facilities and serves as the Borrower, with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor under a Credit Agreement originally dated June 10, 2019 with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent.

**Transaction Description:** This Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver extends COVID-19 related covenant relief through November 30, 2020, representing the fourth iteration of pandemic-related waivers since May 2020. The waiver addresses operational disruptions caused by government-mandated casino facility closures.

**Purpose of Financing:** The waiver provides temporary relief from financial covenant breaches and closure defaults, allowing the borrower to maintain credit facility access during the COVID-19 pandemic while casino operations remain suspended.

**Company Background:** MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure by OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming) due to COVID-19 pandemic restrictions, causing significant operational and financial impact requiring ongoing covenant relief.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• OLG support through financial relief measures including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA agreement
• Borrower maintains minimum liquidity requirement of CAD 15,000,000 throughout the waiver period
• Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
• Government-regulated casino operations with established gaming authority relationships

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extended operational closure creating fundamental cash flow disruption requiring multiple waiver extensions since May 2020
• Financial covenant breaches under Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicating deteriorated financial performance
• Pricing moved to Level 5 (highest cost level) reflecting increased credit risk
• Restricted credit availability as borrower cannot request advances under either credit facility during waiver period
• Regulatory dependency risk as event of default triggered if OLG ceases relief payments for more than 5 business days
