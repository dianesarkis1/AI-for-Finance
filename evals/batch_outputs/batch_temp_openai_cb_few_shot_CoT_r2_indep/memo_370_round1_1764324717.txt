# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2024-11-26

**Borrower:** IPC Alternative Real Estate Operating Partnership, LP, a Delaware limited partnership engaged in real estate operations with headquarters in Oak Brook, Illinois.

**Transaction Overview:** Modification to existing revolving credit facility between affiliated entities, extending the maturity date by one year. The original $22,500,000 revolving credit facility was established October 27, 2023, between the Borrower and Inland Private Capital Corporation (Lender).

**Purpose:** Extension of facility maturity from November 30, 2024 to November 30, 2025, providing continued liquidity for real estate operations.

**Company Background:** The Borrower operates as a real estate investment partnership within the Inland Private Capital organization, sharing corporate headquarters and management structure with the affiliated Lender.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Borrower confirms compliance with all existing loan covenants and financial maintenance terms
• Organized as Delaware limited partnership in good standing with adequate operational powers
• Management reaffirms all representations and warranties as of modification date

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Affiliate lending arrangement may lack market-rate pricing and arm's-length negotiation

• Broad borrower release provisions limit future recourse against lender
• Limited visibility into underlying real estate portfolio performance and financial metrics
