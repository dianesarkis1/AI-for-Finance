# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-06-10

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Affiliated Managers Group, Inc. (AMG), a Delaware corporation, serves as the borrower under this credit facility arrangement.

**Transaction Description:** This transaction represents a Second Amendment to a Third Amended and Restated Term Credit Agreement originally dated January 18, 2019. The counterparties include AMG as borrower, multiple bank and financial institution lenders, and Bank of America, N.A. as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically modifies the Applicable Margin pricing structure contained in Annex I to the original Credit Agreement, establishing a new rating-based pricing grid that adjusts borrowing costs based on debt ratings from S&P, Moody's, and Fitch.

**Company Background:** AMG operates under a term credit facility with institutional lenders. The pricing amendment establishes a comprehensive rating-based margin structure accommodating investment-grade credit profiles from A+/A1/A+ down to BBB/Baa2/BBB levels, indicating the company maintains investment-grade creditworthiness across multiple rating agencies.
