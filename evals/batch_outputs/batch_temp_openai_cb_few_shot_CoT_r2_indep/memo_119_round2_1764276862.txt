## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-04-14

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Apollo Management Holdings, L.P., a Delaware limited partnership, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit facility. Apollo operates through a sophisticated financial structure encompassing multiple investment management and asset management entities.

**Transaction Description:** This Guarantor Joinder Agreement adds Apollo Global Management, Inc. and Apollo Asset Management, Inc. as new guarantors to an existing credit facility originally established on October 12, 2022. The facility provides revolving loans and letters of credit, with Citibank, N.A. serving as Administrative Agent. The transaction expands the guarantee support base beyond the thirteen Initial Guarantors already party to the credit agreement.

**Purpose of Financing:** The new guarantors are joining to induce lenders to maintain existing commitments and potentially extend additional loans, while encouraging issuing banks to maintain current letters of credit and issue additional ones. This action also provides consideration for previously extended loans and issued letters of credit.

**Company Background:** Apollo's credit structure involves extensive cross-border operations with Initial Guarantors spanning multiple jurisdictions including twelve Cayman Islands limited partnerships, one Anguilla limited liability company, and one additional Cayman Islands partnership. The addition of these U.S.-based operating entities enhances the domestic guarantee profile.
