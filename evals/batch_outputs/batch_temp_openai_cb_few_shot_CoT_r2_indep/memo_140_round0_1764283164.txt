# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On January 28, 2021, Square, Inc. entered into a Third Amendment to its Revolving Credit Agreement originally dated May 1, 2020.
- Square, Inc. serves as the Borrower in this credit facility, with Goldman Sachs Bank USA acting as Administrative Agent. The lending syndicate includes major financial institutions: Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association.
- The amendment updates definitions related to COVID-19 relief programs including the CARES Act, Paycheck Protection Program Liquidity Facility (PPPLF), PPP Loans, and Small Business Act to reflect legislative changes through the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021.
- The primary purpose is to increase the permitted "Specified Indebtedness" that Square can incur pursuant to the PPPLF from an unspecified amount to $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount outstanding at any time.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

• The borrower represents that no Default or Event of Default has occurred and is continuing, demonstrating current compliance with all credit agreement terms.

• Amendment provides Square with enhanced liquidity access through increased PPPLF borrowing capacity, supporting financial flexibility during COVID-19 recovery period.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
• The amendment allows Square to significantly increase its indebtedness exposure through PPPLF borrowings up to $1 billion, which could impact overall leverage profile.

• Limited visibility into underlying credit facility terms such as pricing, total commitment size, maturity, and other financial covenants constrains comprehensive credit assessment.
