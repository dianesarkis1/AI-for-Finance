## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-03-04

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP (formerly America First Multifamily Investors, L.P.) is a Delaware limited partnership focused on multifamily housing investments.

**Transaction Description:** Fifth Amendment to Credit Agreement between Greystone Housing Impact Investors LP as borrower, BankUnited N.A. as Administrative Agent, and a syndicate of lenders including Bankers Trust Company and NexBank.
