# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On April 25, 2024, Accuray Incorporated (a Delaware corporation) entered into a Third Amendment to its Credit Agreement with Silicon Valley Bank (now a division of First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company) as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders including HSBC Bank US.
- The original Credit Agreement was dated May 6, 2021, and had been previously amended in October 2022 and November 2023.
- This amendment primarily modifies the definition of "Consolidated EBITDA" to allow additional addbacks related to restructuring charges and cost savings from a "Specified Headcount Reduction" that occurred after September 30, 2023 and on or prior to April 25, 2024.
- Accuray Incorporated appears to be seeking covenant relief through enhanced EBITDA calculation flexibility, particularly related to workforce reductions and associated cost savings.
- The borrower paid an amendment fee of 0.05% of outstanding revolving commitments and term loan principal amounts to consenting lenders.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Established banking relationship with reputable financial institutions including First-Citizens Bank & Trust Company (Silicon Valley Bank division) and HSBC Bank US
- Amendment includes comprehensive release of claims provision, suggesting cooperative lender relationship
- Borrower remains in compliance with existing covenants as confirmed by representations and warranties
- Amendment provides specific, time-limited relief rather than permanent covenant modifications

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- EBITDA definition modifications are borrower-favorable and may mask underlying operational challenges, particularly the need for significant workforce reductions
- Specific addback limits suggest the borrower may be experiencing financial stress requiring covenant relief (up to $2.6M in restructuring charges and $6.8M in run-rate cost savings for certain periods)
- Limited visibility into overall credit facility size, pricing, or maturity terms constrains comprehensive credit assessment
- The amendment represents the third modification since 2021, indicating ongoing need for covenant adjustments
