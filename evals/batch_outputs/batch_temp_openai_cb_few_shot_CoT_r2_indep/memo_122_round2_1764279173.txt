# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2020-12-28

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation is a Delaware corporation operating as a business development company focused on specialty lending activities. The transaction includes subsidiary guarantors OCSL SRNE, LLC (a Delaware limited liability company) and FSFC Holdings, Inc. (a Delaware corporation).

**Transaction Description:** This incremental commitment agreement provides additional borrowing capacity under the Amended and Restated Senior Secured Revolving Credit Agreement dated February 25, 2019. The transaction increases MUFG Union Bank's commitment by $25,000,000, expanding the existing credit facility. ING Capital LLC serves as Administrative Agent and Issuing Bank, with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. and Bank of America, N.A. as Syndication Agents, and joint lead arrangers comprising ING Capital LLC, JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., and Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated.

**Purpose of Financing:** The incremental commitment provides $25,000,000 of additional borrowing capacity, increasing MUFG Union Bank's total commitment from $50,000,000 to $75,000,000 under the existing revolving credit facility to support the borrower's ongoing specialty lending operations.

**Company Background:** Oaktree Specialty Lending Corporation operates under the Oaktree Capital Management platform, providing specialty lending solutions in the credit markets.
