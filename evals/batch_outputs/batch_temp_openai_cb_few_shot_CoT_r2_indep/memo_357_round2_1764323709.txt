# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2024-08-26

**Borrower:** Lumen Technologies, Inc., a Louisiana corporation operating in the telecommunications sector.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to the Superpriority Term B Credit Agreement originally dated March 22, 2024. This technical modification involves Lumen Technologies, Inc. as borrower, with Wilmington Trust, National Association serving as Administrative Agent and Bank of America, N.A. as Collateral Agent.

**Purpose:** To correct a technical error in Section 6.01(v)(1)(ii) of the original Credit Agreement relating to Incremental Equivalent Debt provisions. This amendment addresses documentation inconsistencies as permitted under the original agreement's technical modification provisions.

**Company Background:** Lumen Technologies, Inc. is a Louisiana-based telecommunications corporation with an existing superpriority credit facility supported by Lumen Guarantors and QC Guarantors.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established institutional counterparties provide operational stability (Wilmington Trust, Bank of America)
• Proactive documentation maintenance demonstrates strong legal and operational oversight
• Robust structural protections govern additional debt incurrence, including subordination and collateral restrictions
• Multiple guarantee layers enhance credit protection through Lumen and QC Guarantors

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Amendment enables potential future leverage expansion through Incremental Equivalent Debt mechanisms
• Limited visibility into current facility performance, utilization levels, or borrower financial metrics
• Technical correction requirement may signal broader documentation complexity or ongoing structural refinements
