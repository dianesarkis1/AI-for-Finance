# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2024-02-20

**Borrower / Company Overview:** CrossAmerica Partners LP, a Delaware limited partnership, and Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc., a Delaware corporation, serve as co-borrowers. CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel wholesale and retail convenience store business through an extensive network of subsidiaries including retail, wholesale, and property management entities.

**Transaction Description:** First amendment to an amended and restated credit agreement with Citizens Bank, N.A. serving as Administrative and Collateral Agent, alongside a syndicate of twelve additional lenders. The amendment specifically modifies EBITDA covenant definitions to accommodate acquisition-related expenses.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment facilitates the acquisition of assets under the Applegreen Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 26, 2024, whereby CAPL Retail LLC acquires assets from Applegreen Midwest LLC and Applegreen Florida, LLC. The modification excludes lease termination fees payable under this purchase agreement from existing EBITDA covenant limitations.

**Company Background:** CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel distribution and convenience store sector with a diversified portfolio spanning wholesale fuel distribution, retail operations, and real estate holdings. The credit relationship originated in April 2019 and was previously amended and restated in March 2023, demonstrating sustained institutional support.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

• Limited financial transparency as amendment documents do not disclose facility size, pricing terms, maturity profile, or other financial maintenance covenants, constraining comprehensive credit risk assessment
