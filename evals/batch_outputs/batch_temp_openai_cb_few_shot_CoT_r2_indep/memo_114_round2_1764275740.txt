# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2023-10-12

**Borrower/Company Overview:** MSCC Funding I, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the borrower under a revolving credit and security agreement. Main Street Capital Corporation, a Maryland corporation, acts as the sole member and collateral manager.

**Transaction Description:** EverBank N.A. is joining as an additional lender to an existing syndicated revolving credit facility originally established on November 22, 2022. The current lending syndicate includes Truist Bank as administrative agent and swingline lender, Citibank N.A. as collateral agent/custodian/document custodian, and Virtus Group LP as collateral administrator.

**Purpose of Financing:** The lender joinder adds EverBank N.A. with a $75,000,000 commitment, expanding the total available capacity under the existing revolving credit facility for the borrower's ongoing operations.

**Company Background:** Main Street Capital Corporation operates as a business development company utilizing this structured lending facility through its subsidiary MSCC Funding I, LLC. The facility requires lenders to maintain qualified purchaser and qualified institutional buyer status.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

• Regulatory compliance framework ensures sophisticated institutional participation

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
