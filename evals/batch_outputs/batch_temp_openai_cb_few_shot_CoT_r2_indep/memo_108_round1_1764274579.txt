## 1. Executive Summary

**Date:** 2024-12-19

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Box, Inc., a Delaware corporation, operates as a technology company in the cloud content management and file sharing sector.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Amended and Restated Credit Agreement between Box, Inc. (Borrower) and Wells Fargo Bank, National Association (Lender). This bilateral credit facility amendment modifies the original Amended and Restated Credit Agreement dated June 30, 2023.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment updates key definitions within the existing credit agreement, specifically adjusting the commitment amount and revising liquidity covenant requirements to maintain the credit facility structure.
