# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-01-28

**Borrower / Company Overview:** Square, Inc. serves as the Borrower under a revolving credit facility with Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of major financial institutions as lenders.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Amendment modifies Square's existing Revolving Credit Agreement originally executed May 1, 2020, and previously amended twice (May 28, 2020 and November 9, 2020). The transaction involves Square, Inc., Goldman Sachs Bank USA as Administrative Agent, and a lending syndicate including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment serves two primary purposes: (1) updating definitions related to COVID-19 relief legislation to reflect changes through the Consolidated Appropriations Act, 2021, including the CARES Act, Paycheck Protection Program Liquidity Facility (PPPLF), PPP Loans, and Small Business Act; and (2) increasing Square's permitted borrowing capacity under the PPPLF to $1,000,000,000 in aggregate principal amount outstanding.

**Company Background:** Square, Inc. operates as a financial services and digital payments company. The company maintains credit relationships with premier financial institutions and has demonstrated ability to access capital markets through multiple credit facility amendments during the COVID-19 pandemic period.
