# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-01-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Square, Inc. is the borrower under a revolving credit agreement with a syndicate of major financial institutions.

**Transaction Description:** This Third Amendment to Square, Inc.'s Revolving Credit Agreement (originally dated May 1, 2020) involves Square, Inc. as Borrower, multiple Lenders including Goldman Sachs Bank USA, Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association, with Goldman Sachs Bank USA serving as Administrative Agent.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment enables Square to access increased liquidity through the Federal Reserve's Paycheck Protection Program Liquidity Facility (PPPLF) by raising the permitted borrowing limit to $1 billion aggregate principal amount outstanding.

**Company Background:** Square, Inc. operates under a revolving credit facility that has been previously amended twice (May 28, 2020 and November 9, 2020), indicating an actively managed credit relationship with ongoing modifications to accommodate business needs.

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
