# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On September 30, 2020, Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent granted a Fourth Amended and Restated Limited Waiver to MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., extending COVID-19 related covenant relief.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment Inc., a casino operator, serves as the Borrower with Complex Services Inc. as guarantor. The original Credit Agreement was dated June 10, 2019 with Bank of Montreal as Administrative Agent.
- This waiver extends relief from financial covenant breaches and closure defaults through November 30, 2020 (the "Extended Waiver Period"), representing the fourth iteration of COVID-19 related waivers since May 2020.
- The financing relief addresses operational disruptions caused by government-mandated casino facility closures due to the COVID-19 pandemic, with OLG (Ontario Lottery and Gaming) providing certain financial relief under the COSA agreement.
- MGE Niagara Entertainment operates casino facilities that were subject to temporary closure by OLG in accordance with applicable law during the pandemic, causing significant operational and financial impact.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- OLG support through financial relief measures including threshold payment relief and Fixed Component Per Diem payments under the COSA agreement
- Borrower maintains minimum liquidity requirement of CAD 15,000,000 throughout the waiver period
- Weekly liquidity reporting provides enhanced monitoring and transparency
- Government-regulated casino operations with essential gaming authority relationships

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Extended operational closure creating fundamental cash flow disruption requiring multiple waiver extensions since May 2020
- Financial covenant breaches under Sections 9.4.1 and 9.4.2 indicating deteriorated financial performance
- Pricing moved to Level 5 (highest cost level) with applicable margin of 5.00% for Bankers' Acceptances/Letters of Credit/LIBOR Loans
- Restricted credit availability as borrower cannot request advances under either credit facility during waiver period
- Regulatory dependency risk as event of default triggered if OLG ceases relief payments for more than 5 business days
