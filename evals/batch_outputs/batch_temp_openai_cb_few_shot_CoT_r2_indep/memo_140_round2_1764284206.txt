# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2021-01-28

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Square, Inc. serves as the borrower under a revolving credit agreement with a syndicate of institutional lenders.

**Transaction Description:** This is the Third Amendment to Square, Inc.'s Revolving Credit Agreement, originally executed on May 1, 2020. The transaction involves Square, Inc. as Borrower and a lending syndicate including Goldman Sachs Bank USA (serving as both Administrative Agent and Lender), Sumitomo Mitsui Banking Corporation, JPMorgan Chase Bank N.A., Wells Fargo Bank N.A., HSBC Bank USA National Association, and U.S. Bank National Association.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment specifically modifies the credit agreement to permit Square to access the Federal Reserve's Paycheck Protection Program Liquidity Facility (PPPLF), increasing the allowable aggregate principal amount for PPPLF borrowings from an unspecified previous limit to $1,000,000,000 outstanding at any time.

**Company Background:** Square, Inc. maintains an established banking relationship with multiple amendments to its credit facility (First Amendment: May 28, 2020; Second Amendment: November 9, 2020), demonstrating ongoing credit optimization and adaptation to market conditions.
