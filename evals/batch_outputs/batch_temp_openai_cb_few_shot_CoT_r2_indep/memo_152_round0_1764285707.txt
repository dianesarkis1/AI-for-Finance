# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In February 2024, CrossAmerica Partners LP and Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc. executed a first amendment to their amended and restated credit agreement with Citizens Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent and a syndicate of lenders.
- CrossAmerica Partners LP is a Delaware limited partnership operating in the fuel distribution and convenience store sector. Lehigh Gas Wholesale Services, Inc. is a Delaware corporation that serves as a co-borrower alongside the Partnership.
- This amendment facilitates the acquisition of assets from Applegreen under the Applegreen Asset Purchase Agreement dated January 26, 2024, involving purchases from Applegreen Midwest LLC and Applegreen Florida, LLC by CAPL Retail LLC.
- The amendment modifies the "Consolidated EBITDA" definition to exclude lease termination fees payable under the Applegreen Purchase Agreement from the existing $15,000,000 limitation on early termination fees in any four-quarter fiscal period.
- CrossAmerica Partners operates in the fuel wholesale and retail convenience store business, with multiple subsidiary entities providing guarantees under the credit facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
- Strong, diversified lender syndicate including major financial institutions (Citizens Bank, Bank of America, JPMorgan Chase, TD Bank, Wells Fargo, Truist Bank, and others), demonstrating broad lender confidence
- Strategic acquisition expansion through the Applegreen transaction, potentially enhancing market presence and revenue diversification
- Comprehensive guarantee structure with multiple subsidiary guarantors providing credit enhancement
- Established credit relationship dating back to April 2019, indicating stable long-term banking partnerships

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Amendment provides borrower-friendly covenant relief by excluding acquisition-related lease termination costs from EBITDA limitations, potentially reducing financial flexibility metrics
- Acquisition integration risks associated with the Applegreen asset purchase, including operational and financial integration challenges
- Limited visibility into overall credit terms, pricing, and other financial maintenance covenants constrains comprehensive risk assessment
