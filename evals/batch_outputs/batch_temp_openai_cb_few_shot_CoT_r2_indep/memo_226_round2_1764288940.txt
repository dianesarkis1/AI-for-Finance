# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-03-31

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Vertical Horizons, Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company, serving as the financing vehicle for Frontier Airlines' aircraft acquisition program.

**Transaction Description:** Amendment No. 1 to Seventh Amended and Restated Credit Agreement involving PDP (Pre-Delivery Payment) financing for 59 Airbus aircraft (9 Airbus A320neo and 50 Airbus A321neo). The transaction structure includes Citibank N.A. as both Facility Agent and Arranger, with a syndicate of institutional lenders and Bank of Utah as Security Trustee.

**Purpose of Financing:** The amendment extends certain compliance deadlines from July 1, 2022 to April 1, 2023, and updates references to amended Assignment and Assumption Agreement and Step-In Agreement with Airbus to facilitate the aircraft acquisition program.

**Company Background:** The financing supports Frontier Airlines' fleet modernization through the acquisition of fuel-efficient Airbus neo aircraft, with credit enhancement provided through guarantees from Frontier Group Holdings, Inc., Frontier Airlines Holdings, Inc., and Frontier Airlines, Inc.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Strong institutional lender syndicate including Citibank N.A., Barclays Bank PLC, Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding
• Multiple levels of credit support through corporate guarantees from Frontier Group Holdings and its subsidiaries
• Collateral secured by modern, fuel-efficient Airbus A320neo and A321neo aircraft
• Borrower maintains compliance with LTV (Loan-to-Value) test requirements
• Frontier Group Holdings maintains required minimum unrestricted cash and cash equivalents

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Extension of compliance deadlines may indicate operational or timing challenges, though no defaults are occurring
• Aircraft financing exposure to aviation industry cyclicality and regulatory environment
• Limited financial covenant visibility due to redacted terms in the amendment document
