# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America

**Transaction:** Consent to modify an existing credit agreement between Oil-Dri Corporation of America and BMO Harris Bank N.A. The modification amends the definitions of Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR in the original credit agreement dated January 27, 2006.

**Purpose:** The modification allows for a one-time add-back of a $5,644,000 non-cash goodwill impairment charge related to the Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment, effective for the fiscal quarter ending April 30, 2022 and thereafter.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America operates through multiple domestic subsidiaries including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company. All subsidiaries serve as guarantors under the credit facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established 16+ year banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank demonstrates institutional confidence and relationship stability
• Proactive covenant management approach through formal modification process indicates strong financial oversight
• All representations and warranties remain true and correct with no defaults or events of default
• Comprehensive guarantee structure across seven subsidiary entities provides broad credit support
• Company maintains formal compliance certification process with detailed financial covenant tracking

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• Material goodwill impairment of $5.644 million suggests operational challenges in Retail and Wholesale segment, though isolated to specific business unit
• Need for covenant relief indicates potential pressure on financial maintenance ratios, mitigated by one-time nature of adjustment
• Limited visibility into underlying financial performance and business fundamentals beyond covenant compliance framework
