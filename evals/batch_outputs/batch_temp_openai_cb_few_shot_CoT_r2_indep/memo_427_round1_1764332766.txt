# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

**Date of Agreement:** 2022-06-06

**Borrower/Company Overview:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America serves as the borrower under a credit facility with BMO Harris Bank N.A., with multiple domestic subsidiaries acting as guarantors.

**Transaction Description:** This is a covenant modification consent to an existing credit agreement originally dated January 27, 2006. The transaction involves Oil-Dri Corporation of America as borrower and BMO Harris Bank N.A. as lender.

**Purpose of Financing:** The modification allows Oil-Dri to add back a one-time non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $5,644,000 related to their Retail and Wholesale Reporting Segment to both Consolidated EBITDA and Consolidated EBITR calculations, preventing potential covenant violations.

**Company Background:** Oil-Dri Corporation of America maintains a long-standing banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank dating back to 2006. The company operates through multiple subsidiaries including Oil-Dri Corporation of Georgia, Oil-Dri Production Company, Mounds Production Company LLC, Blue Mountain Production Company, Amlan International, and Taft Production Company, which provide guarantee support for the credit facility.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

**Key Strengths / Credit Positives:**
• Established 16-year banking relationship with BMO Harris Bank demonstrates lender confidence and borrower stability
• Proactive covenant management approach shows responsible financial stewardship
• No existing defaults or events of default under the credit agreement as of consent date
• Comprehensive guarantee structure with multiple operating subsidiaries providing additional credit support
• One-time nature of the goodwill impairment suggests isolated rather than systemic issues

**Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:**
• $5.644 million goodwill impairment in Retail and Wholesale segment indicates potential operational underperformance
• Need for covenant modification suggests company was approaching financial maintenance covenant limits
• Limited financial transparency as detailed credit metrics and overall leverage profile not disclosed in consent document
