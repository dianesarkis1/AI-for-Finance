# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- On June 18, 2025, OFSCC-FS, LLC executed a third amendment to its revolving credit and security agreement with BNP Paribas and other lenders.
- OFSCC-FS, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, serves as the borrower in this revolving credit facility. The company is supported by OFS Capital Corporation as servicer and OFSCC-FS Holdings, LLC as equityholder.
- The original revolving credit and security agreement was dated June 20, 2019, with BNP Paribas acting as administrative agent and lender, alongside Mitsubishi HC Capital America, Inc. as an additional lender. Citibank, N.A. serves as collateral agent, and Virtus Group, LP acts as collateral administrator.
- This amendment extends the Final Maturity Date to June 20, 2027, and modifies the Reinvestment Period to terminate on August 31, 2025, providing the borrower with additional time for both the facility maturity and reinvestment activities.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks

- Borrower representations confirm no defaults or events of default are occurring, indicating current compliance with all facility terms
- Well-structured governance with separate servicer and equityholder entities providing operational oversight

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
- Limited financial information available to assess underlying credit quality and performance metrics
- Extension of maturity date suggests potential need for additional time to manage obligations, though this could also indicate proactive refinancing planning
- No visibility into pricing, facility size, or financial covenants limiting comprehensive credit assessment
