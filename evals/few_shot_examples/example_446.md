# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In November 2022, Tredegar Corporation and its lenders (JP Morgan, Citizens Bank, PNC Bank, and more) amended their original credit agreement, dated June 2022. 
- The amendment modifies the definition of "Consolidated EBITDA", specifying that operations will only be classified as discontinued when actually disposed of, rather than when subject to an agreement for disposal.

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks
### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:

Strong banking consortium comprising major U.S. financial institutions (JPMorgan Chase, Bank of America, Wells Fargo, Citibank, PNC, and others), suggesting lender confidence in Tredegar Corporation.

Reaffirmation that the borrower is currently in compliance with all covenants.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
This amendment makes the agreement generally more borrower-friendly, as it delays recognition of EBITDA reduction. 

Limited information on pricing, maturity, or other financial covenants limit visibility into broader credit profile of the transaction.
