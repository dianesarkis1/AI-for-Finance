# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In August 2022, BMO Harris and Willdan Group entered into a sixth amendment to their credit agreement.
- Willdan Group, an engineering and energy services firm, serves as the Borrower. The company is accompanied by multiple Guarantors, including subsidiaries such as Willdan Energy Solutions, Willdan Engineering, Willdan Financial Services, and others
- The original Credit Agreement was dated June 26, 2019, among Willdan, its Guarantors, and a syndicate of Lenders led by BMO Harris Bank N.A. as Administrative Agent. Other participating Lenders include Bank of America, N.A., U.S. Bank National Association, and MUFG Union Bank, N.A.
- The purpose of this Amendment is to modify Section 8.7(b) of the Credit Agreement to increase or reaffirm limits on purchase money indebtedness and Capitalized Lease Obligations of the Loan Parties and their Subsidiaries. The amended limit is set to not exceed USD 4,000,000 in the aggregate at any one time outstanding

## 2. Investment Highlights & Risks
### Key Strengths / Credit Positives:
The Borrower maintains a long-standing, multi-lender credit relationship with leading financial institutions (BMO Harris Bank, Bank of America, U.S. Bank, MUFG Union Bank), indicating strong lender confidence.

The amendment primarily revises a covenant rather than restructures core credit terms, implying continued credit stability and compliance.

The reaffirmation of collateral documents ensures that existing liens and guarantees remain fully enforceable.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:

Limited scope of information on overall financial performance or liquidity; risk assessment is constrained to covenant-related matters.
