# Investment Memorandum

## 1. Executive Summary

- In May 2022, Morgan Stanley's Direct Lending Fund executed a formal commitment reduction letter (to $280mm) for its credit facility with CIBC Bank.
- CIBC, a Chicago-based bank, and Morgan Stanley's Direct Lending Fund, had first entered into a credit agreement in December 2019, that was amended in February and then November 2020.

Proactive Capital Management: The permanent reduction indicates disciplined utilization of credit lines and potentially reduced leverage exposure.

### Principal Risks and Mitigating Factors:
